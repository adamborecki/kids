# 🐦 Tube Bird Feeder

Roll a tube in seeds 🌻 hang it in a tree 🌳 and watch the birds come! 🐦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 cardboard tube
- [ ] 🥜 Sunflower seed butter or peanut butter (check allergies!)
- [ ] 🌻 Birdseed on a plate
- [ ] 🧈 Butter knife or spoon
- [ ] 🧶 String

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Spread it ✋
Spread a thin layer of seed butter all over the tube.

### 2️⃣ Roll in seeds 🌻
Roll it on the birdseed plate until it's covered.

### 3️⃣ Thread the string 🧶
Push string through the tube. Tie the ends.

### 4️⃣ Hang it 🌳
On a branch you can see from a window.

### 5️⃣ Watch 👀
Birds may take a few days to find it. Be patient!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make it and hang it with a grown-up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Bird count** 🔢 Look out the window every morning for a week. Count visitors.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Who visits?** 🐦 Draw the birds. Find their names.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Best time** ⏰ Check 3 times a day (morning, lunch, evening). When are the most birds?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Seed test** 🧪 Make 2 feeders with different seeds. Which empties first?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Bird journal** 📓 Track species, counts, and weather for 2 weeks. Any patterns?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌻 Birds need lots of energy, especially in cold weather. Seeds are like birdie snacks.
- 🐦 Birds remember good food spots and come back.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥜 Peanut allergy? Use sunflower seed butter.
- 🐿️ Squirrels will try to steal it — that's part of the show.
- 🧴 Hang it high enough that cats can't jump to it.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🍽️ Wash the plate and knife
- [ ] 🧹 Sweep up spilled seed
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash sticky hands
