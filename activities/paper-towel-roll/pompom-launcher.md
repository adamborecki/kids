# 🎯 Pom-Pom Launcher

Pull back… let go… **FWOOP!** A pom-pom flies! 🎯

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 toilet paper roll
- [ ] 🎈 1 balloon
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up cuts the balloon)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms
- [ ] 🥤 Cups (targets)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Prep the balloon 🎈
Grown-up ties a knot and cuts off the top of the balloon (the round part).

### 2️⃣ Stretch it on 🔗
Stretch the knotted part over one end of the tube. Tape it on.

### 3️⃣ Load 🔴
Drop a pom-pom in the open end.

### 4️⃣ Aim + launch 🎯
Pull the knot back… let go!

### 5️⃣ Targets 🥤
Knock down a cup tower!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Knock down a 6-cup pyramid.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Distance** 📏 How far does it fly? Measure 3 launches.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pull test** 🔬 Pull back 2 cm vs. 4 cm vs. 6 cm. Does it go farther?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Angle lab** 📐 Launch at different angles. Which goes farthest? Graph it.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎈 Stretching the balloon stores **energy**.
- 🚀 Letting go turns that stored energy into motion.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎈 Balloons are a serious choking hazard — keep pieces away from little kids.
- 🚫 Rule: **never aim at people or pets.** Targets only.
- 🔴 Pom-poms only — no hard objects.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🎈 Balloon scraps in the trash right away
- [ ] 🔴 Collect pom-poms
