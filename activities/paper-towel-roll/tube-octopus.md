# 🐙 Tube Octopus

Snip, snip, snip… **8 wiggly legs!** 🐙

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 cardboard tube
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 👀 Googly eyes or paper eyes
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint the tube 🎨
Any color! Let it dry.

### 2️⃣ Cut the legs ✂️
Cut 8 strips from the bottom, halfway up.

### 3️⃣ Curl them 🌀
Roll each leg around a pencil to curl it.

### 4️⃣ Add a face 👀
Eyes and a big smile.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make 1 octopus. Count the legs out loud: 1–8!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make an **ocean family** 🐟 Try a jellyfish (lots of thin legs) or a crab (6 legs + 2 claws).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Octopus facts** 🔍 Octopuses have 3 hearts and blue blood. Find 2 more facts and write them on the printable!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐙 "Octo" means **eight**. Octagon = 8 sides. October was once month 8!
- 🧠 Real octopuses are super smart — they can open jars!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Pre-mark leg lines for 6-year-olds.
