# 🎆 Firework Painting

Snip a tube into a **fringe**, dip it, stamp it — **BOOM!** Fireworks! 🎆

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 2–3 tubes
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Bright paint on plates
- [ ] ⚫ Black or dark blue paper
- [ ] 👕 Smock

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fringe it ✂️
Cut slits all around one end, about 4 cm long.

### 2️⃣ Splay it 🌟
Push the strips outward like a flower.

### 3️⃣ Dip + stamp 🎨
Dip in paint. Press on the dark paper. Twist a little!

### 4️⃣ Layer colors 🎆
Stamp over bursts in a new color.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make 5 fireworks in different colors.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add glitter while the paint is wet ✨

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**City skyline** 🏙️ Paint buildings at the bottom. Fireworks above!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Celebration card** 💌 Make a firework card for a birthday or holiday.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎆 Real fireworks burst in a circle because the sparks fly out evenly in all directions.
- 🎨 Bright colors pop most on dark paper (contrast!).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Washable paint. Newspaper under everything.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🗑️ Toss painty tubes
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
