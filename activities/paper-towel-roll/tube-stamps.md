# 🖼️ Tube Stamp Art

Dip, stamp, **WOW** — circles, hearts, and flowers! 🌸

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 2–3 cardboard tubes
- [ ] 🎨 Washable paint on paper plates
- [ ] 📄 Big paper
- [ ] 👕 Smock or old shirt

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Circles ⭕
Dip the tube end in paint. Stamp!

### 2️⃣ Hearts ❤️
Squeeze the tube flat, then push one side in. Heart shape! Dip and stamp.

### 3️⃣ Flowers 🌸
Stamp 5 hearts in a circle (points in the middle). Add a stem.

### 4️⃣ Fill the page 🎨
Bubbles, caterpillars, flowers — go!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make circles in 3 colors.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Pattern** 🔁 Red-blue-red-blue in a row.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Picture** 🖼️ Make a whole garden or a bubble aquarium 🐠

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⭕ A tube end is a perfect circle. Squish it → new shapes!
- 🎨 Stamping = printmaking, one of the oldest art forms.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Washable paint + smock. Put newspaper down.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🗑️ Toss painty tubes
- [ ] 🍽️ Paper plates in the trash
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table
