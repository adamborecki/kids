# ✏️ Tube Desk Organizer

Pens, pencils, scissors — **everything gets a home.** ✏️🏠

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 4–6 tubes (cut to different heights)
- [ ] 📦 A piece of cardboard for the base
- [ ] 🖊️ Strong glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paint, washi tape, or paper to cover
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut different heights ✂️
Tall for pencils, short for erasers.

### 2️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Wrap in paper or paint them. Let dry.

### 3️⃣ Arrange 🧩
Stand them on the base. Try a few layouts.

### 4️⃣ Glue them down 🖊️
Glue each tube to the base AND to the tube next to it.

### 5️⃣ Fill it ✏️
Sort your supplies!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
3 tubes, one color each.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Label each tube: PENS · PENCILS · MARKERS 🏷️

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Design first** 📐 Sketch the layout from above on the printable.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Gift version** 🎁 Make one for a teacher or grown-up's desk.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Measure it** 📏 Measure your tallest pencil. Cut tubes so each tool sticks out exactly 3 cm.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏠 When things have a "home," they don't get lost.
- 📐 Planning from above = how designers plan furniture.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖊️ Use tacky glue and wait for it to dry before filling.
