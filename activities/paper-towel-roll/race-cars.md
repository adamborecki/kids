# 🏎️ Tube Race Cars

Build a car 🏎️ … then race it down a ramp! 🏁

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 Tubes (1 per car)
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps (4 per car)
- [ ] 📌 Brass paper fasteners (brads) or strong glue
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 📐 A ramp (a board or big book on a pile of books)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut a seat ✂️
Cut a rectangle hole on top of the tube for the driver.

### 2️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Racing stripes! Numbers! Flames!

### 3️⃣ Attach wheels ⚪
Poke holes in the caps and tube. Push brads through, loosely so wheels spin.

### 4️⃣ Driver 🧑‍🚀
Draw a driver on paper. Stick it in the seat.

### 5️⃣ RACE! 🏁
Let go at the top of the ramp. No pushing!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build 1 car. Race a friend's car.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Distance** 📏 Measure how far each car rolls past the ramp.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Ramp angle** 📐 Raise the ramp 1 book at a time. Does the car go farther?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Weight test** ⚖️ Tape coins inside. Does a heavier car go farther?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Fair test** 🔬 Run each test 3 times and average. Change only ONE thing at a time. Graph the results.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⬇️ A higher ramp gives the car more **energy** at the bottom.
- 🛞 Wheels that spin freely = less friction = farther.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📌 Brads are sharp-ish — grown-ups poke the holes for 7-year-olds.
- 🛞 Wobbly wheels? Make the holes exactly in the middle of the caps.
