# 🎵 Tube Pitch Lab

Long tubes sound **low**. Short tubes sound **high**. Build a tube band! 🎵

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 Several tubes (paper towel + toilet paper rolls)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 📏 Ruler

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut different lengths ✂️
Make tubes 30, 25, 20, 15, and 10 cm long (tape tubes together for long ones).

### 2️⃣ Bop! 🎵
Hold one end. Bop the other end on your palm.

### 3️⃣ Listen 👂
Which is lowest? Which is highest?

### 4️⃣ Line them up 📏
Order them from low to high.

### 5️⃣ Play a song 🎶
Bop them in order. Can you play a tune?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Order 5 tubes from low to high by ear.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tube band** 👫 Each person gets one tube. Conductor points to play!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Measure** 📏 Record each length. What's the pattern?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Tune it** 🎼 Use a free tuner app. Cut a tube until it plays a real note. Half the length = about one octave higher. Test it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎵 Bopping makes the air inside the tube vibrate.
- 📏 Longer air column = slower vibrations = **lower** sound.
- 🎺 Pipe organs and flutes work the same way.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📢 Bopping is loud and fun. Great outdoor band!
