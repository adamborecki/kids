# 🐛 Counting Caterpillar

Slice tubes into rings 🍩 paint them 🎨 and build a **long counting caterpillar!** 🐛

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 2–3 tubes
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up starts the cuts)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🧶 String or a pipe cleaner
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker for numbers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut rings ✂️
Slice tubes into rings about 2 cm wide.

### 2️⃣ Paint 🎨
Alternate 2 colors. Let dry.

### 3️⃣ Number them 🔢
Write 1, 2, 3… on each ring.

### 4️⃣ String them 🧶
Thread them in order. Add a head with a face and antennae!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Number 1–10. Count forwards and backwards.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Skip count** 🦘 Number by 2s (2, 4, 6…) or 5s (5, 10, 15…).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Pattern code** 🔁 Color pattern ABC or AAB. Can someone guess the rule?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔢 Seeing numbers in order helps you understand counting.
- 🔁 Patterns are the start of algebra (really!).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Tubes squish when cut — a grown-up can start each cut.
