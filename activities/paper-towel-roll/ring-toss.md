# 🎯 Tube Ring Toss

Toss paper-plate rings onto tube posts. **Ringer!** 🎯

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 3–5 cardboard tubes
- [ ] 🍽️ 3–5 paper plates
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the posts 🧻
Tape each tube standing up on a plate base (or a piece of cardboard).

### 2️⃣ Make the rings 🍽️
Cut out the middle of paper plates. Now they're rings!

### 3️⃣ Points 🔢
Write points on each post: 1, 2, 3, 5.

### 4️⃣ Toss! 🎯
Stand behind a line. 3 rings per turn.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Stand close. Count your ringers.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add the points. Who has the most?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Farther = double** 📏 Step back one step for double points.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tournament** 🏆 5 rounds. Keep score on the printable. Make the far posts worth more.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🥏 Flat rings fly better when you **spin** them, like a frisbee.
- 🎯 Practice makes your arm learn the right strength.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍽️ Sturdy plates fly better. Two plates glued together = heavier ring.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🍽️ Stack rings and posts in a bag
