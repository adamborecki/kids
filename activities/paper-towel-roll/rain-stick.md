# 🌧️ Rain Stick

Tip it slowly… and hear the **rain** fall! 🌧️ Shhhhhhh…

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 paper towel roll
- [ ] 🥈 Aluminum foil
- [ ] 🍚 A handful of rice or dried beans
- [ ] 📄 Paper + 🔗 rubber bands (to close the ends)
- [ ] 🎨 Markers, washi tape, or paint

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Close one end 📄
Paper circle over the end + rubber band.

### 2️⃣ Make a foil spiral 🌀
Scrunch 2 long strips of foil into snakes. Twist them into springs. Push inside.

### 3️⃣ Add rice 🍚
About 2 spoonfuls.

### 4️⃣ Close the other end 📄
Paper + rubber band. Tape it extra tight!

### 5️⃣ Decorate + tip 🎨
Tip it slowly. Listen!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make it. Tip it slowly. Is it a drizzle or a storm?

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Decorate with a pattern 🔁

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sound test** 🔬 Rice vs. beans vs. beads. Which sounds most like rain?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Slow motion** ⏱️ How long can you make the "rain" last in one tip?

### 🔟 Age 10
**History** 🌎 Rain sticks are traditional instruments in parts of South America, made from dried cactus. Find out more and draw one.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Make it better** 🔧 More foil? Longer tube? Mix fillings? Test 3 changes and rank the sounds.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🍚 Rice bumps the foil on the way down, making lots of tiny taps.
- 🌧️ Lots of tiny taps sound like raindrops.
- 🌀 More foil = more bumps = longer, softer rain.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍚 Tape the ends really well. Spilled rice gets everywhere.
- 🫘 Small beans are a choking hazard for toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧹 Sweep up any spilled rice
- [ ] ✂️ Scraps in recycling
