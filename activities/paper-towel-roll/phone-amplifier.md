# 🔊 Phone Speaker Amplifier

Make your phone **louder** with no batteries! 🔊 Just a tube and two cups.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 paper towel roll
- [ ] 🥤 2 paper or plastic cups
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil
- [ ] 🔪 Craft knife (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 📱 A phone playing music

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Trace the phone's bottom 📱
On the middle of the tube, trace the shape of the phone's bottom edge.

### 2️⃣ Grown-up cuts the slot 🔪
The phone should slide in snugly.

### 3️⃣ Trace the tube on each cup ✏️
On the side of each cup, near the bottom.

### 4️⃣ Grown-up cuts the cup holes 🔪
Push the tube ends into the cups.

### 5️⃣ Play music 🎶
Slide the phone in (speaker inside). Louder!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Build it with a grown-up. Compare: phone alone vs. in the amp.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Measure it** 📏 Use a free sound meter app. How many decibels louder?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Improve it** 🔧 Try bigger cups, a longer tube, or no cups. Which design is loudest? Explain why.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔊 The tube **directs** sound that would spread out in all directions.
- 🥤 Cups act like horns — they push the sound out toward you.
- 📯 Old record players used big horns the same way!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Craft knife = grown-up job. Kids trace and design.
- 📱 Keep volume moderate — protect ears.
