# 📞 Tube Telephone

Whisper through a tube chain… did the message make it? 📞🤫

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 4+ tubes
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 👫 2+ players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make a long tube 🟨
Tape tubes end to end.

### 2️⃣ Whisper 🤫
One person whispers a message into one end.

### 3️⃣ Listen 👂
The other person puts their ear at the other end.

### 4️⃣ Repeat it back 🔁
Did you hear it right?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Whisper animal names. Guess the animal! 🐶

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Telephone game** 📞 Pass a sentence through 3 people. Does it change?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Corner call** 🔄 Bend the tube chain around a corner. Can you still hear?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔊 Sound travels through the air inside the tube instead of spreading out.
- 👂 That's why the whisper stays loud enough to hear.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👂 Whisper only — no shouting into ears!

## 🧽 Cleanup
🛍️ Save the tubes
