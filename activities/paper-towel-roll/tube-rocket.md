# 🚀 Tube Rocket

3… 2… 1… **BLAST OFF!** 🚀 Build a rocket from a tube.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 cardboard tube
- [ ] 📄 Paper or cardstock (for the nose cone and fins)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors + 🟨 tape or glue
- [ ] 🎨 Markers, paint, or foil
- [ ] 🔥 Red/orange tissue paper (for flames)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint or wrap the tube 🎨
Foil = shiny space rocket!

### 2️⃣ Make the nose cone 🔺
Cut a circle of paper. Cut a slit to the middle. Overlap into a cone. Tape on top.

### 3️⃣ Add fins ✂️
Cut 3 triangles. Tape them at the bottom.

### 4️⃣ Flames! 🔥
Stuff tissue paper in the bottom.

### 5️⃣ Name your rocket 🏷️
Write it on the side.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Decorate it and give it a name. Zoom it around the room! 🚀

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add windows with astronauts looking out 👩‍🚀

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Mission patch** 🛡️ Design a patch for your crew on the printable.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Real rockets** 🔍 Look up a real rocket. Copy its colors and name. Why do rockets have fins? (Hint: they keep it flying straight.)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔺 The pointy nose helps a real rocket slice through air.
- 🪶 Fins keep it stable — like feathers on an arrow.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Cone-cutting is tricky. Help 6-year-olds with the slit.

## 🧽 Cleanup
