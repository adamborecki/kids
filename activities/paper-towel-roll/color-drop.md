# 🎨 Pom-Pom Color Drop

Drop each pom-pom down the tube with the **matching color!** 🔴🔵🟡

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 4 tubes
- [ ] 🎨 Markers or colored paper (4 colors)
- [ ] 🟦 Painter's tape
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms (or crumpled paper balls) in 4 colors
- [ ] 🥣 Bowls to catch them

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Color the tubes 🎨
One color each.

### 2️⃣ Tape to the wall 🟦
At different angles, with a bowl under each.

### 3️⃣ Sort + drop 🔴
Pick a pom-pom. Find its tube. Drop it in!

### 4️⃣ Count 🔢
How many in each bowl?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Sort 20 pom-poms by color. Count each bowl.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Speed sort** ⏱️ How fast can you sort them all? Beat your time!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Sorting by color is an early math skill (grouping).
- ✋ Pinching pom-poms builds finger strength for writing.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Pom-poms are a choking hazard for babies and toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms back in a bag
- [ ] 🟦 Peel tape
