# 🗼 Tube Tower Challenge

How **tall** can you build with tubes and paper plates? 🗼📏

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 10–20 tubes
- [ ] 🍽️ Paper plates or cardboard squares
- [ ] 📏 Tape measure
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Rules 📋
Tubes stand up. Plates go on top as floors. No tape!

### 2️⃣ Build ⏱️
10 minutes. Go!

### 3️⃣ Measure 📏
Measure from the floor to the very top.

### 4️⃣ Test it 💨
Does it survive a gentle fan or a stomp nearby?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build as tall as you can. Measure it.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Team build** 👫 Teams race for the tallest tower.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Strength test** 📚 Can your tower hold a book on top?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Design challenge** 🏗️ Tallest tower that holds a book. Sketch your plan first.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Efficiency** 🧮 Tallest tower per tube: height ÷ number of tubes. Who built smartest?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏗️ Wide bases make towers stable.
- 🧱 Tubes standing up are surprisingly strong — they spread weight around the circle.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🛋️ Build away from walkways so towers don't get knocked by accident.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🛍️ Tubes and plates back in the bag
