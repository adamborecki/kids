# 🎳 Tube Bowling

10 tubes = 10 pins. Roll a ball. **STRIKE!** 🎳

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 10 cardboard tubes
- [ ] ⚽ A small ball
- [ ] 🟨 Tape (for the throw line)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers (optional — number the pins)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Number the pins 🔢
Write 1–10 on the tubes.

### 2️⃣ Set up a triangle 🔺
4 in the back, then 3, then 2, then 1 in front.

### 3️⃣ Make a throw line 🟨
Tape line 3 big steps away.

### 4️⃣ Roll! 🎳
Two rolls per turn. Count how many fall.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Count the pins that fall. Set them back up!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Add the numbers** ➕ on the pins you knocked down.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Keep score** 📋 for 5 rounds on the printable. Who wins?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎯 Rolling straight takes aim + gentle control.
- 🔢 Adding pin numbers = sneaky math practice.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🏠 Use a soft ball inside. Hallways make great lanes!

## 🧽 Cleanup
Stack the tubes in a bag for next time 🛍️
