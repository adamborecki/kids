# 🎁 Tiny Gift Box

Fold the ends in… and a tube becomes a **tiny gift box!** 🎁

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 toilet paper roll
- [ ] 🎨 Wrapping paper, washi tape, or markers
- [ ] 🎀 Ribbon or string
- [ ] 🍬 A small gift or note

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fold one end 🌙
Push one side of the end in, then the other side, so they curve over each other. Closed!

### 2️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Wrap in paper or decorate.

### 3️⃣ Fill it 🍬
A note, a sticker, a small treat.

### 4️⃣ Fold the other end 🌙
Same trick. Tie with ribbon.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make one for a friend.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Write a riddle inside. They solve it to find out who it's from 🤔

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Party favors** 🎉 Make one for every guest at a party.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Coupon box** 🎟️ Fill with coupons: "1 free hug," "I'll do your chore."

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Mini time capsule** ⏳ Fill with notes for future you. Open next birthday.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📐 Folding curved cardboard = engineering with no tape!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍬 Check allergies before adding treats.
