# 🎭 Tube People Puppet Show

Turn tubes into **characters** 🎭 … then put on a show! 🎬

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 4–6 tubes
- [ ] 🎨 Markers, paint, yarn, fabric scraps
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue
- [ ] 🪵 Popsicle sticks (optional, for handles)
- [ ] 📦 A box or table for a stage

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Plan characters 💡
A hero, a silly friend, a villain, a pet…

### 2️⃣ Make faces 🎨
Draw or paint faces. Yarn hair! Paper hats!

### 3️⃣ Add handles 🪵
Glue a stick inside, or just hold the tube from below.

### 4️⃣ Build a stage 📦
Kneel behind a table with a blanket over it.

### 5️⃣ SHOW TIME! 🎬
Use different voices!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make your family as tube people 👨‍👩‍👧

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make 3 characters and a short show.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Script** 📝 Write who says what on the printable.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Sound effects** 🔊 Add music and sounds (pots, kazoos).

### 🔟 Age 10
**Tickets + audience** 🎟️ Invite family. Make programs listing the cast.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎭 Puppets make it easier to be brave and silly on "stage."
- 📖 Plays have a **beginning, middle, and end**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Yarn and glue get messy — cover the table.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🎨 Craft supplies back in the box
- [ ] ✂️ Scraps in recycling
