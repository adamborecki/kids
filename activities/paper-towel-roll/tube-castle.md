# 🏰 Tube Castle

Tubes = towers. Add a box = **a whole castle!** 🏰👑

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 4+ tubes (towers)
- [ ] 📦 A shoebox or cereal box (the castle walls)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors + 🟨 tape
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🚩 Toothpicks + paper (flags)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Tower tops ✂️
Cut little square notches around the top of each tube (battlements!).

### 2️⃣ Attach towers 🟨
Tape a tower to each corner of the box.

### 3️⃣ Door 🚪
Draw (or cut, with help) a drawbridge door.

### 4️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Bricks, windows, vines.

### 5️⃣ Flags 🚩
Toothpick + paper triangle on each tower.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
4 towers + a door. Name your castle!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add windows and a flag for each tower.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Drawbridge** 🌉 Make a door that opens and closes with string.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Castle map** 🗺️ Add a moat, a village, and a forest around it.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Real castles** 🔍 Look up one real castle. Copy one feature (round towers? moat?) and explain why it helped defend the castle.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏰 Notched tops (battlements) let defenders hide and peek out.
- 🌊 Moats made castles hard to reach.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Notches are easy with scissors; the door may need grown-up help on thick boxes.
