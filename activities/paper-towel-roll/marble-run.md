# 🎢 Marble Run Wall

Tape tubes to a wall or door… drop a marble… **whoosh!** 🎢

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 6–10 tubes (some cut in half the long way)
- [ ] 🟦 Painter's tape (gentle on walls)
- [ ] 🔮 Marbles or small balls
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🥤 A cup at the bottom to catch the marble

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut some chutes ✂️
Cut a few tubes in half the long way. Now they're open slides.

### 2️⃣ Start at the top ⬆️
Tape the first tube high on the door, slanted down.

### 3️⃣ Zig-zag down ↘️↙️
Each tube catches the marble from the one above.

### 4️⃣ Test after every tube 🔮
Drop a marble. Fix any spot where it falls out.

### 5️⃣ Catch cup 🥤
Tape a cup at the end.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Build 3 tubes with a grown-up. Drop and cheer!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build 5 tubes by yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Longest run** ⏱️ Time the marble. Can you make it take longer?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Two paths** 🔀 Make a split where the marble can go 2 ways.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Speed test** 🏎️ Steep vs. gentle slopes. Which is faster? Measure with a timer.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Exactly 5 seconds** 🎯 Design a run where the marble takes as close to 5.0 seconds as possible. Adjust angles to hit it.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⬇️ Gravity pulls the marble down. Slopes turn "down" into "sideways."
- 🏎️ Steeper slope = faster. Gentle slope = slower.
- 🧠 Testing and fixing over and over = how **engineers** work.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔮 Marbles are a choking hazard for little siblings. Count them before and after.
- 🟦 Painter's tape only — regular tape can pull paint off.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🟦 Peel tape slowly
- [ ] 🔮 Count the marbles back into the bag
