# 🔭 Explorer Binoculars

Two tubes + tape = **binoculars!** 🔭 Now go spot some birds. 🐦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 2 toilet paper rolls (or 1 paper towel roll cut in half)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers or stickers
- [ ] 🧶 String or yarn (for the neck strap)
- [ ] ✂️ Hole punch or pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Tape them together 🟨
Side by side. Wrap tape around the middle.

### 2️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Colors, stripes, your name!

### 3️⃣ Add a strap 🧶
Poke a hole on each outside edge. Tie on a string long enough to go over your head.

### 4️⃣ Go exploring 🌳
Look through them. What can you spot?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Spot 5 things: a bird, a tree, a car, a cloud, a dog 🐶

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**I Spy** 👀 "I spy something red…" with your binoculars.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Bird tally** 🐦 Count every bird you see in 10 minutes.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Field notes** 📓 Draw 3 birds. Find their names in a bird book or app.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔭 Tubes block light from the sides, so you **focus** on one spot.
- 👀 Real binoculars use lenses to make things bigger. These help you look closely.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧶 Keep the strap loose and short enough not to tangle. Take it off for climbing.
- 😎 Never look at the sun.
