# ✨ Constellation Viewer

Poke star holes 📌 shine a flashlight 🔦 and put the **night sky on your ceiling!** ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 cardboard tube
- [ ] ⚫ Black paper (circles a bit bigger than the tube end)
- [ ] 📌 A pushpin
- [ ] 🔗 Rubber bands
- [ ] 🔦 A flashlight (phone flashlight works)
- [ ] 📋 The star maps (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Copy a constellation ✏️
Put the black paper over a star map. Mark each star with a pencil dot.

### 2️⃣ Poke the stars 📌
Poke a hole at each dot. Big stars = wiggle the pin for bigger holes.

### 3️⃣ Cap the tube 🔗
Rubber band the paper over one end.

### 4️⃣ Lights off 🌙
Shine the flashlight through the open end. Stars on the ceiling!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make the **Big Dipper** (7 stars).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Make 3 constellations. Can someone guess which is which?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Find it for real** 🌌 On a clear night, find the Big Dipper outside!

### 🔟 Age 10
**The story** 📖 Look up the myth behind Orion or Cassiopeia. Tell it while showing your projection.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Navigation** 🧭 The two end stars of the Big Dipper's cup point to **Polaris** (the North Star). Use it to find north outside, then check with a compass.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⭐ Constellations are star patterns people named long ago.
- 🔦 Light only passes through the holes, so the pattern shows up big on the ceiling.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📌 Pushpin pokes are a grown-up-supervised job. Put a folded towel under the paper.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📌 Pushpin back in its box
