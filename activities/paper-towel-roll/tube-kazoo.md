# 📯 Tube Kazoo

Hum into a tube… and it **BUZZES!** 📯🐝

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 1 cardboard tube
- [ ] 📄 Wax paper (a square bigger than the tube end)
- [ ] 🔗 A rubber band
- [ ] ✏️ A pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cover one end 📄
Put wax paper over one end. Hold it on with the rubber band.

### 2️⃣ Poke a tiny hole ✏️
Poke a small hole in the side of the tube, near the wax paper end.

### 3️⃣ Hum into the open end 🎵
Don't blow — **hum**! "Doo-doo-doo!"

### 4️⃣ Feel it buzz 🐝
Touch the wax paper while you hum. Tickly!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Hum "Happy Birthday" on your kazoo 🎂

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Kazoo band** 🎺 Make 2 more. Play a song together.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Loud vs soft** 🔊 Try with and without the side hole. Which is louder?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Pitch test** 🎵 Try a short tube and a long tube. Do they sound different?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎵 Humming makes the air shake (vibrate).
- 📄 The thin wax paper shakes too — that's the buzz!
- 🎺 Real kazoos work the exact same way.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📢 It's loud and buzzy. Maybe outside 😅

## 🧽 Cleanup
Nothing! Keep it for the next concert 🎶
