# 🌱 Tube Seed Starters

Little cardboard pots you can **plant straight into the ground!** 🌱

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧻 Toilet paper rolls (or paper towel rolls cut in thirds)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🪴 Potting soil
- [ ] 🌻 Seeds (beans, sunflowers, peas grow fast)
- [ ] 🍽️ A tray
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the bottom ✂️
Cut 4 slits (2 cm) at one end. Fold the flaps in like a box bottom.

### 2️⃣ Fill with soil 🪴
Pack it gently. Stand pots on the tray.

### 3️⃣ Plant 🌻
Poke a hole as deep as your fingertip. 2 seeds each. Cover.

### 4️⃣ Mist 💦
Keep the soil damp, not soggy.

### 5️⃣ Plant outside 🌳
When the sprout has a few leaves, plant the whole tube in the ground. It rots away!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Plant 3 pots with a grown-up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Label each pot with the plant name 🏷️

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sprout race** 🏁 Plant 3 kinds of seeds. Which sprouts first?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Measure** 📏 Measure the tallest sprout every 2 days.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Graph** 📈 Line graph of growth. When did it grow fastest?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Transplant test** 🔬 Plant one tube whole, and take one plant out of its tube. Which grows better after a week?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌱 Cardboard is made of plant fiber — it breaks down in soil.
- 🌳 Planting the whole pot means the roots don't get disturbed.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🌻 Big seeds (beans, sunflowers, peas) are easiest for kids.
- 🍄 Fuzzy mold on the cardboard? Too wet — let it dry a bit.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧹 Sweep soil
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
