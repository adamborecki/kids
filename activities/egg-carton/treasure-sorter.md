# 💎 Treasure Sorter

Buttons, rocks, LEGO… **sort them!** 💎 Every cup is a group.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 💎 A pile of small things to sort (buttons, beads, LEGO, shells, coins)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Dump the pile 💎
On the table.

### 2️⃣ Pick a rule 🤔
By color? Size? Shape?

### 3️⃣ Sort 🗂️
Each cup = one group.

### 4️⃣ Explain 🗣️
Tell someone your rule. Can they guess it?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Sort by **color**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Sort by **size** — small, medium, big.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Secret rule** 🕵️ Sort by a hidden rule (shiny vs. not, 2 holes vs. 4 holes). Can someone figure it out?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗂️ Sorting = grouping things by what they have in common. Scientists do this!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔘 Small items are choking hazards for toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
💎 Everything back in its jar
