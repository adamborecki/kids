# ⭕ Pom-Pom Tic-Tac-Toe

Use 9 cups as the board. Pom-poms are your pieces! ⭕❌

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (cut to 9 cups — 3 by 3)
- [ ] 🔴 5 pom-poms of one color
- [ ] 🔵 5 pom-poms of another color
- [ ] 👫 2 players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut a 3×3 square ✂️
Grown-up cuts 9 cups.

### 2️⃣ Pick colors 🔴🔵

### 3️⃣ Take turns ⏳
Drop one pom-pom in an empty cup.

### 4️⃣ Three in a row! 🏆
Across, down, or diagonal wins.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Play best of 3.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Block!** 🛑 Watch for 2 in a row — block it!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Never lose** 🧠 Find the best first move (hint: the middle or a corner). Can you always at least tie?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Tic-tac-toe is "solved": if both players play perfectly, it's always a tie!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Pom-poms: keep away from toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🔴 Pieces in a bag
