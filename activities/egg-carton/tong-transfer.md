# 🥢 Tong Transfer Race

No fingers allowed! Move pom-poms into cups using **tongs or clothespins.** 🥢⏱️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🔴 12 pom-poms in a bowl
- [ ] 🥢 Kitchen tongs, a clothespin, or chopsticks
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Bowl + carton 🥣
Side by side.

### 2️⃣ Grab with the tool 🥢
One pom-pom at a time.

### 3️⃣ Fill every cup 🥚
One per cup!

### 4️⃣ Time it ⏱️
Beat your best time.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Use big tongs. Fill all 12.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Use a **clothespin**. Harder!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Chopsticks** 🥢 The hardest tool. Can you do it in under 2 minutes?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ✋ Squeezing tools builds the same hand muscles you use to write.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Pom-poms: keep away from toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🔴 Pom-poms back in the bowl
