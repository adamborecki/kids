# 🍕 Egg Carton Fractions

12 cups make fractions **easy to see!** ½ ⅓ ¼ 🍕

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🔴🔵🟡🟢 Pom-poms or beads in 4 colors (12 of each)
- [ ] 📋 The worksheet

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ 12 = one whole 🥚
The full carton is 12/12.

### 2️⃣ Halves ½
Fill half the cups with red. How many is that? (6!)

### 3️⃣ Thirds ⅓
Empty it. Fill ⅓ with blue. How many? (4!)

### 4️⃣ Quarters ¼
Fill ¼ with yellow. How many? (3!)

### 5️⃣ Mix 🎨
½ red + ¼ blue + ¼ green. Does it fill the carton?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Show ½, ⅓, ¼ of the carton.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Equal fractions** 🟰 Show that 2/4 = 6/12 = ½.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Add fractions** ➕ ⅓ + ¼ = ? (Count the cups: 4 + 3 = 7, so 7/12!)

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Sixths + twelfths** 🧮 What is ⅚ of the carton? ⅔ − ¼? Show it with pom-poms.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🥚 12 splits evenly into halves, thirds, quarters, sixths, and twelfths.
- 🍕 That's why 12 is so handy — it's why there are 12 eggs in a dozen and 12 inches in a foot!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Beads and pom-poms: choking hazard for toddlers.
