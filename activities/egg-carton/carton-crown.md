# 👑 Egg Carton Crown

Every king and queen needs a **crown.** 👑 This one's made from egg cups!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A cardboard egg carton
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Gold or yellow paint
- [ ] 💎 Stickers, gems, foil
- [ ] 📄 A paper strip (headband)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut the cups ✂️
Cut a row of cups. Snip each cup into a point.

### 2️⃣ Paint gold 🎨
Let dry.

### 3️⃣ Jewels 💎
Stick on gems!

### 4️⃣ Headband 📄
Tape the crown to a paper strip. Fit it to your head.

### 5️⃣ Royal decree 📜
Make one royal rule for the day!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make the crown. Wear it at dinner! 👑

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Royal decree** 📜 Write one silly royal rule on the printable.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Coat of arms** 🛡️ Design a family shield with 4 parts: favorite animal, color, food, and hobby.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👑 Real crowns were symbols — they showed who was in charge.
- 🛡️ Coats of arms used pictures to show a family's story.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Grown-ups cut through the thick parts.
