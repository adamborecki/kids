# 🐞 Ladybug Spot Math

Make ladybugs from egg cups 🐞 Count (and add!) their **spots**.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (cut into single cups)
- [ ] 🎨 Red and black paint (or markers)
- [ ] ⚫ Black paper dots or a marker
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut single cups ✂️
Grown-up helps.

### 2️⃣ Paint red 🔴
Let dry.

### 3️⃣ Head + line ⚫
Paint the front black. Draw a line down the middle of the back.

### 4️⃣ Add spots ⚫
Different numbers on each side!

### 5️⃣ Math! ➕
Left spots + right spots = ?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make 3 ladybugs. Count the spots.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Spot sums** ➕ Write the math under each: 3 + 2 = 5.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Doubles** 🪞 Make "doubles" ladybugs: 4 + 4, 6 + 6. Then try near-doubles: 4 + 5.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐞 Ladybug spots are often the same on both sides — **symmetry**!
- ➕ Doubles (4 + 4) are a shortcut for harder math (4 + 5 = 4 + 4 + 1).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🐞 Fun fact: spot number does NOT tell a ladybug's age. It shows the species.
