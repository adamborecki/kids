# 🐢 Turtle Family

Single egg cups = perfect **turtle shells!** 🐢

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (single cups)
- [ ] 🎨 Green and brown paint or markers
- [ ] 📄 Green paper (head, legs, tail)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint the shell 🎨
Green with a pattern on top.

### 2️⃣ Cut body parts ✂️
1 head, 4 legs, 1 tail from green paper.

### 3️⃣ Glue 🖊️
Glue them under the shell, sticking out.

### 4️⃣ Make a family 🐢🐢🐢
Big turtle, small turtle!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make 2 turtles. Count all their legs.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Shell patterns** 🔁 Each turtle gets a different pattern: dots, stripes, hexagons ⬡.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐢 A turtle's shell is part of its skeleton — it can't take it off!
- ⬡ Many shells have shapes like hexagons.
