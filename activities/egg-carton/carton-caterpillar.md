# 🐛 Egg Carton Caterpillar

Cut a row of cups… paint it… **wiggle wiggle!** 🐛

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A cardboard egg carton
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up helps)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🌀 Pipe cleaners (antennae)
- [ ] 👀 Googly eyes or paper eyes

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut a row ✂️
Cut one long row of 6 cups.

### 2️⃣ Paint 🎨
Green body? Rainbow stripes? You choose!

### 3️⃣ Face 👀
Eyes and a smile on the first cup.

### 4️⃣ Antennae 🌀
Poke 2 pipe cleaners into the head.

### 5️⃣ Name it 🏷️

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make one caterpillar. Count its body parts!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Pattern body** 🔁 Paint the cups in a pattern: red-yellow-red-yellow.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Life cycle** 🦋 Read about how caterpillars become butterflies. Draw the 4 stages on the printable.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐛 Real caterpillars have body sections (segments) — like egg cups!
- 🦋 Egg → caterpillar → chrysalis → butterfly. That's **metamorphosis**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Egg cartons are thick at the joins. Help with cutting.
