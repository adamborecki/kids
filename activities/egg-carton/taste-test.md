# 👅 Blind Taste Test

Close your eyes 🙈 Taste. Is it **sweet, salty, sour, or bitter?** 👅

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A clean plastic egg carton (washed)
- [ ] 🍋 Tiny tastes: lemon (sour), pretzel (salty), apple (sweet), dark chocolate or grapefruit (bitter), cheese or tomato (umami)
- [ ] 🥄 Toothpicks or spoons
- [ ] 🙈 Blindfold
- [ ] 💧 Water to sip between tastes

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Grown-up fills cups 🥄
A tiny taste in each. Secret order!

### 2️⃣ Blindfold 🙈

### 3️⃣ Taste one at a time 👅
Sip water between each.

### 4️⃣ Guess 🤔
Sweet? Salty? Sour? Bitter? What food is it?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Guess the taste type for 4 foods.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Name the food** 🍎 Guess the exact food, not just the taste.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Nose plug** 👃 Hold your nose and taste again. Harder? Why?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Umami** 🍅 Learn the 5th taste (savory). Find 3 umami foods.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Design a test** 📋 Make a fair test: same amount each time, random order, record every guess.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👅 Your tongue senses **5 basic tastes**: sweet, salty, sour, bitter, and umami (savory).
- 👃 Most "flavor" actually comes from your **nose**! That's why food tastes bland with a cold.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥜 Check every allergy before choosing foods.
- 🧼 Wash plastic cartons with hot soapy water first, or use a cupcake tray.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥚 Wash the carton
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table
