# 🪇 Egg Cup Maracas

Two cups + rice = **shake shake shake!** 🪇🎶

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (cut 2 cups per maraca)
- [ ] 🍚 Rice or dried beans
- [ ] 🟨 Tape (lots!)
- [ ] 🥄 A plastic spoon (handle)
- [ ] 🎨 Markers or washi tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fill one cup 🍚
A small spoonful of rice.

### 2️⃣ Add the handle 🥄
Lay a spoon between the 2 cups.

### 3️⃣ Close it up 🟨
Put the 2 cups together, rim to rim. Tape all the way around — tight!

### 4️⃣ Decorate 🎨

### 5️⃣ SHAKE! 🪇

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Shake along to a song! 🎶

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Loud vs soft** 🔊 Rice vs. beans. Which is louder?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Rhythm copy** 👏 Someone shakes a pattern. Copy it exactly!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🪇 The grains bump the walls and each other — lots of tiny sounds.
- 🫘 Bigger grains = louder, chunkier sound.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍚 Tape extra tight — spills are a pain.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧹 Sweep any spilled rice

3. 🪇 shake · pause · shake · shake · shake
