# 🔢 Counting Cups

Number the cups 1–12. Fill each one with **that many** beans! 🔢🫘

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 🫘 Dry beans, pasta, or buttons (about 80)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Number the cups 🖊️
Write 1–12 in the bottom of each cup.

### 2️⃣ Fill them 🫘
Put 1 bean in cup 1, 2 beans in cup 2…

### 3️⃣ Check 👀
Dump a cup. Count it again. Right?

### 4️⃣ Mix-up game 🔀
A grown-up secretly adds or removes a bean. Find the wrong cup!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Numbers 1–6. Count out loud.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Numbers 1–12. **Skip count** the total: how many beans in all? (It's 78!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔢 Matching a numeral to real objects = **number sense**.
- 🤏 Picking up beans builds pinch strength.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🫘 Beans are a choking hazard for toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🫘 Beans back in the bag
