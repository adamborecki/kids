# 🎲 Shake-and-Land Probability

Put a pom-pom in, close the lid, **SHAKE!** 🎲 Where will it land?

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton with a lid
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 🔴 1 pom-pom (or a marble)
- [ ] 📋 The tally sheet

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Number the cups 🖊️
1–12.

### 2️⃣ Predict 🤔
Will some numbers come up more? Why?

### 3️⃣ Shake 30 times 🎲
Close the lid. Shake. Open. Tally the cup!

### 4️⃣ Look at the data 📊
Which cup won? Which never came up?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Shake 30 times. Tally the results.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Bar graph** 📊 Graph all 12 cups. Is it even?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Corners vs. middle** 🧐 Do corner cups come up more or less? Why might that be?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**More data** 📈 Combine everyone's tallies (100+ shakes). Does it get more even? That's the **law of large numbers**.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎲 If every cup is equally likely, each has a 1 in 12 chance.
- 📈 Small tests look random and lumpy. Big tests even out.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Marbles bounce harder and make more noise.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🔴 Pom-pom back in the bag
