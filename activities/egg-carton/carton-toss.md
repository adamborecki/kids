# 🎯 Egg Carton Toss

Bounce a pom-pom into the cups 🎯 **Big points** in the hard cups!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms, coins, or paper balls
- [ ] 🟨 Tape for a throw line

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Write points 🖊️
Different numbers in each cup: 1, 2, 5, 10…

### 2️⃣ Lid off ✂️
Cut the lid off (or tuck it under).

### 3️⃣ Toss 3 🎯
From behind the line.

### 4️⃣ Score ➕
Add up the cups you landed in.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Points 1–3. Add 2 tosses.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Points up to 10. Add 3 tosses.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Subtract race** ➖ Start at 100. Subtract each toss. First to 0 wins!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Multiply** ✖️ Points are 2–9. Multiply your two tosses.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Strategy** 🧠 Hard cups (corners) = ×2. Is aiming for them worth the risk?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎯 Short tosses need a soft, gentle arm.
- 🔢 Every round is quick mental math!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🪙 Coins bounce out more. Pom-poms are easiest.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms back in the bag
- [ ] 🟨 Peel the tape
