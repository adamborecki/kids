# 🪨 Rock Collection Display

Hunt for rocks 🪨 then display them like a **museum!** Each cup = one rock.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🪨 Interesting rocks (collect outside)
- [ ] 🔍 Magnifying glass
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (labels)
- [ ] 🪥 Old toothbrush + water (to clean rocks)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Rock hunt 🔍
Collect 12 different rocks. Look for different colors and textures.

### 2️⃣ Wash them 🪥
Scrub off the dirt. Let dry.

### 3️⃣ Examine 🔍
Sparkly? Striped? Smooth? Rough?

### 4️⃣ Display 🥚
One rock per cup. Label each: where you found it + what it looks like.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
12 rocks, sorted by color.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Describe** 🗣️ Write 2 words for each rock (sparkly, striped, smooth).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Scratch test** 🧪 Can a coin scratch it? Can it scratch glass? (Grown-up holds the glass.) Hardness helps identify rocks!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Name them** 🔍 Use a rock guide to name 3 (granite, quartz, sandstone…).

### 🚀 Age 11+
**3 rock types** 🌋 Sort into igneous (from lava), sedimentary (layers), metamorphic (squeezed + heated). Explain one.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌋 Rocks form in 3 ways: cooling lava, layers of sand/mud pressing together, or heat + pressure changing old rocks.
- 💎 Scientists test hardness with a scale called **Mohs** (1 = soft, 10 = diamond).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🪨 Only collect where it's allowed (not parks that forbid it).
- 🪟 Glass scratch tests: grown-up holds a thick glass tile, edges taped.
