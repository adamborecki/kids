# 🌸 Egg Carton Flowers

Cut cups into **petals** 🌸 paint them, and make a bouquet that never wilts!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A cardboard egg carton
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Paint
- [ ] 🌀 Pipe cleaners or sticks (stems)
- [ ] 🔘 Buttons or pom-poms (flower centers)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut single cups ✂️

### 2️⃣ Make petals ✂️
Cut 4–5 slits down each cup. Round the tips. Fold them out.

### 3️⃣ Paint 🎨
Bright colors! Let dry.

### 4️⃣ Centers 🔘
Glue a button in the middle.

### 5️⃣ Stems 🌀
Poke a pipe cleaner through the bottom. Twist to hold.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make 3 flowers.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Petal shapes** ✂️ Pointy (tulip), round (daisy), fringe (carnation).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Real flower match** 🔍 Copy a real flower: count its petals and match the color.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Bouquet gift** 💐 Make 6 flowers and a vase from a jar. Give it to someone!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Flower parts** 🌼 Label the petal, stamen, pistil on the printable. What does each one do?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌸 Many flowers have petals in 3s, 4s, or 5s.
- 🐝 Bright petals attract bees and butterflies to carry pollen.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Petal cutting is fiddly — great fine-motor work for 7+.
