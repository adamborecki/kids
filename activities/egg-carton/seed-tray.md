# 🌱 Egg Carton Seed Tray

12 cups = **12 tiny gardens.** 🌱 Watch seeds sprout in a week!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A cardboard egg carton
- [ ] 🪴 Potting soil
- [ ] 🌻 Seeds (beans, peas, sunflowers, marigolds)
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle
- [ ] 🍽️ A tray (cartons leak!)
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (labels)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Poke drain holes ✏️
A small hole in the bottom of each cup.

### 2️⃣ Fill with soil 🪴
Pack it gently.

### 3️⃣ Plant 🌻
2 seeds per cup, as deep as your fingertip.

### 4️⃣ Label 🏷️
Use the lid! Write each plant name.

### 5️⃣ Mist + sun ☀️
Keep damp. Sunny window. Wait!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Plant beans. Mist them every day.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Plant 3 kinds of seeds. Label them.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sprout race** 🏁 Which sprouts first? Record the day.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Measure** 📏 Measure every 2 days. Who's tallest?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Light test** ☀️🌑 Half in sun, half in a dark cupboard. What happens?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Transplant plan** 🗺️ Plan where each plant goes in the garden. How far apart do they need to be? (Check the seed packets.)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌱 Seeds carry their own food to get started. They need water + warmth to wake up.
- 🌑 Sprouts in the dark grow tall and pale, stretching for light.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍽️ Wet cartons get soggy — always use a tray.
- 🧼 Use clean cardboard cartons (no egg goo).

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧹 Sweep soil
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
