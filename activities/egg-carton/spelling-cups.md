# 🔤 Spelling Cups

Letters in cups 🔤 Pull them out… **spell a word!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 2 egg cartons
- [ ] 📄 Small paper squares
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make letter tiles 📄
Write letters on squares. Lots of vowels (A, E, I, O, U)!

### 2️⃣ Sort into cups 🥚
A–L in carton 1, M–Z in carton 2. Or sort vowels and consonants.

### 3️⃣ Spell 🔤
Grown-up says a word. Pull the letters and line them up!

### 4️⃣ Check ✅
Did you spell it right?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
3-letter words: CAT, DOG, SUN.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
4-letter words: FROG, JUMP, CAKE.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Word race** ⏱️ How many words can you make from 7 random letters in 3 minutes?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Word ladder** 🪜 Change one letter at a time: CAT → COT → DOT → DOG.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔤 Touching and moving letters helps your brain remember spelling.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📄 Make extra E, A, S, T — they're used most.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📄 Letters back in their cups
