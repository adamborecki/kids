# 🎨 Color Mixing Palette

12 cups = the **perfect paint palette.** 🎨 How many colors can you make from 3?

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (plastic or foam is best — it doesn't soak up paint)
- [ ] 🎨 Red, yellow, blue, white paint
- [ ] 🖌️ Brushes + water cup
- [ ] 📄 Paper
- [ ] 👕 Smock

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ 3 primaries 🔴🟡🔵
Put red, yellow, and blue in 3 cups.

### 2️⃣ Mix two 🔀
In an empty cup: red + yellow. What do you get?

### 3️⃣ Keep mixing 🎨
Fill every cup with a new color!

### 4️⃣ Paint swatches 🖌️
Paint a stripe of each one on paper. Label it.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make orange, green, and purple.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Light + dark** ⚪ Add white to make tints (pink, sky blue).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Color wheel** 🎡 Paint a 6-color wheel in the right order.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Brown mystery** 🟤 What 3 colors make brown? Test it!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Match a color** 🎯 Pick something in the room. Mix paint to match it exactly.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Recipe card** 📋 Write recipes: "2 dabs blue + 1 dab yellow = teal." Can someone else follow yours?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔴🟡🔵 Red, yellow, and blue are **primary** colors for paint.
- 🟠🟢🟣 Mixing two primaries makes **secondary** colors.
- ⚪ Adding white makes a **tint**. Adding black makes a **shade**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Washable paint. Cover the table. Expect splatter.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖌️ Rinse brushes until the water runs clear
- [ ] 🥚 Plastic cartons rinse clean — reuse them!
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
