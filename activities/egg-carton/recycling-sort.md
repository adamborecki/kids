# ♻️ Recycling Sort

What goes where? **Sort small samples** into labeled cups and become a recycling expert! ♻️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (labels)
- [ ] 🗑️ Small clean samples: a bottle cap, foil scrap, paper scrap, cardboard bit, plastic wrap, a can tab, a receipt…
- [ ] 📋 Your town's recycling rules (look them up)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Label cups 🟨
PAPER · CARDBOARD · PLASTIC · METAL · GLASS (picture) · TRASH · COMPOST

### 2️⃣ Sort 🗂️
Drop each sample in the right cup.

### 3️⃣ Check 🔍
Look up your town's rules. Any surprises?

### 4️⃣ Fix the house 🏠
Make a sign for your real recycling bin!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Sort paper, plastic, metal.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add **compost** 🍌 What food scraps can go in?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tricky items** 🤔 Greasy pizza box? Plastic bag? Receipts? Look them up!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Trash audit** 📊 Count what your family throws away in one day. What could be recycled instead?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Where it goes** 🌍 Research what happens to recycling after the truck takes it. Make a flowchart.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ♻️ Recycling turns old stuff into new stuff — saving trees, energy, and space in landfills.
- 🤔 Every town has different rules! Always check yours.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧼 Use clean, dry, safe samples only (no sharp can lids or broken glass).

♻️ Samples into the right real bins
