# 🫘 Egg Carton Mancala

One of the **oldest board games in the world** — and you can play it with an egg carton! 🫘

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A 12-cup egg carton (the board)
- [ ] 🥣 2 small bowls (the "stores")
- [ ] 🫘 48 beans, beads, or pebbles
- [ ] 👫 2 players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Set up 🫘
Carton between you, long side facing each. The 6 cups on your side are yours. Put **4 beans in every cup**. A bowl at each end — your store is on your **right**.

### 2️⃣ Your turn: scoop 🤲
Pick up all beans from one of YOUR cups.

### 3️⃣ Sow ➡️
Going **counter-clockwise**, drop 1 bean in each cup — including your own store (skip your opponent's store).

### 4️⃣ Bonus turn 🔁
Last bean lands in your store? Go again!

### 5️⃣ Capture 🏴‍☠️
Last bean lands in an **empty cup on your side**? Take it AND all the beans in the cup across from it. Into your store!

### 6️⃣ Game over 🏁
When one side is empty, the other player puts their leftover beans in their own store. Most beans wins!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Learn the rules with 3 beans per cup.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Play with 4 beans per cup.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Plan ahead** 🧠 Count the beans before you pick a cup. Where will the last one land?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Bonus turn hunter** 🔁 Look for cups that land exactly in your store. Chain bonus turns!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**History** 🌍 Mancala games have been played for well over 1,000 years in Africa and Asia. Look up "Oware" or "Bao" and try a different version.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧮 Mancala is all about **counting** and planning ahead.
- 🌍 It's one of the oldest game families in the world.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🫘 Beans are a choking hazard for toddlers.
- 📜 This is the popular "Kalah" version of the rules.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🫘 Beans back in a bag
