# 🧊 Ice Cube Painting

Freeze colored water in egg cups 🧊 … then **paint with ice!** 🎨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A plastic egg carton (cardboard leaks)
- [ ] 💧 Water + food coloring or liquid watercolor
- [ ] 🥄 Popsicle sticks (handles)
- [ ] 📄 Thick paper
- [ ] 🧺 A tray

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fill + color 💧
Water in each cup. A few drops of color. Mix.

### 2️⃣ Add sticks 🥄
Stand a stick in each cup.

### 3️⃣ Freeze overnight 🌙
In the freezer, flat.

### 4️⃣ Pop out + paint 🎨
Slide the ice over the paper. It melts into paint!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Paint a rainbow with ice.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Mixing on paper** 🔀 Slide blue over yellow. What happens?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Melt race** ⏱️ Which melts faster: in the sun or in the shade?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Salt magic** 🧂 Sprinkle salt on the wet painting. Watch the starburst patterns!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧊 Ice melts from your hand's warmth and the room's heat.
- 🧂 Salt soaks up water and pulls color into little bursts.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Food coloring stains hands and clothes for a day. Smock up!
- ☀️ Outside on a warm day = less mess, faster melt.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧺 Dump melted water
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the tray
- [ ] 🖐️ Scrub hands
