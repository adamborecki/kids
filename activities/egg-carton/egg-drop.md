# 🥚 Egg Drop Challenge

Design packaging that keeps a **real egg** safe from a big drop! 🥚🪂 Will it survive?

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 Raw eggs (1 per test + spares)
- [ ] 📦 Egg carton pieces, straws, paper, tape, cotton balls, balloons, plastic bags
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🪜 A safe high spot (a grown-up drops from a deck, window, or ladder)
- [ ] 🧽 Paper towels + a trash bag

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Rules 📋
Same materials for everyone. 20 minutes to build.

### 2️⃣ Design ✏️
Sketch first: cushion? parachute? crumple zone?

### 3️⃣ Build 🏗️
Wrap your egg!

### 4️⃣ DROP! 🪂
**Grown-up drops** each design from the same height.

### 5️⃣ Unwrap 🥚
Cracked? Whole? Higher drop for survivors!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Build and test one design.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Redesign** 🔁 Look at what broke. Change one thing and test again.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Least materials** 🧮 Survive the drop with the fewest materials. Engineers call it efficiency!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🛏️ Cushions make the stop **slower** — slower stops mean less force on the egg.
- 🪂 Parachutes slow the fall before it even lands.
- 🚗 Car crumple zones and airbags use the same idea.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🪜 **Grown-ups do all dropping** from heights. Kids stand back.
- 🥚 Raw egg has germs — wash hands after cleanup.
- 🎈 Balloons are a choking hazard for younger siblings.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥚 Bag up broken eggs right away
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe and hose the landing spot
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands with soap
