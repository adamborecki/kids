# 🔥 Campfire Starters

Make **fire starters** for the next camping trip 🏕️ — a real outdoor skill. Grown-up runs the wax.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 A cardboard egg carton (not foam!)
- [ ] 🧺 Dryer lint or wood shavings
- [ ] 🕯️ Old candle stubs or wax
- [ ] 🍳 An old pot + a can for melting wax (double boiler)
- [ ] 🧤 Oven mitts
- [ ] 📰 Newspaper under everything

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Stuff the cups 🧺
Kid job: pack each cup full of dryer lint.

### 2️⃣ Melt the wax 🍳
**Grown-up only:** melt wax in a can inside a pot of simmering water. Never directly on the stove.

### 3️⃣ Pour 🕯️
**Grown-up pours** wax over each cup to soak the lint.

### 4️⃣ Cool + cut ✂️
When hard, cut into 12 starters. Store in a bag.

### 5️⃣ Use one 🏕️
At a campfire, a grown-up lights one corner. It burns long enough to light kindling.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Stuff the cups. Watch the grown-up pour.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fire triangle** 🔺 Learn the 3 things fire needs: heat, fuel, air. Which does each part give?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Campfire safety plan** 📋 Write the rules: bucket of water nearby, clear area, never leave a fire alone, drown it until cold.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔥 Lint catches fire easily. Wax makes it burn longer and steadier.
- 🔺 Fire needs heat, fuel, and air — remove one and it goes out.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔥 **Grown-up only:** melting and pouring wax. Hot wax burns badly.
- 🍳 Never melt wax directly on a burner — it can catch fire.
- 🔒 Store starters away from heat, out of reach of younger kids.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🕯️ Let leftover wax harden in the can
- [ ] 📰 Toss the newspaper
- [ ] 🧤 Mitts away

## 🖨️ Printable
### 🏕️ Campfire Safety Checklist
- [ ] 🪣 Bucket of water ready
- [ ] 🧹 Clear 3 meters around the fire pit
- [ ] 🧑 A grown-up is in charge
- [ ] 👀 Never leave the fire alone
- [ ] 💦 Drown, stir, drown again — until cold to the touch
