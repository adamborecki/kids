# 🍂 Nature Scavenger Hunt

12 cups, 12 clues 🔍 Fill every cup with **something from nature!** 🍂

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 📋 The clue list (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Write clues in the lid 🖊️
Or use the printable list.

### 2️⃣ Go outside 🌳
Find one thing for each clue.

### 3️⃣ Fill the cups 🥚
One treasure per cup.

### 4️⃣ Show + tell 🗣️
Share your finds!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Picture clues: something round, something green, something soft.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Read the clues yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Harder clues** 🤔 "Something older than you." "Something a bird would use."

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Rainbow hunt** 🌈 One thing in every color. Hardest: blue!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Make clues** ✏️ Write 12 tricky clues for a younger kid.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👀 Clues make you **look closely** at things you'd usually walk past.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🌸 Take only things on the ground — leave living plants alone.
- 🍄 No mushrooms or berries in hands. Look only.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🍂 Put nature back outside
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
