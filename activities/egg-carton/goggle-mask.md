# 🥽 Bug-Eye Goggles

Two egg cups = **giant bug eyes!** 🥽🪰

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥚 An egg carton (2 cups still joined)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up cuts eye holes)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🧶 Elastic or string

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut 2 joined cups ✂️

### 2️⃣ Eye holes 👀
Grown-up cuts a hole in the bottom of each cup.

### 3️⃣ Paint 🎨
Bug colors! Stripes, dots, sparkles.

### 4️⃣ Strap 🧶
Poke a hole on each side. Tie on elastic.

### 5️⃣ Look around 🪰
You're a bug now!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make bug goggles. Buzz around!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Antennae** 📡 Add pipe-cleaner antennae.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Bug facts** 🔍 Flies have **compound eyes** with thousands of tiny lenses! Draw what a fly might see.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Animal eyes** 👁️ Make goggles for another animal (owl, cat). Find out how they see differently.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🪰 Many bugs have compound eyes — great at spotting movement.
- 🦉 Owls have huge eyes for seeing in the dark.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👀 Make sure eye holes are big enough to see safely. No running in goggles!
