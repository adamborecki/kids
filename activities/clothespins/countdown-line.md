# 📅 Countdown Line

Counting down to a birthday, holiday, or trip? **Clip a surprise for every day!** 📅🎁

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧵 String
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins (one per day)
- [ ] ✉️ Small envelopes or folded paper
- [ ] 🖊️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ How many days? 🔢
Count the days until the big day.

### 2️⃣ Number envelopes ✉️
Write a number on each one (count down!).

### 3️⃣ Fill them 💌
A joke, an activity idea, a fun fact, a riddle.

### 4️⃣ Clip them on 🐊
In order on the string.

### 5️⃣ Open one a day 📅

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
A 7-day countdown.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Activities inside** 🎨 "Bake cookies." "Read outside." "Dance party."

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Riddle countdown** 🤔 One riddle per day. Answer in tomorrow's envelope!

### 🔟 Age 10
**For someone else** 🎁 Make one for a sibling's birthday.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Calendar math** 🧮 Count exactly how many days until the event (watch out for months with 30 vs. 31 days!).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📅 Countdowns make waiting feel shorter and more fun.
- 🧮 Counting days across months is tricky math!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💌 Write the envelopes ahead — keep a few secret from the kid if it's for them!

## 🧽 Cleanup
✉️ Save the envelopes for next year
