# 🔤 Clip-a-Word Spelling

Letters on clothespins 🔤 Clip them in order to **spell a word!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 26+ clothespins
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 📇 Index cards with pictures or words

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Letter pins 🖊️
Write one letter on each pin. Extra vowels (A E I O U)!

### 2️⃣ Word cards 📇
Draw a picture (🐱) or write the word on a card.

### 3️⃣ Spell it 🤏
Clip letters along the card's edge to spell the word.

### 4️⃣ Check ✅
Write the word on the back to check.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Spelling list** 📝 Use this week's spelling words from school.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔤 Moving letters in order helps your brain build the word.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🐊 Store letter pins on a ribbon in ABC order.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Pins back in ABC order
