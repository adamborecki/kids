# 📈 Clip-Up Goal Chart

Move your clothespin **UP** every time you reach a goal! 📈🐊

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📄 A long strip of paper or cardboard
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers
- [ ] 🐊 1 clothespin per person (with names)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make levels 🪜
5 levels on the strip: START → GOOD → GREAT → AMAZING → GOAL! 🏆

### 2️⃣ Pick a goal 🎯
Read 5 books. Make your bed 7 days. Practice piano 5 times.

### 3️⃣ Clip your pin at START 🐊

### 4️⃣ Move it up ⬆️
Every step toward your goal = one level up.

### 5️⃣ Celebrate 🎉
Reach the top? Celebrate!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Simple goal: brush teeth every night for a week.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Pick your own goal.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Two goals** 🎯🎯 Two strips, two pins.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**SMART goals** 🧠 Make it specific and countable: "Read 20 minutes, 5 days this week."

### 🔟 Age 10
**Track + reflect** 📝 At the top, write what helped and what was hard.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Long goal** 📅 A month-long goal with weekly levels. Plan the steps.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📈 Seeing progress keeps you motivated.
- 🎯 Specific goals are easier to reach than vague ones.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💛 Celebrate effort, not just finishing.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Pin stays on the chart
