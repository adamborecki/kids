# 🛍️ Labeled Bag Clips

Keep snack bags closed AND labeled! 🛍️🐊 Real kitchen helper job.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker (or masking tape + marker)
- [ ] 🛍️ Open snack bags in the pantry

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Find open bags 🔍
Chips, cereal, crackers.

### 2️⃣ Label the pins 🏷️
CHIPS · CEREAL · PRETZELS · OPENED: (date)

### 3️⃣ Roll + clip 🛍️
Roll the top of the bag down. Clip!

### 4️⃣ Check weekly ✅
Stale? Empty? Tell a grown-up.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Clip 3 bags.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add the **date opened** 📅

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Stale test** 🧪 Leave one cracker unclipped overnight, one clipped. Taste test. Which is crunchier?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pantry patrol** 🔍 Clip and label every open bag in the pantry.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Why stale?** 🧠 Look up why air makes crackers soft and chips stale. Write 2 sentences.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💨 Air carries moisture. Moisture makes crunchy snacks soggy.
- 🐊 Clipping keeps air out = snacks stay crunchy!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🏷️ Pairs well with 🥫 Pantry Patrol in the label maker section.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Extra pins back in the drawer
