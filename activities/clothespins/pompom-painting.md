# 🎨 Pom-Pom Clip Painting

Clip a pom-pom in a clothespin = **a fluffy paintbrush!** 🎨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms
- [ ] 🎨 Washable paint on plates
- [ ] 📄 Paper
- [ ] 👕 Smock

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make brushes 🐊
Clip a pom-pom in each clothespin.

### 2️⃣ One brush per color 🎨
No mixing (unless you want to!).

### 3️⃣ Dab! 🖌️
Dot, dot, dot. Make pictures with dots.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Dot a rainbow, flowers, or a polka-dot animal.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Dot art** 🔵 Make a whole picture only from dots (like the artist Georges Seurat!).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Pictures made of tiny dots = **pointillism**. From far away, dots blend together!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Smock + newspaper. Pom-poms soak up a lot of paint.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔴 Toss painty pom-poms
- [ ] 🐊 Wash clothespins
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
