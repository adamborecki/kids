# 🌈 Color Match Clip

Paint clothespins in rainbow colors 🌈 then **clip each one to its matching color!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers (6 colors)
- [ ] 🍽️ A paper plate

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Color the plate 🍽️
Divide the edge into 6 color sections, like a pie.

### 2️⃣ Color the pins 🎨
Paint one end of each pin to match.

### 3️⃣ Mix 🔀

### 4️⃣ Clip! 🤏
Match each pin to its color.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Match all 6 colors. Name them out loud.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Shades** 🎨 Use light + dark of each color (light blue, dark blue). Harder!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌈 Matching colors trains your eyes to notice differences.
- 🤏 Clipping builds finger strength.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Paint the pins a day ahead so they're dry.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Store pins clipped on the plate
