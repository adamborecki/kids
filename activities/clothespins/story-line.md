# 📖 Clothesline Story

Hang story pictures on a string 🧵 **in the right order!** 📖

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧵 String (hung between 2 chairs)
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 📄 Paper cards (draw on them)
- [ ] 🖍️ Crayons

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a story 📖
A favorite book, or something you did today.

### 2️⃣ Draw 4–6 scenes 🖍️
One scene per card.

### 3️⃣ Mix them up 🔀

### 4️⃣ Hang in order 🐊
Beginning → middle → end!

### 5️⃣ Tell it 🗣️
Point to each card and tell the story.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
3 cards: beginning, middle, end.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
5 cards from a book you read.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Your day** 📅 Morning to bedtime in 6 cards.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Mixed-up mystery** 🕵️ Make cards for someone else. Can they put them in order?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📖 Putting events in order = **sequencing**, a big reading skill.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧵 Hang the line low enough for kids to reach.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧵 Roll up the string
