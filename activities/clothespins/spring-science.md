# 🔬 Spring Science

Take a clothespin apart 🔧 How does the **spring** work? Then put it back together!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Wooden clothespins (2–3)
- [ ] 🥽 Safety glasses (springs can jump!)
- [ ] 🔍 Magnifier
- [ ] 📏 Ruler

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Safety glasses on 🥽

### 2️⃣ Take it apart 🔧
Twist the two wooden halves sideways. The spring pops loose.

### 3️⃣ Look closely 🔍
The spring is a coil with two long arms.

### 4️⃣ Put it back 🧩
Hook the spring in the notches. Squeeze the halves on. (Tricky!)

### 5️⃣ Find the lever ⚖️
Where do you push? Where's the pivot? Where's the grip?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Take it apart and rebuild it.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Lever lab** ⚖️ Push near the tip vs. near the spring. Which is easier? Why?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Spring force** 📏 Clip it on a ruler hanging from string. Add coins to the ruler until it slips. Compare 2 brands of clothespins.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌀 The spring stores energy when you squeeze — and pushes back when you let go.
- ⚖️ A clothespin is two **levers** joined at a pivot. Pushing far from the pivot is easier.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥽 Springs can fly out. Safety glasses, pointed away from faces.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Rebuilt pins back in the bag
