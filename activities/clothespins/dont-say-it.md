# 🤐 Don't Say It!

Everyone wears 3 clothespins. Say the **forbidden word**? Someone steals your pin! 🤐🐊

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 3 clothespins per player
- [ ] 👨‍👩‍👧 3+ players (great for parties and long car rides)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Clip on 🐊
Everyone clips 3 pins on their shirt.

### 2️⃣ Pick the forbidden word 🚫
"Yes," "no," "like," or "um."

### 3️⃣ Talk normally 🗣️
Ask each other sneaky questions to trick them!

### 4️⃣ Caught! 🫵
Say the word → whoever catches you takes one of your pins.

### 5️⃣ Winner 🏆
After 20 minutes, most pins wins!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Forbidden word: "yes." Ask lots of yes/no questions!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Two words** 🚫🚫 "Yes" AND "no."

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Sneaky questions** 🕵️ Plan 5 tricky questions on the printable.

### 🔟 Age 10
**All day** 📅 Play during a whole family outing.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Filler words** 🧠 Forbid "um" and "like." Great practice for speeches!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Stopping a habit word means thinking before you speak.
- 🎤 Speakers practice dropping "um" and "like" to sound confident.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🐊 Clip on clothes, not skin!

🐊 Collect all pins
