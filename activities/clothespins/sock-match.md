# 🧦 Sock Match Laundry Helper

Real chore, real clothespins! 🧺 Match socks and **hang them up** like a pro.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧦 A basket of clean, damp laundry (or just socks)
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🧵 A drying rack or clothesline

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Dump the socks 🧺

### 2️⃣ Find pairs 👀
Match by color, size, and pattern.

### 3️⃣ Clip pairs together 🐊
One clothespin per pair.

### 4️⃣ Hang them 🧵
On the line or rack.

### 5️⃣ Lonely socks 😢
Put single socks in a "lost sock" bin.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Match 5 pairs.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Match all the socks. Count the pairs.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sort by owner** 👨‍👩‍👧 Whose socks are whose?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Hang a full load** 🧺 Shirts by the hem, pants by the waist.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Lost sock detective** 🕵️ Track lost socks for a week. Where do they go?!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Laundry master** 🧺 Learn to run the washer with a grown-up: sort, settings, detergent amount.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧦 Matching = noticing small differences (great for reading too!).
- ☀️ Line-drying uses sun and wind instead of electricity.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧺 Pay with praise, points, or allowance — it's real work!

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Clothespins back in the bag
