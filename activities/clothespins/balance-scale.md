# ⚖️ Hanger Balance Scale

A coat hanger + 2 cups + clothespins = **a real balance scale!** ⚖️ Which is heavier?

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧥 A plastic coat hanger (with notches)
- [ ] 🥤 2 paper cups
- [ ] 🧵 String
- [ ] 🐊 2 clothespins
- [ ] 🪙 Pennies, LEGO, crayons, small toys

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the pans 🥤
Poke 2 holes in each cup rim. Tie string handles.

### 2️⃣ Hang them 🐊
Clip each cup's string to an end of the hanger with a clothespin.

### 3️⃣ Hang the hanger 🧥
On a doorknob or a broom across 2 chairs.

### 4️⃣ Level it ⚖️
Empty cups should balance. Slide clips to adjust.

### 5️⃣ Compare! 🪙
Which is heavier: 5 crayons or 1 toy car?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Which is heavier? Predict, then test.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Penny weight** 🪙 How many pennies does a crayon weigh?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Order by weight** 📊 Rank 5 objects from lightest to heaviest.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Same size, different weight** 🤔 A cotton ball vs. a marble. Size doesn't tell weight!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Grams** ⚖️ A US penny made after 1982 weighs about 2.5 grams. Use that to estimate other objects' weights in grams.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⚖️ The heavier side pulls down. When both sides are equal, the scale is level — **balanced**.
- 🏛️ Balance scales have been used for thousands of years.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🪙 Pennies are small — keep away from toddlers.
