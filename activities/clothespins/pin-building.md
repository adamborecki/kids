# 🏗️ Clothespin Sculptures

Clip clothespins to each other and to popsicle sticks… **build sculptures!** 🏗️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 30+ clothespins
- [ ] 🪵 Popsicle sticks or cardboard strips
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Learn the trick 🤏
Clothespins clip onto sticks AND onto each other.

### 2️⃣ Build 🏗️
Towers, stars, animals, bridges.

### 3️⃣ Challenges ⏱️
Tallest in 5 minutes? A shape that stands alone?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build a star ⭐ and a fence.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Animal** 🦕 Build a dinosaur or a dog.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tallest tower** 🗼 5 minutes. Measure it!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Bridge** 🌉 Span 20 cm between 2 books. How much can it hold?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Self-standing** 🏛️ Build something with no base that stands up on its own. Explain why it's stable.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏗️ Wider bases = more stable structures.
- 🤏 Clips grip by spring force — they squeeze tight.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🐊 Plastic clothespins clip better to each other than wooden ones.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Pins back in the bag
