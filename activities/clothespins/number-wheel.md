# 🎡 Clip-the-Answer Wheel

A paper plate wheel of questions 🎡 **Clip the right answer!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🍽️ A paper plate
- [ ] 🐊 10 clothespins
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Write problems on the plate 🖊️
Around the edge: 3+4, 2+5, 9+1…

### 2️⃣ Write answers on clothespins 🐊
One answer per clothespin.

### 3️⃣ Mix them up 🔀

### 4️⃣ Clip! 🤏
Find each answer and clip it next to its problem.

### 5️⃣ Check ✅
Write the answers on the back of the plate to self-check.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Count dots → clip the number.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add to 20.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Times tables 2, 5, 10.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Mixed times tables to 10 × 10.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🤏 Clipping = finger strength. Matching = math practice. Two in one!
- ✅ Self-checking on the back means no waiting for a grown-up.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍽️ Make several plates for different skills. Store them with clips on.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Clip pins on the plate edge for storage
