# 🧲 Fridge Magnet Clips

Glue a magnet on a clothespin = **a fridge clip** for notes and art! 🧲

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🧲 Small craft magnets (strong ones)
- [ ] 🖊️ Strong glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paint, washi tape, or stickers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Paint or wrap with washi tape.

### 2️⃣ Glue the magnet 🧲
On the flat back side. Press and hold.

### 3️⃣ Dry overnight 🌙

### 4️⃣ Stick it on the fridge 🧊
Clip notes, art, or the lunch menu!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make 3 clips.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Themed set** 🎨 Matching set: fruits, animals, or emojis.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Magnet test** 🧲 Which magnets hold the most paper? Count sheets before it slides.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Gift set** 🎁 Make 5 for a grandparent. Package them nicely.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Magnet science** 🔬 Test what your magnet sticks to around the house. What do they have in common? (Hint: iron!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧲 Magnets stick to metals with **iron** in them, like steel fridge doors.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧲 **Swallowed magnets are dangerous.** Keep loose magnets away from little kids and pets.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧲 Count and store leftover magnets safely
