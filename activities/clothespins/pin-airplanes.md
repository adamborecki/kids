# ✈️ Clothespin Airplanes

A clothespin body + popsicle stick wings = **a tiny airplane!** ✈️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🪵 Popsicle sticks (2 per plane: wings + tail)
- [ ] 🖊️ Strong glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint parts 🎨
Body, wings, tail. Let dry.

### 2️⃣ Wings 🪵
Glue one stick across the top of the clothespin.

### 3️⃣ Tail 🪵
Break a stick in half (grown-up). Glue a small piece at the back.

### 4️⃣ Pilot 👩‍✈️
Draw a pilot face on the front.

### 5️⃣ Fly! ✈️
Zoom it around (it won't really fly — you're the engine!).

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Build one plane. Name it!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Airline** ✈️ Build 3 planes with the same colors and logo.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Airport** 🛬 Make a runway on paper with gates and a control tower.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Real planes** 🔍 Copy a real airplane's colors. Why are wings shaped curved on top? Look up "lift."

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ✈️ Real wings are curved so air pushes them **up** — that's called **lift**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖊️ Wood glue or tacky glue holds best. Dry overnight.

- [ ] 🎨 Wash brushes
