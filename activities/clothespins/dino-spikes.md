# 🦕 Dino Clip Spikes

Cut a dinosaur from cardboard 🦕 Clothespins become its **spikes and legs!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 Cardboard (cereal box)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins (6–10)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw a dino 🦕
A big body shape on cardboard. No legs!

### 2️⃣ Cut it out ✂️

### 3️⃣ Color 🖍️

### 4️⃣ Legs 🐊
Clip 4 clothespins on the bottom. Now it stands!

### 5️⃣ Spikes 🐊
Clip pins along the back — stegosaurus style!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make one dino that stands up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Spike count** 🔢 Write a number on each spike. Count them!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Dino facts** 🔍 Stegosaurus had plates, not spikes, on its back (and spikes on its tail!). Find out what the plates might have been for.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🦕 4 legs spread out = stable. That's why it stands!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Grown-up cuts thick cardboard.

## 🧽 Cleanup
✂️ Scraps in recycling
