# 👨‍👩‍👧 Clothespin People

Turn clothespins into **tiny people** — your whole family! 👨‍👩‍👧

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Wooden clothespins (the one-piece "peg" kind is best, but any works)
- [ ] 🖊️ Fine markers
- [ ] 🧶 Yarn (hair)
- [ ] ✂️ Fabric or paper scraps (clothes)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Face 😊
Draw a face near the top.

### 2️⃣ Hair 🧶
Glue on yarn.

### 3️⃣ Clothes 👕
Wrap fabric or paper around the middle. Glue.

### 4️⃣ Make the family 👨‍👩‍👧
Everyone gets a mini-me!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make yourself and one more person.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make your whole family (and the pet!) 🐶

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Story world** 📖 Make characters from a book you love. Act out a scene!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Careers** 👩‍🚒 Make 5 people with different jobs. Each needs a tool or uniform.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Tiny details (hair, clothes) make characters recognizable.
- 🎭 Mini people are great for storytelling.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖊️ Tacky glue holds yarn best.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧶 Yarn scraps in the craft box
