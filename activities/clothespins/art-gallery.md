# 🖼️ Art Gallery Line

String + clothespins = a **gallery wall** for your art! 🖼️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧵 String or yarn (2 meters)
- [ ] 🐊 Clothespins
- [ ] 🟦 Painter's tape or 2 hooks
- [ ] 🖼️ Your drawings

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Hang the line 🧵
Tape or hook both ends on a wall. Let it sag a little.

### 2️⃣ Pick the art 🎨
Your best 5–10 pieces.

### 3️⃣ Clip 'em up 🐊
Space them out.

### 4️⃣ Gallery opening 🎉
Invite family for a tour!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Hang 5 pictures. Tell the story of each one.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Title cards** 🏷️ Clip a small card under each: title + artist.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Themes** 🎨 Change the show every week (animals, space, family).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Curator** 🧐 Choose the order. Why does that one go first?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Artist statement** ✍️ Write 3 sentences about your art.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Critique** 💬 Family leaves sticky notes: one thing they love, one question.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🖼️ Displaying work makes artists proud — and want to make more!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🟦 Painter's tape is gentle on walls.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖼️ Store old art in a folder
