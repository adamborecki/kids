# ⏰ Clip-the-Clock

Make a paper plate clock ⏰ and **clip on the minutes!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🍽️ A paper plate
- [ ] 🐊 12 clothespins
- [ ] 🖊️ Marker
- [ ] 📌 A paper fastener + 2 paper strips (hands)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Numbers 🖊️
Write 1–12 around the plate like a clock.

### 2️⃣ Minute pins 🐊
Write 5, 10, 15… 60 on 12 clothespins.

### 3️⃣ Clip them on 🤏
Clip each minute pin next to the right hour number (5 goes by the 1, 10 by the 2…).

### 4️⃣ Hands 📌
Short hour hand + long minute hand on a paper fastener.

### 5️⃣ Tell time! ⏰
Grown-up says a time. You set the clock!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
O'clock times: 3:00, 7:00.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Half past and quarter past: 3:30, 7:15.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Any 5 minutes: 4:25, 9:50.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⏰ Each number on a clock = 5 minutes for the minute hand.
- 🔢 That's skip-counting by 5s!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ⏰ Practice with real times: "Dinner is at 6:30. Show me!"

## 🧽 Cleanup
⏰ Hang the clock on the wall
