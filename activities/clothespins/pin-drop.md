# 🫙 Clothespin Drop

Stand tall, hold a pin at your nose, and **drop it into the jar!** 🫙🎯

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 10 clothespins per player
- [ ] 🫙 A jar or bottle with a wide mouth

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Jar on the floor 🫙
Right at your feet.

### 2️⃣ Nose height 👃
Stand straight. Hold a pin at your nose.

### 3️⃣ Drop! ⬇️
Let go. Did it go in?

### 4️⃣ 10 tries 🔢
Count how many land in the jar!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Use a big bowl instead of a jar.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Use a jar. Count your score.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Narrow neck** 🍾 Use a bottle. Way harder!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Percent** 🔢 Score out of 10 = your percent × 10. 7 out of 10 = 70%.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Technique test** 🧠 Hold it vertical vs. sideways. Which lands in more?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Tournament** 🏆 Best of 3 rounds, with a bracket.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⬇️ Falling objects can tumble. Holding it straight helps it fall straight.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🫙 Plastic jars are safest.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Pins back in the bag
