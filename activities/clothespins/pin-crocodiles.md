# 🐊 Clothespin Crocodiles

Paint a clothespin green 🐊 … add eyes … **CHOMP CHOMP!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 Wooden clothespins
- [ ] 🎨 Green paint or marker
- [ ] 👀 Googly eyes or a black marker
- [ ] ✂️ White paper (teeth)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint green 🎨
Let dry.

### 2️⃣ Eyes 👀
Glue 2 eyes near the top of the "mouth."

### 3️⃣ Teeth ✂️
Cut a white zigzag strip. Glue inside the jaws.

### 4️⃣ CHOMP! 🐊
Squeeze to open. Let go to chomp!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
**Croc snacks** 🍪 The croc chomps paper "snacks." Count how many it eats!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Number croc** 🔢 The croc eats the BIGGER number! Line up number cards. The croc chomps the bigger one in each pair. (That's the > sign!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🤏 Pinching clothespins builds the hand muscles for writing.
- 🐊 The "hungry croc eats the bigger number" trick helps remember > and <.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Washable paint dries in about 20 minutes.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🎨 Wash brushes
