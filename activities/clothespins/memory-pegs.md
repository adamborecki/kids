# 🧠 Memory Peg Game

Stickers hidden under clothespins 🐊 Flip two… **find the matching pair!** 🧠

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 12–20 clothespins
- [ ] 🌟 Matching sticker pairs (or draw matching pictures)
- [ ] 📦 A box edge or paper plate

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Sticker the pins 🌟
A sticker on the inside jaw of each pin. Pairs must match!

### 2️⃣ Clip face-down 🐊
Clip them around a box edge so stickers are hidden.

### 3️⃣ Take turns 🔄
Open 2 pins. Match? Keep them! No match? Clip them back.

### 4️⃣ Most pairs wins 🏆

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
6 pairs.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
8 pairs.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
10 pairs.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Math pairs** ➕ Match a problem to its answer (3×4 and 12).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Remembering where things are = **working memory**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🌟 Picture stickers are easier than letters for younger kids.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Store pins clipped on the box
