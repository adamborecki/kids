# 💪 60-Second Clip Challenge

How many clothespins can you clip in **60 seconds?** ⏱️💪

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 20–30 clothespins
- [ ] 📦 A box edge, cup rim, or paper plate
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pile the pins 🐊

### 2️⃣ Ready… GO! ⏱️
Clip them onto the edge, one at a time.

### 3️⃣ STOP! 🛑
Count them.

### 4️⃣ Beat your record 🏆
Try again!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Use your strong hand. Count to 10 pins.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Weak hand** 🤚 Try your other hand. Harder?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Two hands race** 🙌 One pin per hand at a time.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Graph it** 📈 Try once a day for a week. Do you get faster?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pinch only** 🤏 Use just thumb + one finger.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Practice effect** 📊 Track 10 tries. Graph them. When did you stop improving? (That's a "plateau.")

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💪 Your hand gets stronger and faster with practice.
- 🧠 Your brain also learns the movement — that's **muscle memory**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤏 Fun warm-up before handwriting.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🐊 Pins back in the bag
