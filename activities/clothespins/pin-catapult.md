# 🏹 Clothespin Catapult

A clothespin + 2 popsicle sticks = **a mini catapult!** 🏹 Launch pom-poms at targets.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 1 clothespin
- [ ] 🪵 2 popsicle sticks
- [ ] ⚪ A bottle cap (the cup)
- [ ] 🖊️ Strong glue
- [ ] 🔴 Pom-poms
- [ ] 🥤 Cups (targets)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Base 🪵
Glue the bottom jaw of the clothespin to one popsicle stick.

### 2️⃣ Arm 🪵
Glue the second stick to the top jaw, sticking out the front.

### 3️⃣ Cup ⚪
Glue the bottle cap on the end of the arm.

### 4️⃣ Dry 🌙
Let glue dry fully.

### 5️⃣ Launch! 🏹
Hold the base. Press the arm down. Let go!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Knock down a cup pyramid.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Distance** 📏 Measure 5 launches. Average them.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Ammo test** 🔬 Pom-pom vs. paper ball vs. eraser. Which flies farthest?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Aim for a target** 🎯 Put a cup 1 meter away. Adjust your angle (prop the base on books) until you land in it 3 times in a row.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌀 The spring stores energy. The arm turns it into a launch.
- 📐 Launch angle changes the distance.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🚫 **Never aim at faces or pets.** Soft ammo only.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔴 Collect ammo
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue away
