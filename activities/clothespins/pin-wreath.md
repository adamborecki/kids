# 🌸 Clothespin Wreath Frame

Clip-and-glue clothespins around a ring for a **wreath or photo frame!** 🌸🖼️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐊 30–40 clothespins
- [ ] ⭕ A cardboard ring (cut from a box) or a wire wreath ring
- [ ] 🔥 Hot glue gun (**grown-up only**) or strong tacky glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paint
- [ ] 🖼️ Photos or flowers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Paint the pins 🎨
Let dry.

### 2️⃣ Clip around the ring 🐊
Clip pins all the way around, close together, pointing out (like sunbeams!).

### 3️⃣ Glue them 🔥
**Grown-up hot-glues** each pin in place.

### 4️⃣ Decorate 🌸
Clip photos, paper flowers, or holiday decorations in the pins.

### 5️⃣ Hang it 🪝

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Make it and decorate with paper flowers.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Photo wreath** 📸 Clip family photos in each pin.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Seasons** 🍂 Swap decorations for each season or holiday.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Design it** ✏️ Plan a color pattern for all the pins on paper first.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌞 Pins pointing outward from a circle = **radial** design, like sunbeams or flower petals.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔥 Hot glue burns — grown-up only. Kids can use tacky glue (slower drying).

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔥 Unplug the glue gun and let it cool on a plate
- [ ] 🎨 Wash brushes
