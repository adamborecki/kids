# 🏴‍☠️ Treasure Chest

Turn a box into a **treasure chest** for your most special stuff. 🏴‍☠️💎

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box (cut the top into a flap lid)
- [ ] 🎨 Brown + gold paint, or brown paper + yellow markers
- [ ] 💎 Stickers, gems, foil
- [ ] 🔗 String or a button (latch)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make a lid ✂️
Cut 3 sides of the top so it flips open (grown-up helps).

### 2️⃣ Paint it 🎨
Brown wood planks + gold bands.

### 3️⃣ Bling 💎
Gems, stickers, a skull-and-crossbones or your initials.

### 4️⃣ Latch 🔗
A button + string loop to close it.

### 5️⃣ Fill it 💎
Your treasures!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Paint it and fill it.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add a treasure map to keep inside 🗺️

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Treasure list** 📋 Write what's inside and why each thing is special.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Secret code lock** 🔢 Write a riddle on the lid. Only people who solve it may open it!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pirate log** 🏴‍☠️ Write a pirate story about where the treasure came from.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Hidden compartment** 🕵️ Make a false bottom with cardboard. Hide a note underneath!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💎 Special things deserve a special home.
- 🕵️ False bottoms were really used to hide things in old trunks.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Lid cutting is easier with grown-up help.
