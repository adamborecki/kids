# 🐞 Bug Hotel

Build a cozy hotel for **garden bugs!** 🐞🏨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box (cut the top off, or use it sideways)
- [ ] 🌿 Sticks, pinecones, bark, dry leaves
- [ ] 🧃 Straws or hollow stems
- [ ] 🧶 String

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Collect materials 🌿
Dry stuff from the yard.

### 2️⃣ Stuff the box 🪵
Pack it tight with sticks, pinecones, straws, and leaves. Bugs like tiny gaps!

### 3️⃣ Find a spot 🌳
A sheltered, shady place: under a bush, against a fence.

### 4️⃣ Check weekly 🔍
Look carefully. Who moved in?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build it and check it every week.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Rooms** 🏨 Different materials in different sections. Which room is most popular?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Guest log** 📋 Draw every bug you find. Use a bug guide to name them.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Waterproof it** ☔ Add a plastic "roof" so it lasts longer outside.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Why bugs matter** 🌍 Research pollinators. Write 3 reasons gardens need bugs.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐝 Many helpful bugs need small, dry hiding places.
- 🌸 Some bugs pollinate flowers. Others eat garden pests!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📦 Cardboard falls apart in rain — expect to rebuild. That's OK.
- 🐝 Look, don't touch.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖐️ Wash hands after collecting
