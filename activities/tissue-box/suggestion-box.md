# 🗳️ Family Suggestion Box

Got an idea? **Drop it in the box!** 💡 Read them at family meeting. 🗳️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🎨 Paper + markers to decorate
- [ ] 📝 Small paper slips + pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Label it: **IDEAS 💡** or **SUGGESTIONS**.

### 2️⃣ Set rules 📋
Ideas must be kind. Sign your name (or not — your choice).

### 3️⃣ Put it somewhere everyone walks by 🚶
The kitchen counter works.

### 4️⃣ Weekly opening 📬
Read every slip at a family meeting. Talk about each one.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Draw or write 1 idea a week.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Ideas for **fun**: game night themes, weekend trips 🎳

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Ideas for **fixing** things: "We always lose the remote" → "Remote basket!"

### 🔟 Age 10
**Meeting leader** 📋 Run the opening: read slips, take votes, write decisions.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Follow-up** ✅ Keep a list: which ideas happened? Which didn't? Why?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗣️ Everyone gets a voice — even quiet people.
- 💡 Writing ideas down helps you think them through.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤝 Take every idea seriously (even "ice cream for dinner" — maybe once?).

## 🧽 Cleanup
📝 Keep the box stocked with slips
