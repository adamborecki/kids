# 🔢 Number Drop Math

Toss into numbered boxes 🎯 then **do the math!** 🔢

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 3–5 tissue boxes
- [ ] 📄 Paper + marker (number signs)
- [ ] 🧦 Rolled socks or bean bags
- [ ] 🟨 Tape for a throw line

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Number the boxes 🔢
Tape a big number on each: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10.

### 2️⃣ Line them up 📏
Close boxes = small numbers. Far boxes = big numbers.

### 3️⃣ Toss 3 🧦
From behind the line.

### 4️⃣ Do the math ➕
Add up where your socks landed!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
**Add** 2 tosses.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Add** 3 tosses.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Subtract** 🔢 Start at 50. Subtract each toss. First to 0 wins!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Multiply** ✖️ Multiply your 2 tosses.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Target number** 🎯 Call a target (like 24). Use +, −, × with your 3 tosses to hit it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏃 Moving + math = your brain remembers it better.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧦 Rolled socks are perfect indoor bean bags.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧦 Socks back in the drawer
- [ ] 🟨 Peel tape
