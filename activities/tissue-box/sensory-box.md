# 💎 Hidden Treasure Sensory Box

Dig through the rice 🍚 to find **hidden treasures!** 💎

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 A tissue box with the top cut off (or 2 boxes)
- [ ] 🍚 Dry rice or dried beans
- [ ] 💎 Small treasures: coins, toy figures, beads, buttons
- [ ] 🥄 Spoons and scoops
- [ ] 🧺 A big tray or towel underneath

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fill + hide 🍚
Pour in rice. Bury 10 treasures.

### 2️⃣ Dig 🥄
Use hands or spoons.

### 3️⃣ Count 🔢
How many did you find?

### 4️⃣ Hide again 🔁
Your turn to hide for someone else!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Find all 10. Sort them by color or type.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Treasure list** 📋 Check off each item on the list as you find it. Time yourself!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🖐️ Digging and pinching build hand muscles for writing.
- 🧘 Running fingers through rice is calming.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🫘 Small items and beans are choking hazards for babies and toddlers.
- 🍚 Rice WILL spill. Tray + towel underneath.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🍚 Pour rice into a bag to reuse
- [ ] 🧹 Sweep or vacuum strays
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
