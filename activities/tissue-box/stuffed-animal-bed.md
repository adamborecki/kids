# 🛏️ Stuffy Bed & Bedroom

Every stuffed animal deserves a **cozy bed!** 🧸🛏️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box (cut the top off)
- [ ] 🧦 A sock or washcloth (blanket)
- [ ] 🧸 A small stuffed animal
- [ ] 🎨 Markers + paper

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the bed 🛏️
Cut the top off the box (grown-up helps). Stuff a tissue or cloth inside as a mattress.

### 2️⃣ Blanket + pillow 🧦
A washcloth blanket. A folded sock pillow.

### 3️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Draw a headboard. Add the stuffy's name.

### 4️⃣ Bedtime! 🌙
Read your stuffy a story and tuck it in.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make the bed. Tuck in your stuffy. Sing a lullaby 🎵

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Bedroom** 🏠 Add a nightstand (small box), a lamp (bottle cap), a rug.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Hotel** 🏨 Make beds for 3 stuffies. Check them in with name cards!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧸 Caring for a "pet" stuffy practices kindness and routines.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Cutting the top off is easier for grown-ups.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🛏️ Bed goes on the shelf
