# 🎟️ Family Raffle

Earn tickets all week 🎟️ … then **draw a winner!** 🏆

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box (the raffle box)
- [ ] 🎟️ Ticket slips (printable)
- [ ] 🖊️ Pen
- [ ] 🎁 A prize (pick the movie, skip a chore, choose dessert)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decide how to earn 🎟️
A ticket for: chores, kindness, trying something new, reading 20 minutes.

### 2️⃣ Write your name ✍️
On each ticket you earn. Drop it in!

### 3️⃣ Draw day 🥁
At the end of the week, shake the box. Draw one ticket!

### 4️⃣ Winner! 🏆
The name drawn wins the prize.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Earn tickets for chores. Draw on Friday.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Track it** 📋 Keep a tally of everyone's tickets.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Chances** 🧮 If you have 5 tickets out of 20, what are your chances? (5 in 20 = 1 in 4!)

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fair rules** ⚖️ Write the rules: how many tickets max per day? What counts?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Percent** 📊 Turn each person's chance into a percent before the draw.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎟️ More tickets = better chances, but never a sure thing.
- 🧮 Chance = your tickets ÷ all tickets.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💛 Small prizes work best (experiences > stuff).

## 🧽 Cleanup
🎟️ Empty the box after each draw
