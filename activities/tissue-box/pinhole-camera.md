# 📷 Pinhole Camera

See the world **upside down** with a box and a tiny hole! 📷🙃

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🔪 Craft knife (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🥈 Aluminum foil + 📌 a pin
- [ ] 📄 Wax paper or tracing paper
- [ ] ⚫ Black tape or black paper
- [ ] 🧣 A dark towel

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Grown-up cuts both ends 🔪
Cut a square window in each short end of the box.

### 2️⃣ Tape the top shut ⚫
Cover the tissue hole with black paper. Light-tight!

### 3️⃣ Foil + pinhole 🥈
Tape foil over one window. Poke ONE tiny hole with the pin.

### 4️⃣ Screen 📄
Tape wax paper over the other window.

### 5️⃣ Look! 👀
Point the pinhole at a bright window. Put the towel over your head and the screen end. Look at the screen!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
See the upside-down image. Wave your hand in front of the hole!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Hole size** 🔬 Make a second hole bigger. Brighter but blurrier — why?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Explain it** ✏️ Draw how light rays cross through the tiny hole and flip the image. This is how every camera (and your eye!) works.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💡 Light travels in straight lines.
- 🔄 Rays from the top of a tree go through the hole and hit the **bottom** of the screen — so the image flips!
- 👁️ Your eye does this too. Your brain flips it back.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Craft knife is a grown-up job.
- 😎 **Never** point it at the sun or look at the sun.
