# 🎸 Rubber Band Guitar

Stretch rubber bands over the hole… **strum!** 🎸🎶

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🔗 4–6 rubber bands (different thicknesses)
- [ ] ✏️ 2 pencils (bridges)
- [ ] 🧻 A paper towel tube (neck — optional)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Stretch the bands 🔗
Wrap rubber bands around the box, over the hole.

### 2️⃣ Add bridges ✏️
Slide a pencil under the bands on each side of the hole. They lift the strings.

### 3️⃣ Add a neck 🧻
Tape a tube to one end.

### 4️⃣ Strum! 🎸
Pluck each band. Which is lowest?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Strum and sing! 🎤

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Thick vs thin** 🎵 Which band sounds lowest?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Order them** 📏 Arrange bands from lowest to highest.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tighter = higher** 🔧 Pull a band tighter. What happens to the sound?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Play a tune** 🎼 Find the notes for "Mary Had a Little Lamb."

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Explain it** 🧠 Write how thickness, tension, and length change pitch. Test each one.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎵 Plucking makes the band vibrate. The box makes the sound louder.
- 🔧 Tighter or thinner = faster vibration = higher pitch.
- 🎸 Real guitars work the same way!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔗 Rubber bands can snap. Keep faces back when stretching.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🎸 Keep it on the music shelf
