# 🎲 Giant Dice

A cube tissue box = a **GIANT DIE!** 🎲 Roll it for games.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 A cube-shaped tissue box
- [ ] 📄 Paper to cover it
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cover the hole 📄
Tape paper over the top so all 6 sides are flat.

### 2️⃣ Draw the dots 🎲
1 to 6. **Opposite sides add up to 7!** (1 across from 6, 2 from 5, 3 from 4.)

### 3️⃣ Roll it! 🎲
Toss it across the floor.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
**Move game** 🏃 Roll it and do that many jumps.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Action dice** 🤸 Make a second die with actions: hop, spin, clap, squat, wiggle, freeze!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Math dice** ➕ Roll 2 dice. Add them. First to 50 wins.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Probability** 📊 Roll 30 times. Tally each number. Are they about even?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎲 On real dice, opposite sides always add up to 7.
- 📊 Each side has a 1 in 6 chance — but short runs can look uneven!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎲 Cube tissue boxes work best. Rectangle ones don't roll fair.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🎲 Store with the games
