# 👾 Hungry Box Monster

The monster is **HUNGRY!** 👾 Feed it paper balls through its mouth! 😋

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🎨 Paint, markers, colored paper
- [ ] 👀 Googly eyes or paper eyes
- [ ] 📄 Scrap paper (monster food)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the monster 🎨
The hole is its mouth! Add teeth (paper triangles), eyes, horns.

### 2️⃣ Name it 🏷️
"Chompy"? "Mr. Munch"?

### 3️⃣ Make food 📄
Crumple scrap paper into balls.

### 4️⃣ FEED IT! 😋
Toss balls into the mouth. Count every bite!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Feed it 10 paper balls. Count out loud.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Step back** 👣 One step back after every 3 bites.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Picky eater** 🧐 The monster only eats words that rhyme with CAT (or math answers!). Write on the paper balls first.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎯 Tossing into a small hole builds aim and arm control.
- 🎭 Giving the monster a personality = storytelling!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📄 Great way to use up scrap paper.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📄 Paper balls into recycling (feed the recycling bin!)
- [ ] 🎨 Craft stuff away
