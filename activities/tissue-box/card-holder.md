# 🗂️ Desk Tray Organizer

Cut a tissue box into a **desk tray** for cards, notes, and pencils. 🗂️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil + 📏 ruler
- [ ] 🎨 Paper, washi tape, or paint

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw the cut line 📏
Draw a slanted line on the sides — tall at the back, short at the front.

### 2️⃣ Cut ✂️
Cut along the lines. Now it's an open tray.

### 3️⃣ Dividers 🧩
Cut leftover cardboard into dividers. Tape them in.

### 4️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Cover with paper or tape.

### 5️⃣ Organize 🗂️
Cards, sticky notes, pencils!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Make the tray. Fill it.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Add 2 dividers for sections.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Label sections** 🏷️ With the label maker!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Measure fit** 📏 Measure what goes in first. Make each section exactly the right size.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📐 Measuring before cutting = fewer mistakes.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Tissue box cardboard is thin enough for kid scissors.

## 🧽 Cleanup
✂️ Scraps in recycling
