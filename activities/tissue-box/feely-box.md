# 🫳 Mystery Feely Box

Reach in… **don't peek!** 🙈 What's in the box? 🫳

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🧸 Mystery objects: a spoon, a sock, a toy car, a pinecone, a sponge, a LEGO
- [ ] 👫 2+ players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Secret load 🤫
One person hides an object in the box (no peeking!).

### 2️⃣ Reach in 🫳
The guesser puts a hand through the hole.

### 3️⃣ Describe it 🗣️
Bumpy? Soft? Cold? Heavy? Say 3 words before guessing.

### 4️⃣ Guess! 🤔
Right? You get a point. Switch!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Big, easy objects (a ball, a spoon).

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**3 clue words** 🗣️ Describe it before you guess.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Tricky pairs** 🧐 Coin vs. button. Fork vs. spoon. Can you tell them apart?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Shape match** 🔷 Put 2 of the same object in — one in the box, one in a pile. Find the match by touch.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🖐️ Your fingertips have thousands of touch sensors.
- 🧠 Your brain builds a picture from touch alone!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🚫 Nothing sharp, slimy, or alive (unless they agree 😄).

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧸 Objects back where they belong
