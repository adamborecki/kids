# ❓ Dinner Question Box

Pull a question 🎲 Everyone answers. Best dinner talk ever! 🍽️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 📝 Paper slips
- [ ] 🖊️ Pen
- [ ] 🎨 Markers to decorate

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Write questions 📝
One question per slip. Fold it. In the box!

### 2️⃣ At dinner 🍽️
Someone pulls a slip and reads it.

### 3️⃣ Everyone answers 🗣️
Go around the table. No wrong answers!

### 4️⃣ Add new ones 🔁
Anyone can add questions anytime.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Pull and read with help. Answer with a picture or words.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Read the question out loud yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Write 5 questions of your own.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Would you rather** 🤔 Add tricky choice questions.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Follow-up** 💬 After someone answers, ask one "why?" question.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Deep questions** 🧠 Add questions like "What's something you changed your mind about?"

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗣️ Good questions get people talking — and listening.
- 💛 You learn surprising things about your own family!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📵 Phones away at the table makes this work best.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📝 Put the slips back in
