# 🏀 Tissue Box Basketball

The box is the hoop. Paper balls are the basketball. **SWISH!** 🏀

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box (the hoop)
- [ ] 📄 Paper balls (crumple 5)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape (for lines)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Place the hoop 🤧
On the floor or a chair, hole facing up.

### 2️⃣ Make lines 🟨
1-point line (close), 2-point line (middle), 3-point line (far).

### 3️⃣ Shoot! 🏀
5 shots each. In the hole = points!

### 4️⃣ Add it up ➕
Who has the most?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Shoot from the close line. Count the baskets.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Use all 3 lines. Add your points.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Trick shots** 🌀 Over your shoulder, eyes closed, off the wall.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Percent** 🔢 Out of 10 shots, how many went in? That's your shooting average.

### 🔟 Age 10
**League** 🏆 3 rounds, a scoreboard, and a champion.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Stats** 📊 Track each player's makes from each line. Who should shoot 3-pointers?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏀 Arc your shot high — the ball drops into the hole more easily.
- 🔢 Shooting percentage = makes ÷ shots × 100.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📄 Paper balls are safe indoors. Clear breakables anyway.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📄 Paper balls into recycling
- [ ] 🟨 Peel tape lines
