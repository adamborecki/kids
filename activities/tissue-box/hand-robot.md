# 🤖 Hand Robot

Your hand goes in the box… and it comes alive as a **ROBOT!** 🤖 BEEP BOOP.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🥈 Foil or gray paper
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps, buttons (robot buttons!)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Robot skin 🥈
Wrap the box in foil. Leave the hole open.

### 2️⃣ Face 🤖
Bottle cap eyes. A mouth grid. Antennae from straws or pipe cleaners.

### 3️⃣ Control panel 🎛️
Glue buttons and caps on the side.

### 4️⃣ Activate! ⚡
Slide your hand in. Talk in a robot voice!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make the robot. Give it a name. BEEP!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Robot commands** 🎮 The robot only does what you say: "Walk 3 steps. Turn left."

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Robot rules** 📜 Write 3 rules your robot follows.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Code it** 💻 Write a list of commands (a program) to get the robot from the couch to the door. Test it with a friend as the robot!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🤖 Robots follow **exact** instructions — that's what coding is.
- 🐛 If the robot bumps into something, fix the program (that's "debugging").

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥈 Foil edges can be scratchy — fold them under.
