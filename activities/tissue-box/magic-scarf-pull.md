# 🪄 Magic Rainbow Pull

Pull… and pull… and PULL… the rainbow **never ends!** 🌈🪄

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] 🧣 Scarves, ribbons, or fabric strips
- [ ] 🎨 Paper to decorate a "magic" box
- [ ] 🪄 A pencil "wand"

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Tie them together 🔗
Knot the ends of the scarves into one long chain.

### 2️⃣ Stuff the box 🤧
Push it all in, with one end peeking out the top.

### 3️⃣ Practice the patter 🎤
"Ladies and gentlemen! An ordinary box…"

### 4️⃣ Abracadabra 🪄
Tap with your wand. Pull slowly… dramatically!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Pull the rainbow for your family. Take a bow! 🙇

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Magic words** 🪄 Make up your own spell. Add a second trick (a coin "disappears" into the box).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Full show** 🎩 3 tricks, a costume, and a name: "The Amazing ___!"

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎩 Magicians plan **everything** before the show — the audience only sees the surprise.
- 🗣️ Talking while you do a trick is called "patter."

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧣 Old t-shirts cut into strips work great.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧣 Re-stuff the box for the next show
