# 💪 Strong Tissue Test

Which is stronger — dry or wet? Tissue or paper towel? **Pile on the pennies!** 🪙

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 Tissues + paper towels (different brands if you have them)
- [ ] 🥤 A cup
- [ ] 🔗 A rubber band
- [ ] 🪙 Pennies (lots!)
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Stretch it 🥤
Stretch one tissue over the cup. Rubber band around the edge.

### 2️⃣ Predict 🤔
How many pennies before it breaks?

### 3️⃣ Add pennies one at a time 🪙
Gently, in the middle.

### 4️⃣ Record 📋
Count when it rips.

### 5️⃣ Repeat wet 💦
New tissue. 3 sprays of water. Test again!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Test dry tissue vs. wet tissue.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tissue vs. paper towel** 🧻 Which holds more?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fair test** ⚖️ Same cup, same spot, same number of sprays. Run each 3 times.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Bar graph** 📊 Graph your results. Bonus: why might paper towels be made to stay strong when wet? (Hint: what are they for?)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧻 Paper is made of tiny fibers holding hands.
- 💧 Water loosens their grip — so most wet paper gets weaker.
- 💪 Paper towels have extra stuff added so they stay strong when wet.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🪙 Pennies are small — keep away from toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🪙 Collect pennies
- [ ] 🧻 Toss soggy tissue
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe drips
