# 📬 Family Mailbox

Write a note ✉️ drop it in 📬 and wait for **mail day!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 1 tissue box per person (or one family box)
- [ ] 🎨 Paper + markers
- [ ] 📝 Note paper + envelopes (or folded paper)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make your mailbox 🎨
Decorate it. Put your name and an address: "123 Bedroom Lane." 🏠

### 2️⃣ Write a note ✉️
Kind words, a drawing, a joke.

### 3️⃣ Address it 📮
TO: and FROM: on the outside.

### 4️⃣ Deliver 🚶
Drop it in their box!

### 5️⃣ Mail day 📬
Open your mail at dinner.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw a picture for someone. A grown-up writes TO and FROM.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write a 1-sentence note.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Pen pals** ✍️ Write back and forth all week.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Stamps** 🎨 Design postage stamps on the printable. Every letter needs one!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Mail carrier** 🧢 Deliver everyone's mail at a set time. Sort by name first!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💌 Getting mail feels special — and writing is good practice.
- 📮 Real addresses go: name, street, city, then country.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💛 Make sure everyone gets at least one letter.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📬 Keep favorite notes in a shoebox
