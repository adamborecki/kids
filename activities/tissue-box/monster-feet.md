# 👣 Giant Monster Feet

Stick your feet in two tissue boxes… **STOMP STOMP!** 👣🦖

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 2 tissue boxes per player
- [ ] 🎨 Markers or paint
- [ ] 📄 Paper claws (triangles)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue or tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Scales, spots, fur. Glue claws on the front!

### 2️⃣ Step in 👣
Slide your feet in the holes.

### 3️⃣ Monster walk 🦖
Big stomps! Roar!

### 4️⃣ Race 🏁
Monster race across the room.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Monster walk around the house. ROAR! 🦖

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Monster race** 🏁 Who reaches the couch first?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Monster obstacle course** 🚧 Step over pillows, around chairs.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🦶 Walking in boxes = balance practice!
- 🎭 Pretend play builds imagination.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧦 Hard floors get slippery. Play on carpet or grass.
- 🚫 No stairs in monster feet!

## 🧽 Cleanup
👣 Store feet in the dress-up bin
