# 🌿 Habitat Diorama

Build a **tiny world** inside a box! 🌿🐸 Rainforest, ocean, desert, or arctic.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 A tissue box (cut the top open)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint, colored paper, clay
- [ ] 🌿 Natural stuff: twigs, moss, sand, stones
- [ ] 🐾 Small toy animals or paper animals
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a habitat 🌍
Rainforest 🌴 · Ocean 🌊 · Desert 🏜️ · Arctic ❄️

### 2️⃣ Background 🎨
Paint or paper the inside walls (sky, water, trees).

### 3️⃣ Ground 🏞️
Sand, moss, blue paper — whatever fits!

### 4️⃣ Animals 🐸
Add 3+ animals that really live there.

### 5️⃣ Label it 🏷️
Name the habitat + animals.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
3 animals + 3 plants that live in your habitat.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Food chain** 🍃➡️🐛➡️🐦 Show what eats what with arrows.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Adaptations** 🧠 For each animal, write one way its body helps it survive there.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Present it** 🎤 A 1-minute talk about your habitat, like a museum guide.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌍 A habitat is where an animal finds food, water, and shelter.
- 🐻‍❄️ Animals have special body features (adaptations) for their home.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🕐 This is a long project — split it over 2 days.
