# 🚂 Tissue Box Train

Choo choo! 🚂 Link boxes together into a train for your toys.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 3–5 tissue boxes (train cars)
- [ ] 🧶 String
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil (to poke holes)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🧸 Toy passengers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decorate cars 🎨
The first one is the engine — add a smokestack (a tube!).

### 2️⃣ Poke holes ✏️
A hole in the front and back of each box (grown-up helps).

### 3️⃣ Link them 🧶
Tie string between the cars.

### 4️⃣ All aboard! 🧸
Toys ride in the holes. Pull the train around!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
3 cars. Give every toy a ride! 🧸

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Number the cars** 🔢 Deliver toys to the right car number.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔗 Linked cars follow the engine — just like a real train.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✏️ Grown-up pokes the holes.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🚂 Park the train in the toy bin
