# 🎭 Mini Puppet Stage

A tiny theater for **finger puppets!** 🎭🎟️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🤧 An empty tissue box
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up helps on thick cardboard)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint, paper, fabric scraps (curtains!)
- [ ] 📄 Paper finger puppets (printable)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the stage window ✂️
Cut a big rectangle on one side. The tissue hole is where your fingers come up.

### 2️⃣ Curtains 🎭
Tape fabric or paper on both sides of the window.

### 3️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Paint it fancy: gold, red, stars!

### 4️⃣ Puppets 📄
Draw characters, cut them out, tape them into finger rings.

### 5️⃣ Show time! 🎬

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make 2 puppets and do a short show.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add a **background** you can swap (forest, castle, space).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Script** 📝 Write a 3-scene play.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fairy tale twist** 🔄 Retell a fairy tale where the villain is the hero.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Lights + sound** 🔦 Add a flashlight spotlight and sound effects. Run the show like a director.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎭 Small stages make shows easy — no costume changes!
- 📝 Scripts teach dialogue and story order.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Window cutting is easiest for a grown-up with a craft knife.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🎨 Craft supplies away
