# 🟥 Pixel Art Grid

Fill in squares to make a picture — like a **video game!** 🎮

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The pixel grid (printable) or graph paper

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick a picture 👀
Start with the ❤️ heart below. It's the easiest!

### 2️⃣ Find the top row ⬆️
Look at the first row of the pattern.

### 3️⃣ Color the matching squares 🖊️
Click the right color. Fill the square. **Scribble it full!**

### 4️⃣ Next row ⬇️
Go row by row. Use a finger to keep your place 👆

### 5️⃣ Step back… 🤩
It's a picture!

## 🌟 Level Up
### 🥉 Easy
Copy the ❤️ heart.

### 🥈 Medium
Copy the 🐟 fish. Then make it **2 colors** your way!

### 🥇 Hard
**Invent your own** 🎨
Design a pixel picture. Write the code for each row:
"Row 1: 3 white, 1 green, 3 white"
Give it to a friend. Can they build it from your code?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📺 Every screen is made of tiny squares called **pixels**.
- 🎮 Old video games had so few pixels you could count them!
- 📐 Rows and columns = how graphs and spreadsheets work too.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👆 A ruler or sticky note under the current row helps a lot.
- ⬜ "White" means leave it empty.
- 🟩 Ballpoint fills slowly — little kids can do an X or a dot instead of full coloring.
