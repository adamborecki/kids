# 🟥 Pixel Art Grid

Fill in squares to make a picture — like a **video game!** 🎮

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The pixel grid (printable) or graph paper

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick a picture 👀
Start with the ❤️ heart below. It's the easiest!

### 2️⃣ Find the top row ⬆️
Look at the first row of the pattern.

### 3️⃣ Color the matching squares 🖊️
Click the right color. Fill the square. **Scribble it full!**

### 4️⃣ Next row ⬇️
Go row by row. Use a finger to keep your place 👆

### 5️⃣ Step back… 🤩
It's a picture!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Copy the ❤️ heart.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Copy the 🌲 tree. Two colors!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Copy the 🐟 fish. Then recolor it your way.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Design your own** 🎨 on the 10 × 10 grid.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pixel code** 📝
Write your picture as row codes, like: "Row A: 3 white, 1 green, 3 white".
Give it to someone. Can they build it from the code alone?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Compression** 💾
Write rows short: **3W 1G 3W** instead of W W W G W W W.
Count how many characters you saved. Computers shrink images with this trick (it's called *run-length encoding*).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📺 Every screen is made of tiny squares called **pixels**.
- 🎮 Old video games had so few pixels you could count them!
- 📐 Rows and columns = how graphs and spreadsheets work too.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👆 A ruler or sticky note under the current row helps a lot.
- ⬜ "White" means leave it empty.
- 🟩 Ballpoint fills slowly — little kids can do an X or a dot instead of full coloring.

## 🧽 Cleanup
click the pen closed! 🖊️
