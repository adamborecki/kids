# 🧠 Memory Grid

Look for 10 seconds… 👀 then draw it **from memory!** 🧠

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The grids (printable)
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer
- [ ] 👫 A partner

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make a secret pattern 🤫
Player 1 colors some squares in a grid with different colors.

### 2️⃣ Look! 👀
Player 2 looks for **10 seconds**.

### 3️⃣ Hide it 🙈
Flip the paper over.

### 4️⃣ Draw it from memory 🧠
Player 2 copies the pattern on an empty grid.

### 5️⃣ Score ✅
1 point for every right square + right color.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
3×3 grid. 3 colored squares, one color.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
3×3 grid. 4 squares, 2 colors.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
4×4 grid. 5 squares, 3 colors.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
4×4 grid. 7 squares, all 4 colors.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Shorter look** ⏱️ Only 5 seconds!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Memory tricks** 🧠 Make up a trick to remember (like "red L-shape, blue corners"). Does it help? Test with and without.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Your **working memory** holds things for a few seconds.
- 🧩 Grouping things into patterns ("L-shape") helps you remember more — called **chunking**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔁 Keep turns short and switch often.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
