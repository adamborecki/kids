# ✍️ Rainbow Writing

Write a word… then trace it in **all 4 colors!** 🌈 The best way to learn spelling words.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The printable (or lined paper)
- [ ] 📝 Your spelling words

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Write it in BLACK ⚫
Neat letters. Say each letter out loud.

### 2️⃣ Trace in BLUE 🔵
Right on top of the black.

### 3️⃣ Trace in RED 🔴
On top again!

### 4️⃣ Trace in GREEN 🟢
Now it's a rainbow word 🌈

### 5️⃣ Cover + write 🙈
Hide it. Write it from memory. Check!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Rainbow-write your name and 3 short words (CAT, DOG, SUN).

### 7️⃣ Age 7
5 spelling words from school.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Color the tricky part** 🔴 Write the hard letters in red (fr**ie**nd, b**ea**utiful).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Self-test** 📝 Rainbow-write 10 words. Then a grown-up says them, you spell from memory. Score yourself!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔁 Tracing 4 times = 4 practice reps without getting bored.
- 🗣️ Saying letters while writing uses your ears, eyes, AND hand.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✍️ Slow and neat beats fast and messy.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
