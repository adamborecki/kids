# 🌀 Rainbow Ripple Doodles

Draw a shape. Trace around it. Click. Trace again. Click. 🔁
It **ripples** like a pebble in a pond! 🌊

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Paper or the printable

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Draw a small shape ⚫
A circle, a heart ❤️, a star ⭐, or your name!

### 2️⃣ Click BLUE 🔵 — trace around it
Stay close, like a hug. 🤗

### 3️⃣ Click RED 🔴 — trace around the blue

### 4️⃣ Click GREEN 🟢 — trace around the red

### 5️⃣ Click BLACK ⚫ — trace around the green

### 6️⃣ Repeat! 🔁
🔵 🔴 🟢 ⚫ 🔵 🔴 🟢 ⚫ … until the page is full!

### 💡 Fill-in patterns
Fill some ripples with:
- ••••• dots
- ||||| stripes
- /\/\/\ zigzags
- ~~~~~ waves
- ○○○○ bubbles

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Ripples around a **circle** ⭕

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Ripples around **your name** ✍️

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Fill each ripple with a **pattern**: dots, stripes, zigzags, waves.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Four-way mandala** 🏵️
Fold paper into 4. Draw the same pattern in each quarter.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Pattern codes** 🔢
Make a color rule (AB, AAB, ABCD). Can someone guess it and keep going?
- AB: 🔵🔴🔵🔴🔵🔴
- AAB: 🟢🟢⚫🟢🟢⚫
- ABCD: ⚫🔵🔴🟢⚫🔵🔴🟢

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Tessellation** 🧩
Make a shape that covers the page with **no gaps**, like M.C. Escher.
Start with a square, cut a bump from one side, tape it to the opposite side. Trace it over and over!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔢 Patterns are **math!** The part that repeats is called the **core**.
- 🧘 Slow tracing calms your brain down. Ahhh. 😌
- ✋ Staying close to a line builds pencil control for writing.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 😌 Great "quiet time" or waiting-room activity.
- 🎵 Calm music + doodles = instant chill zone.
- 🚫 No "wrong" — wobbly ripples look cool too.

## 🧽 Cleanup
click the pen closed! 🖊️
