# 📊 Family Survey + Graph

Ask everyone a question 🎤 … tally the answers … make a **bar graph!** 📊

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📋 The survey sheet + graph (printable)
- [ ] 👨‍👩‍👧 People to ask (family, friends, neighbors)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a question ❓
"Favorite pizza topping?" · "Cats or dogs?" · "Best ice cream?"

### 2️⃣ Pick 4 choices 🎨
One color per choice!

### 3️⃣ Ask everyone 🎤
Tally marks: IIII = 5

### 4️⃣ Draw the bars 📊
Color each bar in its color. Taller = more votes.

### 5️⃣ Report 📣
"Most people like…!"

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Ask 5 people. Count the tallies.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Ask 10 people. Draw the bar graph.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Title + labels** 🏷️ Add a title, and label both sides of the graph.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fraction of people** ➗ "3 out of 10 like pepperoni." Write each answer as a fraction.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Percent + pie** 🥧 Turn each answer into a percent. Bonus: sketch a pie chart.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📊 Graphs show answers **at a glance** — faster than reading a list.
- 🔢 Tally marks in groups of 5 are fast to count.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎤 Calling grandparents counts as survey work!

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
