# 🔲 4-Player Dots & Boxes

Connect the dots. **Close a box = it's yours!** 🔲
Up to 4 players — one color each. 🖊️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Dot grid (printable)
- [ ] 👫 2–4 players

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick colors 🎨
⚫ 🔵 🔴 🟢 — one each.

### 2️⃣ Draw ONE line 📏
Connect two dots that are next to each other.
⬆️⬇️ or ⬅️➡️ only. **No diagonals!** 🚫↗️

### 3️⃣ Pass the pen 🔁
Next player clicks their color.

### 4️⃣ Close a box? 🎉
Write your **first letter** in it.
Then you get **another turn**!

### 5️⃣ All boxes filled? 🔢
Count your boxes. **Most boxes wins!** 🏆

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Small grid, 2 players, a grown-up as your partner.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Small grid, 2 players. Count your boxes at the end.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Big grid**, 3–4 players 🎨

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Secret strategy** 🤫
Never draw the **3rd side** of a box — the next player will grab it!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Chains** ⛓️
At the end, boxes come in long chains. Sometimes it's smart to **give away 2 boxes** so you win the long chain. Try it!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Invent a variant** 🛠️
Triangles? 8 × 8? Boxes worth different points? Write the rules. Test with family.
🧮 Bonus: how many lines fit in the small grid? (Answer below the score table.)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔮 Thinking **one move ahead** is a strategy skill.
- 🔢 Counting boxes = adding.
- 🤝 Taking turns and losing nicely = big kid skills.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ⏱️ A 4 × 4 grid (9 boxes) takes ~5 minutes. Perfect for restaurants.
- 🎨 One pen for 4 players = natural turn-taking. Hand it over!
- 🤏 Little kids may need help spotting "almost boxes."

## 🧽 Cleanup
click the pen closed! 🖊️
