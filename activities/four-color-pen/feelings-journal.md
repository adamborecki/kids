# 📓 Four-Color Feelings Journal

One page a day. **Four colors, four questions.** 📓 A calm way to end the day. 🌙

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📓 A notebook (or the printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ ⚫ What happened today?
One or two sentences.

### 2️⃣ 🔵 How did I feel?
Happy, tired, frustrated, proud… pick a word.

### 3️⃣ 🔴 What was hard?
Something tricky. It's OK!

### 4️⃣ 🟢 What am I thankful for?
One good thing — even a tiny one.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
One word or a drawing for each color.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
One sentence for each color.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Feelings words** 🎨 Use a new feeling word each day (not just "good"/"bad").

### 🔟 Age 10
**Look back** 🔍 After a week, read it all. Any patterns?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Solve it** 🔴➡️🟢 For the hard thing, add one idea for what you could try next time.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Naming a feeling makes it feel smaller. Scientists call it **"name it to tame it."**
- 🟢 Noticing good things every day trains your brain to spot them.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤐 It's private. Only read it if they invite you.

🖊️ Click the pen closed!
