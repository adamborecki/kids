# 🕵️ Proofreading Detective

This paragraph has **10 mistakes**. Grab the red pen. 🔴🕵️ Can you find them all?

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen (🔴 red for fixes!)
- [ ] 📋 The mystery paragraph (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Read it once 📖
Just read. Don't fix yet.

### 2️⃣ Read it slowly 🐢
Finger under each word.

### 3️⃣ Circle mistakes in RED 🔴
Spelling, capitals, periods, wrong words.

### 4️⃣ Fix them 🔵
Write the right version above in blue.

### 5️⃣ Check the answers ✅
How many did you find?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Find 5 of the 10 mistakes.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Find all 10!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Editor marks** ✏️ Use real marks: ≡ under a letter = capital, ^ = insert, / = lowercase.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Make a trap** 🪤 Write your own paragraph with 10 hidden mistakes for a grown-up to find.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Your brain auto-corrects when you read fast — that's why mistakes hide!
- 🐢 Slow reading + a finger = you see what's **really** there.
- 📰 Real editors do this for every book and newspaper.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔴 Red pen = fixing, not "wrong." Make it a detective game.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
