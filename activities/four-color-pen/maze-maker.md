# 🌀 Maze Maker

Design a maze 🌀 … then trap your friends in it! 😈

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The grid (printable) or graph paper
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil + eraser

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Start + finish 🏁
Mark START 🟢 and FINISH 🔴 on opposite sides.

### 2️⃣ Draw the real path ✏️
In pencil, draw ONE path from start to finish.

### 3️⃣ Add dead ends 🚫
Lots of branches that go nowhere!

### 4️⃣ Ink the walls ⚫
Trace walls in black. Erase the pencil path.

### 5️⃣ Swap + solve 🔄
Solve in blue. Did they make it?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Simple maze with 3 dead ends.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Use the grid. 6+ dead ends.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Theme** 🐭 A mouse finding cheese, a rocket finding home.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Bridges** 🌉 Paths can go over/under each other.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Timed solve** ⏱️ Whose maze takes longest to solve?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Circle maze** ⭕ Make a round maze (rings + gaps), like a labyrinth.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Mazes train **planning** — looking ahead before moving.
- 🤚 Trick: put your hand on one wall and follow it — you'll solve many simple mazes!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✏️ Pencil first saves frustration.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
