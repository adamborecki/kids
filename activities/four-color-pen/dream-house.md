# 🏠 Dream House Floor Plan

Design your **dream house** like a real architect! 🏠📐 Slide? Secret room? You decide.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The grid (printable) or graph paper
- [ ] 📏 Ruler
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make a wish list 📝
Rooms you want: bedroom, kitchen, game room, secret library…

### 2️⃣ Choose the scale 📏
1 square = 1 meter (or 3 feet).

### 3️⃣ Draw walls ⚫
Outside walls first, then rooms. Pencil first, then black pen.

### 4️⃣ Doors + windows 🔵
Gaps for doors. Blue lines for windows.

### 5️⃣ Furniture 🟢 + labels 🔴
Beds, tables in green. Room names in red.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
A house with 4–6 rooms. Label every room.

### 🔟 Age 10
**To scale** 📏 Use 1 square = 1 meter. Measure your real bedroom to compare!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Area + budget** 💰 Calculate each room's area. Total it. If building costs $200 per square meter (example price), what would it cost?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📐 Architects draw "from above" views called **floor plans**.
- 📏 A **scale** keeps everything the right size compared to everything else.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🏠 Walk through your real home first and sketch its plan together — great warm-up.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
