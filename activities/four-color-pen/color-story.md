# 📚 Four-Color Story

Every color is a **character**. 🎭
Take turns. Click your color. Add one sentence. Build a crazy story together! 🤪

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The Story Sheet (printable) or paper
- [ ] 👫 1–3 friends or family (or play solo!)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick characters 🎭
- ⚫ **Black** = the Narrator 📖
- 🔵 **Blue** = the Hero 🦸
- 🔴 **Red** = the Trouble 😈
- 🟢 **Green** = the Helper 🐸

### 2️⃣ Start ⚫
Narrator writes: "Once upon a time…"

### 3️⃣ Take turns 🔁
**Click** your color. Write **one** sentence. Pass the pen!

### 4️⃣ Keep going ➡️
About 12 sentences. Things should get **wild** 🌪️

### 5️⃣ Find the ending 🏁
How does the Hero fix the Trouble?

### 6️⃣ Read it out loud 🎤
Use a different **voice** for each color!

### 💡 Story starters
- 🐉 "A tiny dragon was afraid of fire…"
- 🍕 "The pizza in the fridge started talking…"
- 🚀 "Our couch blasted off into space…"
- 🐶 "The dog found a secret door in the backyard…"

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
**Tell** the story out loud. A grown-up writes it in the colors. 6 sentences.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write your **own** sentences. 8 total.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
12 sentences. Read it aloud with a **different voice** for each color 🎭

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Story rules** 🎲 Must include: 💥 a sound word, 😢 a feeling, 🔄 a twist.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Talking only** 💬
Characters can **only speak** — no narrator. Use real quotation marks: "Like this!"

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Plan + edit** ✂️
Plan the shape first: beginning → rising problem → big moment → ending.
After writing, become the editor: click **red** and fix 3 things.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Colors show **who said what** — like speech bubbles in comics.
- 🧱 Stories have a shape: **beginning → problem → fix**.
- 🤝 Building on someone else's idea = teamwork!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 😄 Silly is good. Let it get weird.
- ⏱️ Little kids: stop at 6 sentences.
- 📸 Snap a photo of good ones — they're fun to reread later.

## 🧽 Cleanup
click the pen closed! 🖊️
