# 🦸 Four-Panel Comic

A whole story in just **4 boxes!** 📗
Box 1 → Box 2 → Box 3 → Box 4 = 😂

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 The comic page (printable)
- [ ] 💡 One silly idea

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ The plan 📝
| Box | Job | Example 🐱 |
|---|---|---|
| 1️⃣ | **Meet** someone | A cat sees a fish bowl |
| 2️⃣ | **Problem** | The fish is too far away |
| 3️⃣ | **Try** something | The cat builds a tower of books |
| 4️⃣ | **Surprise!** | The fish is eating popcorn, watching the cat 🍿 |

### 2️⃣ ⚫ BLACK — draw it
Draw the people, places, and outlines.

### 3️⃣ 🔵 BLUE — speech bubbles 💬
What do they say? Keep it short!

### 4️⃣ 🔴 RED — action! 💥
Sound words: **BOOM! SPLAT! ZOOM!**
Motion lines to show fast moving ≋

### 5️⃣ 🟢 GREEN — the background 🌳
Grass, trees, walls — fill in the world.

### 6️⃣ Title + your name ✍️
Every comic artist signs their work!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw the 4 boxes. Tell the words out loud 🗣️

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add **speech bubbles** 💬

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add **sound words** 💥 and **motion lines** ≋

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**No words at all!** 🤐 Can someone understand it from pictures only?

### 🔟 Age 10
**True story** 📰 A funny thing that really happened this week.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**3-page comic** 📚 12 panels and a cliffhanger.
Mix your "camera shots": close-up 👁️, wide shot 🏞️, from above 🦅.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📗 Newspaper comics have used 4 boxes for 100 years. It's the perfect joke shape!
- 🎬 Planning boxes = a **storyboard**. Movie makers do this too.
- 💬 Short speech bubbles = choosing the best words.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✏️ Pen means no erasing. Tell kids: "Mistakes become part of the art."
- 💡 Stuck? Use a pet, a food, or a family member as the main character.
- 📸 Hang finished comics on the fridge — instant motivation.

## 🧽 Cleanup
click the pen closed! 🖊️
