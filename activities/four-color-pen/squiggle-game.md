# 〰️ The Squiggle Game

I draw a squiggle. You turn it into **something!** 〰️ → 🐉

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Paper (the printable has boxes)
- [ ] 👫 2+ players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Squiggle ⚫
Player 1 draws a random squiggle in black. Any shape!

### 2️⃣ Turn it 🔄
Player 2 turns the paper any way they like.

### 3️⃣ Make it something 🔵🔴🟢
Use the other colors to turn the squiggle into a picture.

### 4️⃣ Name it 📛
Give it a title!

### 5️⃣ Switch 🔁
Now you squiggle for them.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Big simple squiggles. Anything counts!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add a title to every picture.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Speed round** ⏱️ 30 seconds per squiggle!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Story chain** 📖 4 squiggles in a row become a 4-panel story.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Same squiggle, different picture** 🔁 Both players get the SAME squiggle. Compare!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Hard mode** 🎯 Squiggles must become a specific category: animals, machines, or food.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Illustrator Anthony Browne made "The Shape Game" famous.
- 🧠 Seeing pictures in random shapes is called **pareidolia** (like faces in clouds ☁️).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🚫 No "that's wrong" — every idea counts.
- 🍽️ Perfect restaurant game while waiting for food.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
