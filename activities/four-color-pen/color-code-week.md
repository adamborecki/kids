# 🗓️ Color-Code My Week

Plan your week like a pro. Each color has a **job**. 🗓️🖊️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📋 The week planner (printable)
- [ ] 📅 Your schedule (school, sports, plans)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Learn the code 🎨
⚫ School · 🔵 Activities & sports · 🔴 IMPORTANT / due · 🟢 Fun & free time

### 2️⃣ Fill in the fixed stuff ⚫
School hours, practice times.

### 3️⃣ Add deadlines 🔴
Tests, projects, library books due.

### 4️⃣ Add fun 🟢
Playdates, shows, free time.

### 5️⃣ Check it daily ✅
Morning: look at today. Night: look at tomorrow.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Fill in one week with help.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Plan homework** 📚 Break a big project into small steps on different days.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Time budget** ⏱️ Count hours of each color. Too much red? Too little green? Adjust!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎨 Colors let your eyes find things fast.
- 🔴 Seeing deadlines early = no last-minute panic.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤝 Do the first one together. Many adults use this exact system.
