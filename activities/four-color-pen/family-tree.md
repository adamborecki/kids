# 🌳 Family Tree

Who's in your family? 🌳 Map it — **one color per generation!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Big paper (or the printable)
- [ ] ☎️ A grown-up to ask
- [ ] 📸 Family photos (optional)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ You in the middle 🟢
Write your name (and siblings) at the bottom in green.

### 2️⃣ Parents 🔴
Above you, in red. Draw lines down to you.

### 3️⃣ Grandparents 🔵
Above your parents, in blue.

### 4️⃣ Great-grandparents ⚫
Top row, black. (Ask a grown-up!)

### 5️⃣ Add a fact for each 💡
Birthday, where they were born, a fun fact.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
You, parents, grandparents. Names only.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add a fact for each person.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Interview** 🎤 Call a grandparent. Ask 3 questions about when they were your age.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Map it** 🗺️ Mark where each person was born on a map.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Timeline** 📅 Line up everyone's birth years. What was happening in the world each year?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌳 It's called a "tree" because it branches out with each generation.
- 🎤 Family stories disappear if nobody writes them down.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💛 Every family looks different (step, adopted, chosen family). Include everyone who matters.
- 🕊️ Some names may bring up big feelings — that's OK.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
