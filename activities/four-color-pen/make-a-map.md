# 🗺️ Make-a-Map

One pen. Four colors. A **whole world.** 🌍

| Color | Means |
|---|---|
| 🔵 Blue | Water — rivers, lakes, ocean 🌊 |
| 🟢 Green | Land — trees, grass, hills 🌳 |
| 🔴 Red | Roads, paths, and **X marks the spot** ❌ |
| ⚫ Black | Outlines, names, compass 🧭 |

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Paper or the map grid (printable)
- [ ] 🪙 A coin to trace circles (if you want)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick a place 🤔
🛏️ Your room · 🏡 Your street · 🏝️ A made-up island

### 2️⃣ ⚫ Click BLACK — draw the outline
The edges of your room, or the shape of your island.

### 3️⃣ 🔵 Click BLUE — add water
A lake, a river, a whole ocean! 🌊

### 4️⃣ 🟢 Click GREEN — add land stuff
Trees 🌳 Grass Hills ⛰️ A forest!

### 5️⃣ 🔴 Click RED — roads and treasure
Paths, bridges… and an **X** ❌ for treasure!

### 6️⃣ ⚫ Back to BLACK — names
Name everything! "Shark Bay" 🦈 "Snack Mountain" 🍪
Add a compass: **N E S W** 🧭

### 7️⃣ Make a key 🔑
A little box that tells what each color means.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Map **your room from above** — like a bird sees it 🐦 Use all 4 colors.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Treasure island** 🏝️ 5 named places + an X ❌

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add a **compass rose** 🧭 and a **map key** 🔑

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Real treasure map** 🏴‍☠️
Map one room. Hide a toy. Mark it with ❌. Can someone follow your map to it?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Map to scale** 📏
Measure the room in feet (or big steps). On the grid, 1 square = 1 foot.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Memory vs. reality** 🌍
Draw your neighborhood from memory. Then compare with a real online map.
What did you get wrong? Add grid coordinates (A–J, 1–10) and write directions using them.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌍 Almost every map in the world uses **blue for water**. Colors are a code everyone knows!
- 🐦 Looking from above is called **bird's-eye view**.
- 🧭 Remember N E S W clockwise: **N**ever **E**at **S**oggy **W**affles 🧇

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🗺️ Show a real map (paper or phone) first — kids love spotting the color code.
- 🧭 Point out which way north is in your home.
- 🧩 Graph paper makes the Age 10 scale map much easier.

click the pen closed! 🖊️
