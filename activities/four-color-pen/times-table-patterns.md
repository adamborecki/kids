# ✖️ Times Table Patterns

Color the multiples on a 100 chart… and **secret patterns** appear! ✖️🌈

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📋 The 100 chart (printable — print a few!)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a number 🔢
Start with 2.

### 2️⃣ Color its multiples 🔵
2, 4, 6, 8… color every one in blue.

### 3️⃣ Look! 👀
Stripes! 🦓

### 4️⃣ Try another chart 🔁
Multiples of 3, 5, 9, 10 — each on a fresh chart.

### 5️⃣ Compare 🤔
Which makes columns? Which makes diagonals?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Multiples of 2, 5, and 10.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Multiples of 3, 4, and 9. Which make diagonals?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Two at once** 🎨 Color multiples of 2 in blue and 3 in red on the SAME chart. What's special about squares with both? (They're multiples of 6!)

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Primes** ⭐ Cross out multiples of 2, 3, 5, 7 (not the numbers themselves). The un-crossed numbers left (except 1) are **prime numbers**! This is the **Sieve of Eratosthenes** (2,200 years old).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔁 Times tables are **repeating patterns** — counting by the same jump.
- 9️⃣ Multiples of 9 make a diagonal because each one is "1 more ten, 1 less one."
