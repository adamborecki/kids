# 🔢 Make a Color-by-Number

Don't just color one — **make one** for someone else! 🎨🔢

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📄 Paper or the printable grid
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw a picture in pencil ✏️
Simple shapes: a fish, a house, a rocket 🚀

### 2️⃣ Split it into spaces 🧩
Add lines to make areas.

### 3️⃣ Number each space 🔢
1 = ⚫ black · 2 = 🔵 blue · 3 = 🔴 red · 4 = 🟢 green

### 4️⃣ Make the key 🔑
Write the color key at the bottom.

### 5️⃣ Give it away 🎁
A friend colors it. Does it match your idea?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Big simple shapes (a sun, a ball). Grown-up helps with numbers.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
A picture with 8–10 spaces.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Use **all 4** numbers. Make a copy to keep.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Grid version** 🟦 Number squares on the grid to make a pixel picture.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Math version** ➕ Instead of numbers, write problems: 2+2 = 4 = green!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧩 Designing it means planning **before** coloring — that's how designers work.
- 🔑 A "key" is a code everyone can use.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✏️ Pencil first, then numbers in pen.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
