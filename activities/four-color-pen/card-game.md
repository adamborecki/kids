# 🃏 Make Your Own Card Game

Four colors = **four suits!** 🃏 Make a whole deck, then play "Color Match."

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📇 Index cards (40), cut in half if you like
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 👫 2–4 players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the deck 🃏
For each color (⚫🔵🔴🟢), write numbers **1–9** = 36 cards.

### 2️⃣ Add specials ⭐
4 cards: SKIP ⏭️ · REVERSE 🔄 · DRAW 2 ➕ · WILD 🌈

### 3️⃣ Deal 7 each 🤲
Put one card face-up. The rest is the draw pile.

### 4️⃣ Match! 🎯
Play a card that matches the **color** OR the **number**. Can't? Draw one.

### 5️⃣ First to empty their hand wins 🏆
Say "LAST CARD!" when you have one left.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Play with numbers only (no specials).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add the special cards.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Design a new special** ⭐ Invent one: "SWAP HANDS," "EVERYONE DRAWS 1"…

### 🔟 Age 10
**Write the rulebook** 📖 Clear rules a new player could follow without asking you.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Balance test** ⚖️ Is your special card too strong? Play 5 games and track who wins. Adjust the rules.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🃏 Four suits is how real decks work (♠️ ♥️ ♦️ ♣️).
- 🎮 Game designers playtest and change rules over and over.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🃏 Store the deck in an envelope or a rubber band.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🃏 Deck in an envelope
- [ ] 🖊️ Click the pen closed!
