# 🎞️ Sticky Note Flip Book

Draw a tiny movie! 🎬 Flip the pages and it **MOVES.**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 🟨 A pad of sticky notes (20+ sheets)
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Plan a simple action 💡
A ball bouncing ⚽ · A sun rising 🌅 · A stick person jumping 🦘

### 2️⃣ Start at the BACK 🔙
Draw the first picture on the **last** page.

### 3️⃣ Trace + change a little ✏️
Flip the next page down. Trace the last drawing, but move it a tiny bit.

### 4️⃣ Keep going 🔁
20 pages = about 2 seconds of movie!

### 5️⃣ FLIP! 🎬
Hold the top, flick the bottom with your thumb.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
A ball bouncing — 10 pages.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
20 pages. Add color in one part.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Smooth motion** 🐢 Move things in smaller steps. Does it look smoother?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Mini story** 🎬 30+ pages: a beginning, an action, a surprise ending.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎬 Your eyes hold each picture for a split second. Fast flipping blends them into **motion**.
- 🎥 Movies and cartoons work the same way: 24 pictures per second!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🟨 Thin sticky notes let you see the drawing underneath — easier tracing.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
