# ✋ 3D Hand Illusion

Trace your hand… draw some lines… and it **pops off the page!** ✋🤯

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil
- [ ] 📄 Paper
- [ ] 📏 Ruler

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Trace your hand ✏️
Light pencil. Fingers spread.

### 2️⃣ Draw straight lines 📏
Across the paper, every finger-width apart. **Stop** at the hand outline.

### 3️⃣ Arch over the hand 🌈
Inside the hand, connect each line with a curved **hump**.

### 4️⃣ Color the lines 🎨
Rotate colors: ⚫🔵🔴🟢⚫🔵🔴🟢

### 5️⃣ Erase the pencil ✨
It looks 3D!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Hand illusion with one color.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
All 4 colors in a repeating pattern.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Try other shapes** 🍎 An apple, a heart, your initial.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Shading** 🌑 Shade one side of each hump darker. Even more 3D!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Why it works** 🧠 Explain how curved lines trick your brain into seeing depth. Then design your own illusion.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Your brain expects lines to curve over **bumps**. Curves = 3D!
- 🎨 Artists call this using **contour lines**. Maps use them to show hills.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📏 Lines closer together = smoother look.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
