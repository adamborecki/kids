# 🐛 Nature Sketchbook

Sit outside. Look **really** closely. Draw what you see. 🍃🐛

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen
- [ ] 📓 The printable or a notebook
- [ ] 🪑 Something to sit on
- [ ] 🔍 Magnifier (optional)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Find a spot 🌳
Backyard, park, even a window.

### 2️⃣ Pick ONE thing 🍃
A leaf, a bug, a flower, a cloud.

### 3️⃣ Look for 1 minute 👀
Shape? Lines? Colors? Tiny details?

### 4️⃣ Draw it big ✏️
Black outline. Add color details.

### 5️⃣ Add labels 🏷️
Arrows + words: "fuzzy," "6 legs," "jagged edges."

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw one leaf. Count its points.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Draw it + write 2 words about it.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**I notice / I wonder / It reminds me of** 🤔 Write one of each.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Zoom in** 🔍 Draw the whole thing small, then one part BIG with the magnifier.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Same spot, different day** 📅 Draw the same tree 3 times this month. What changed?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Field guide page** 📖 Draw it, measure it, look up its name, write 3 facts.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👀 Drawing makes you notice things you'd never see in a quick look.
- 🔬 Scientists like Charles Darwin kept nature sketchbooks.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🐝 Look, don't touch bugs.
- ☀️ Hat + water on sunny days.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🖊️ Click the pen closed!
