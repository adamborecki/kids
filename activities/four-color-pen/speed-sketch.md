# 📢 Speed Sketch Showdown

Draw it FAST. Team shouts guesses. **LOUD!** 📢✏️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖊️ 4-color pen (one per team, or share)
- [ ] 📄 Lots of paper
- [ ] 📋 Word cards (printable — cut them out)
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer (60 seconds)
- [ ] 👫 2 teams

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut out word cards ✂️
Fold them. Put them in a bowl.

### 2️⃣ Draw a card 🎴
The drawer peeks. Shh! 🤫

### 3️⃣ 60 seconds! ⏱️
Draw — no letters, no numbers, no talking.

### 4️⃣ Team guesses 📢
Shout as many guesses as you want!

### 5️⃣ Score 🏆
Correct = 1 point. Switch teams.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Easy words: cat, sun, house, fish. Grown-up reads the card.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Medium words: pizza, rainbow, robot.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Actions** 🏃 swimming, sleeping, sneezing.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**30 seconds only** ⏱️

### 🔟 Age 10
**Hard words** 🧠 volcano, birthday, homework.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Ideas** 💡 Draw ideas like "freedom," "teamwork," "Monday." Hardest of all!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ✏️ Quick drawings force you to pick the **most important** shape.
- 🧠 Guessing = your brain matching shapes to ideas super fast.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📢 It gets LOUD. Great for family game night, not for a sleeping baby.
- 🤝 Mix ages on each team.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📄 Recycle drawings (keep the funny ones!)
- [ ] 🖊️ Click the pen closed!
