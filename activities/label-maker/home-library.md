# 📚 Home Library

Turn your bookshelf into a **real library** — sections, labels, and checkout cards! 📚🏷️

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Section labels on masking tape. The library's **name sign** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📚 All your books
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 📇 Index cards (for checkout cards)
- [ ] 📋 The printable

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Books OUT 📦
Pile them up. (Yes, it gets messy!)

### 2️⃣ Sort into sections 🗂️
Picture books · Chapter books · Facts · Comics · Favorites

### 3️⃣ Label the shelves 🟨
One label per section.

### 4️⃣ Shelve them ✅
Spines facing out. Tall books together.

### 5️⃣ Make checkout cards 📇
Who borrowed it? When is it due back?

### 6️⃣ Name your library ✨
Plan it, check spelling, print **once** on silver!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Sort into 3 sections. Label them.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add a **checkout card** system. Lend a book to a family member!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Fiction A–Z** 🔤 Put story books in order by the author's last name — like a real library.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Librarian** 📋 Make a catalog list of every book: title, author, section.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Dewey Decimal** 🔢 Label nonfiction with real library numbers: 500 = science, 600 = technology, 700 = arts & sports, 900 = history & geography.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏛️ Libraries sort books so anyone can find one fast.
- 🔤 Alphabetical order by author is how most libraries shelve stories.
- 🔢 Melvil Dewey invented his number system in 1876. Libraries still use it!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📚 It WILL be a big pile for a while. Plan an hour.
- 🟨 Masking tape on shelves, not on book covers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📚 Every book shelved
- [ ] 📦 Donate books nobody reads anymore (ask first!)
