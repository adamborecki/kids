# 🏪 Pretend Store

Open your own shop! 🏪 Price tags, a sign, a register — and **real math**.

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Price tags on masking tape. The **store name** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥫 Things to "sell" (cans, toys, books)
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (price tags)
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 💵 Play money (or the printable)
- [ ] 🛒 A bag or basket

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Set up the shop 🏪
Line up items on a table.

### 2️⃣ Price tags 🏷️
Write a price on each one: $1, $3, $5…

### 3️⃣ Name the store ✨
Plan it, then print once on silver!

### 4️⃣ Open! 🔔
One person shops. One runs the register.

### 5️⃣ Make change 💵
Customer pays with $10 → how much back?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
All prices are $1. Count the items = the total!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Prices $1–$5. Add up 2 items.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Make change** from $10 💵

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Sale day** 🏷️ Half price on everything! What's the new price?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Cents** 🪙 Prices like $2.50 and $1.75. Add with decimals.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ➕ Shopping = adding. Change = subtracting.
- 💵 Practicing with play money makes real money less confusing.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥫 Use real grocery items for realism — then put them back!
- 💵 Printable money works fine.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥫 Everything back in the pantry
- [ ] 🟨 Peel price tags
