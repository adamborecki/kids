# 🍳 Recipe Card Chef

Write your own recipe card ✍️ … then make it! 🥣 No stove needed.

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Ingredient labels on masking tape. Your **signature recipe name** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📇 Index cards or the printable
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 🥣 Bowl + spoon + measuring cups
- [ ] 🥜 No-cook ingredients (see ideas)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a no-cook recipe 🥣
Trail mix · Yogurt parfait · Fruit kebabs · Ants on a log 🐜

### 2️⃣ Write the card ✍️
Name · Ingredients · Steps (1, 2, 3)

### 3️⃣ Label ingredients 🟨
Set them out with labels: GRANOLA, BERRIES…

### 4️⃣ Make it 🥄
Follow your own card exactly!

### 5️⃣ Rate it ⭐
Taste test! Change anything next time?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Fruit kebabs 🍓🍌 — draw the recipe card.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write the card yourself. 3 steps.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Measure** 🥄 Use cups and spoons, not guesses.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Double it** ✖️ Rewrite the recipe for twice as many people.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Recipe book** 📚 Make 5 cards. Add a cover. Give it as a gift.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Cost it** 💰 Look up ingredient prices. How much does one serving cost?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📋 Recipes are **instructions** — they have to be clear and in order.
- ✖️ Doubling a recipe = multiplying fractions (½ cup × 2 = 1 cup).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥜 Check allergies (nuts in trail mix!).
- 🔪 Grown-ups cut fruit for younger kids; older kids with a butter knife.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥣 Dishes in the sink (or dishwasher)
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the counter
- [ ] 🫙 Leftovers labeled in the fridge
