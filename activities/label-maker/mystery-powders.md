# 🧪 Mystery Powder Lab

4 white powders. They all look the same… 🤔 Label them **A, B, C, D** and find out which is which! 🧪

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Test labels on masking tape. When you solve it, the **winning detective** gets a Magic Silver badge ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧂 Salt · 🍬 Sugar · 🧁 Baking soda · 🌽 Cornstarch (a spoonful each)
- [ ] 🥣 4 small cups or a muffin tin
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (labels A B C D)
- [ ] 💧 Water · 🍶 Vinegar
- [ ] 🥄 Spoons · 🔍 Magnifier

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Secret setup 🤫
A grown-up puts each powder in a cup labeled A, B, C, D (and writes down the answers secretly!).

### 2️⃣ LOOK 🔍
Use the magnifier. Cubes? Sparkly? Super fine?

### 3️⃣ WATER test 💧
Add a spoonful of water to a pinch of each. Does it disappear (dissolve)? Turn goopy?

### 4️⃣ VINEGAR test 🍶
Add vinegar to a fresh pinch. FIZZ? 🫧

### 5️⃣ Solve it 🕵️
Use the clue chart to name each powder.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Do the vinegar test. Find the FIZZ one!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Do all 3 tests. Fill in the chart.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Solve all 4** 🕵️ Use the clue chart. Check with the grown-up's secret answers.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Make a key** 🔑 Write a flowchart: "Does it fizz? → Yes = baking soda. No → …"

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Mystery mix** 🧩 A grown-up mixes 2 powders. Can your tests figure out which 2?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🫧 Baking soda + vinegar make **carbon dioxide gas** — the fizz!
- 💧 Salt and sugar **dissolve** in water. Cornstarch doesn't — it gets goopy.
- 🔍 Salt crystals are little **cubes**. Sugar crystals are not.
- 🧪 Real scientists identify things with tests like these.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👅 Lab rule: **never taste** unknown powders (even if you know they're kitchen stuff — it's good practice).
- 🥽 Keep vinegar away from eyes.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥣 Rinse cups in the sink
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the table (cornstarch goo is sneaky)
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
