# ⏳ Time Capsule

Pack a box for **future you** to open in **one year!** ⏳📦

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Item labels on paper. The **DO NOT OPEN UNTIL** label gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A box or big jar with a lid
- [ ] 📋 The "All About Me Now" sheet (printable)
- [ ] 📸 A photo or drawing of you
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 🟨 Tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fill in "All About Me" 📋
Favorite food, best friend, height, what you want to be…

### 2️⃣ Pick 3–5 items 🧸
Small things that show who you are NOW: a drawing, a toy, a ticket.

### 3️⃣ Label each item 🟨
"This is my… because…"

### 4️⃣ Seal it 📦
Tape it shut. Print the silver label: **OPEN ON [DATE]** ✨

### 5️⃣ Hide it 🫣
Top of a closet. Put a reminder on the calendar!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Fill in the sheet with help. Add a drawing.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Measure your height and trace your hand 🖐️ — compare next year!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Predictions** 🔮 Write 3 things you think will happen this year.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Letter to future me** ✉️ Write a full letter: what's hard, what's great, what you hope.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Family capsule** 👨‍👩‍👧 Interview each family member (3 questions each) and add their answers.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⏳ You change a LOT in a year — but you don't notice day to day.
- 🔮 Predictions are fun to check later!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📅 Set a phone/calendar reminder for opening day.
- 🍫 No food in the capsule (bugs!).

## 🧽 Cleanup
📦 Capsule hidden safely
