# 🏆 The Silver Tape Award

The **fanciest** award in the whole house. 👑
Only **ONE** winner. Only **ONE** silver label. ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker with the ✨ Magic Silver Tape
- [ ] 📝 Nomination slips (printable)
- [ ] 🖍️ Crayons or pencils
- [ ] 🥁 A pot + a spoon (drum roll!)
- [ ] 🛋️ A "stage" (the rug works)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Nominations 📝
Everyone fills out a slip:
**Who** should win? **Why?**
It can be a person 👧, a pet 🐶, even a toy 🧸!

### 2️⃣ Plan the words ✏️
Write the award name on paper first.
Keep it short — **12 letters or less** fits best.

### 3️⃣ Choose the winner 🗳️
Vote, or the grown-up picks from the slips.

### 4️⃣ Drum roll… 🥁🥁🥁
Bang the pot. Make it dramatic!

### 5️⃣ Print ONE label ✨
Check spelling. Then — **click!** — print.

### 6️⃣ Present it 🏆
"And the Silver Tape Award goes to…"
The winner sticks it on a cup, a card, or their door.

### 7️⃣ SPEECH! 🎤
The winner says thank you. Bow! 🙇

### 💡 Award ideas
- 🤝 BEST HELPER
- 💖 KIND HEART
- 🦁 BRAVE TRY
- 🧹 TIDY CHAMP
- 😂 FUNNY BONE
- 🎨 TOP ARTIST
- 🐶 GOOD DOG

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
**Draw** your nomination. Hand over the award! 🏆

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write **who** + **one reason** on the slip.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Write **3 reasons**. Do the drum roll 🥁

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Be the host** 🎤
Welcome everyone, read the nominees, open the envelope ✉️, announce the winner.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Award committee** 📋
Write the rules: what does it take to win? Keep the Award Log.
Make sure it rotates fairly ⚖️

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Write the speech** ✍️
5–6 sentences: one **specific** moment, what it showed about them, a thank-you.
Plan the label to fit **12 characters** — spaces count!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👀 You practice **noticing** good things people do.
- 💎 Only ONE silver label = it's **special**. Scarce things feel valuable!
- 🎤 Speeches build brave voices.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ⚖️ Track winners (log below) so it rotates — everyone wins over time.
- 💖 Reward **effort and kindness**, not just results.
- 📅 Once a week or once a month keeps it special — and saves tape.
