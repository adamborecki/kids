# 🧸 Toy Bin Makeover

Every toy gets a **home**. Every home gets a **label**. 🧸🏷️ Cleanup gets SO much faster.

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Bin labels on masking tape (with a picture!). The **favorite bin** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧺 Bins, baskets, or boxes
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 🧸 All the toys (gulp)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Dump + sort 🗂️
Make piles: blocks, cars, dolls, art, games, plushies.

### 2️⃣ One pile = one bin 🧺
Too many? Donate or store some.

### 3️⃣ Label it 🏷️
Word + a tiny drawing (so little kids can "read" it).

### 4️⃣ Put it all away ✅
Time it — then time the next cleanup!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Sort into 3 bins. Draw the pictures for the labels 🖍️

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write the words + pictures yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Speed cleanup** ⏱️ Time cleanup before and after labeling. How much faster?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Little-kid labels** 🧒 Make labels a 3-year-old could use (pictures only!).

### 🔟 Age 10
**Donate box** 📦 Pick toys you've outgrown. Make a list for a grown-up to approve.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏠 When things have a "home," cleaning up is just putting them back.
- 🖼️ Pictures + words help younger kids who can't read yet.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📦 Let kids decide what to donate (with veto power). Forced purges backfire.
- 🧺 Clear bins help kids see what's inside.
