# 🍱 Lunchbox Notes

Tiny label notes that make someone smile at lunch 😊🍱

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Everyday notes on masking tape. Save the Magic Silver for a **really** special day ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker (for the special one)
- [ ] 🍱 Lunchboxes, snack bags, water bottles

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Think of a person 👨‍👩‍👧
Who gets a note tomorrow?

### 2️⃣ Write a short note ✏️
5 words or less! "YOU ROCK 🎸"

### 3️⃣ Stick it 🏷️
On the sandwich bag, the apple, or the water bottle.

### 4️⃣ Keep it secret 🤫
Surprise!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw a heart or smiley + one word: **HI!**

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write a 3-word note: **HAVE FUN TODAY**

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Joke notes** 😂 Write a riddle on one label, the answer on another.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Note streak** 📅 A different note every day for a week. Plan them on the printable.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Code note** 🔢 Write it in A=1, B=2 code. They decode it at lunch!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Specific praise** ✍️ Write about something **real** they did: "GREAT GOAL SAT." Specific notes mean more.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💌 Small surprises make people feel remembered.
- ✂️ Short notes force you to pick the **best** words.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍎 Tape on wrappers or containers, not directly on food.
- 🏫 Some schools are nut-free etc. — notes only, no extra treats.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🟨 Tape + marker back in the drawer
