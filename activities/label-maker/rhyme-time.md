# 🎵 Rhyme Time Labels

CAT 🐱 → HAT 🎩 → MAT → BAT 🦇 … Label things with **rhyming words!**

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Rhymes on masking tape. The **longest rhyme chain** wins the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 🏠 Things around the house

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a thing 👉
Like the BED 🛏️

### 2️⃣ Find a rhyme 🎵
BED → RED → HEAD → BREAD 🍞

### 3️⃣ Label it 🟨
Write the rhyme. Stick it ON something that matches if you can (BREAD on the bread!).

### 4️⃣ Rhyme walk 🚶
Walk and read every rhyme out loud — in a sing-song voice!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Rhyme with **3-letter words**: CAT, PIG, SUN, BED.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Rhyme chain** ⛓️ How many rhymes in a row? (CAT, HAT, MAT, BAT, SAT…)

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Silly poem** 📜 Use 4 of your rhymes in a 4-line poem. Read it like a rap 🎤

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎵 Rhymes help kids hear the **sounds** inside words.
- 📖 Hearing sounds = big step for reading and spelling.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔤 Nonsense rhymes ("ZAT") are fine for 6-year-olds — they're still hearing the pattern.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🟨 Peel rhyme labels
