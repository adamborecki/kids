# 📏 Measure Everything

How tall is the door? How long is the couch? **Measure it. Label it.** 📏🏷️

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Measurements on masking tape. The **biggest thing in the house** gets the Magic Silver label ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📏 Tape measure + ruler
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 📋 The printable

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Guess first 🤔
How long is it? Write your guess.

### 2️⃣ Measure 📏
Start at the very end. Keep the tape straight.

### 3️⃣ Label it 🟨
"DOOR — 80 INCHES" or "203 cm"

### 4️⃣ Compare 🔁
How close was your guess?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Measure 5 things in inches or cm.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Guess first** — who's the best guesser? 🎯

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Biggest to smallest** 📊 Put all measurements in order.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Both units** 🌍 Label in inches AND cm. (1 inch = 2.54 cm)

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Area** 🟦 Measure a room's length and width. length × width = area. How many square feet (or m²)?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📏 Measuring makes "big" and "small" into exact numbers.
- 🌍 Most countries use cm and meters. The US mostly uses inches and feet.
- 🎯 Guessing first trains your brain to **estimate**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📏 Metal tape measures snap back fast — let it roll back slowly.
- 🟨 Masking tape peels clean from most furniture.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🟨 Peel labels after a few days
- [ ] 📏 Tape measure back
