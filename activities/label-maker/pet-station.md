# 🐾 Pet Care Station

Set up a **pet care station** with labels and a feeding chart. 🐾 No pet? Use a stuffed one! 🧸

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Supply labels on masking tape. The **pet's name** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🐾 Pet supplies (food, bowl, leash, brush)
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 📋 Feeding chart (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Gather the supplies 🧺
Put everything in one spot.

### 2️⃣ Label it all 🟨
FOOD · TREATS · LEASH · BRUSH

### 3️⃣ Learn the routine 📋
How much food? How many times a day? When's the walk?

### 4️⃣ Chart it ✅
Check off every feeding.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Fill the water bowl every morning 💧 with a grown-up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Do the feeding chart for a week.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Pet facts card** 📇 Name, age, favorite treat, vet phone number.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Measure food** 🥄 Use the exact scoop amount from the food bag.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Care plan** 📋 Write instructions so a pet-sitter could take over.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Budget** 💰 How much does food cost per month? Check the bag price and how long it lasts.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐾 Pets need the **same routine** every day to feel safe.
- ✅ Charts stop "double feeding" (pets get overweight!).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🍫 Some human foods are toxic to pets (chocolate, grapes, onions for dogs). Post a "NO" list.
- 🐾 Grown-up stays in charge of meds and vet stuff.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧺 Supplies back in the station
