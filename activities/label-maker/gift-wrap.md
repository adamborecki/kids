# 🎁 Gift Wrap Studio

Wrap a gift like a pro — handmade paper, a bow, and a fancy tag! 🎁✨

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Practice tags on paper. The **TO / FROM** tag on the real gift gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🎁 A gift (or a box to practice)
- [ ] 📄 Plain paper (brown paper bag or newsprint)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers or stamps
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors + 🟨 tape
- [ ] 🎀 String or ribbon
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Decorate the paper 🎨
Patterns, drawings, stamps. Fill it up!

### 2️⃣ Measure 📏
Paper should wrap around the box with a little overlap.

### 3️⃣ Wrap 🎁
Box upside down in the middle. Fold one side, then the other. Tape.

### 4️⃣ Fold the ends 📐
Fold in the sides like triangles, then fold up. Tape.

### 5️⃣ Bow + tag 🎀
Tie string around. Add the silver TO/FROM tag!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Decorate the paper. A grown-up helps wrap 🤝

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Wrap a box yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Neat corners** 📐 Practice the triangle-fold ends until they're crisp.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Pattern paper** 🔁 Design a repeating pattern (stars, stripes, dots).

### 🔟 Age 10
**No-tape wrap** 🧣 Try **furoshiki** — Japanese cloth wrapping with knots instead of tape.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Wrap to measure** 📏 Measure the box. Calculate the paper size (around + overlap) BEFORE cutting. Did it fit?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📦 Folding paper around a box is real **geometry** — flat paper becomes 3D.
- ♻️ Brown paper and cloth wraps are reusable and recyclable.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Scissors safety for young kids.
- 🎁 Let kids wrap a gift for someone else — they're proudest of that.
