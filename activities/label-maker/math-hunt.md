# 🧮 Math Fact Hunt

Math problems are **hiding** around the house! 🕵️ Find them. Solve them. Match them. 🧮

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Problems on masking tape. Solve them all → you earn a Magic Silver **MATH WIZ** label ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 📋 The answer sheet (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Grown-up hides problems 🫣
10 problems on masking tape, stuck around the house.

### 2️⃣ Hunt! 🏃
Find a problem. Solve it on your sheet.

### 3️⃣ Find the answer label ✅
Answers are hidden too! Match them up.

### 4️⃣ All matched? 🎉
You earned the silver label!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Adding to 10: **3 + 4**

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Adding to 20: **8 + 7**, subtract: **12 − 5**

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Two-digit: **25 + 13**, and easy times: **2 × 5**

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Times tables to 10 × 10.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Make the hunt** 🧩 You write and hide 10 problems for a younger sibling (at THEIR level!).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏃 Moving + math = you remember more.
- 🔁 Practice in a game feels less like homework.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎯 Pick problems slightly easy — speed and fun beat struggle here.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🟨 Collect all 20 labels (count them!)
