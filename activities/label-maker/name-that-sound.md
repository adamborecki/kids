# 🎵 Name That Sound

Close your eyes 🙈 … CLANG! What made that sound? 🎵

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Sound labels on masking tape. The **best guesser** wins the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🥄 Sound makers: pot + spoon, keys, crinkly paper, a bell, a squeaky toy
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🙈 A blindfold (or closed eyes)
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Label each sound maker 🟨
Number them: 1, 2, 3…

### 2️⃣ Eyes closed 🙈
The guesser turns around.

### 3️⃣ Make a sound 🔔
One at a time!

### 4️⃣ Guess! 🤔
Say the name — or the number on its label.

### 5️⃣ Score 🏆
1 point per right guess. Switch!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
5 sounds, very different (bell, pot, paper).

### 7️⃣ Age 7
8 sounds. Some are similar (keys vs coins!).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sound story** 📖 Tell a story and make sounds at the right moments.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Loud to soft** 🔊 Rank all the sounds from loudest to softest. Agree?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👂 Your ears + brain can tell sounds apart even with your eyes closed.
- 🎵 Every object vibrates differently — that's why they sound different.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👂 Protect ears: no banging next to someone's head.
- 🔊 It's loud! Warn the neighbors (or the napping baby).

## 🧽 Cleanup
