# 🪴 Garden Markers

What's growing where? **Label the garden!** 🌱🏷️

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Practice on popsicle sticks. Your **favorite plant** gets the Magic Silver label ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🪵 Popsicle sticks, spoons, or flat rocks
- [ ] 🖊️ Waterproof marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 🪴 Pots or garden rows

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Find every plant 🪴
Pots, rows, the herb box.

### 2️⃣ Find its name 🔍
Ask, check the seed packet, or use a plant app.

### 3️⃣ Write the marker 🖊️
Plant name + date planted.

### 4️⃣ Stick it in 🌱
At the front so it's easy to see.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw a picture of the plant on the stick.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write the name: **TOMATO** 🍅

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add the **date planted** 📅

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Water needs** 💧 Add: LOTS / SOME / A LITTLE

### 🔟 Age 10
**Garden map** 🗺️ Draw the whole garden and number each plant.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Grow log** 📈 Track when each plant sprouts, flowers, and fruits. Which grew fastest?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌱 Seedlings look alike when they're small — labels prevent mix-ups.
- 📅 Dates tell you when to expect flowers or food.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖊️ Use a permanent or paint marker — regular marker washes off in the rain.
- 🏷️ Label tape fades in sun; use it on pots, not stakes in full sun.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖊️ Markers capped
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash dirty hands
