# 🏛️ The Toy Museum

Your room is now a **MUSEUM!** 🎟️
You're the boss. You make the labels. You give the tours. 🎤

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Practice labels on masking tape. Only the **STAR exhibit** ⭐ gets the Magic Silver label.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧸 5–10 favorite toys
- [ ] 🪑 A table or shelf (the "gallery")
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker (practice tape)
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker with the ✨ Magic Silver Tape
- [ ] ✂️ Paper for tickets
- [ ] 📋 The printable label cards

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick your exhibits 🧸
Choose 5 to 10 special toys.

### 2️⃣ Set up the gallery 🪑
Line them up. Leave space so visitors can see.

### 3️⃣ Write practice labels 🟨
On masking tape. Keep it short!
- **Name:** RED DINO 🦖
- **Found:** Birthday 2025 🎂
- **Fact:** Very loud roar

### 4️⃣ Choose the STAR ⭐
Which toy is the most special?
It gets **one** Magic Silver label. ✨
Plan the words on paper. Check spelling. **Print once.**

### 5️⃣ Make tickets 🎟️
Draw tickets. Price: 1 hug 🤗 or 3 high-fives ✋

### 6️⃣ OPEN THE MUSEUM! 🎉
Give tours. Tell a story about each toy. 🎤
Rule: **look with your eyes, not your hands** 👀

## 🌟 Level Up
### 🥉 Easy
Just a name on each label. Give one tour.

### 🥈 Medium
Every label gets **3 things**: name, where it came from, one fun fact.

### 🥇 Hard
- 🗺️ Make **wings**: Dino Wing, Car Wing, Plush Wing
- 🗺️ Draw a **museum map**
- 🛍️ Open a **gift shop** with your drawings (pretend money!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏛️ Real museums label everything so visitors learn fast.
- ✂️ Short labels are **hard**! Picking the best words = great writing.
- ⭐ Picking ONE star means deciding what matters most.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧾 Real museum labels have: title, date, material, and 1–2 sentences. Show kids one if you can (a museum website works).
- 🟨 Put masking tape on toys, **not** label tape — label tape glue can leave residue on some plastics. Silver label can go on a card or the shelf in front of the toy.
- ✂️ Label tape is precious: keep silver labels to ~12 letters.

## 🧽 Cleanup
**$** — easy
- [ ] 🧸 Toys back home
- [ ] 🟨 Peel masking-tape labels (save them in an envelope for next time!)
- [ ] ✨ Keep the silver label forever
