# 🔤 Label the House

Put **word labels** on real things. 🚪 = DOOR
Walk around. Read them ALL! 📖

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Everyday words go on masking tape. The **hardest word you learn** this week gets the Magic Silver label. ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + a thick marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker with ✨ Magic Silver Tape
- [ ] 📋 The word list (printable)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Pick 10 things 🔟
Walk around. Point at things. 👉🚪👉🛏️👉💡

### 2️⃣ Write the words 🟨
Big letters on masking tape: **DOOR, BED, LAMP**

### 3️⃣ Stick them on 🏷️
Right on the thing!

### 4️⃣ Reading walk 🚶
Walk around. Touch each label. Read it out loud! 🗣️

### 5️⃣ Mix-up game 🔀
A grown-up pulls off 3 labels.
Can you stick them back on the right things?

### 6️⃣ Silver word ✨
Which word was hardest? Plan it on paper.
Print it **once** on silver. You earned it! 🏆

## 🌟 Level Up
### 🥉 Easy — 3 letters
CUP · BED · RUG · MAT · TOY · BOX · HAT · PAN

### 🥈 Medium — 4 or 5 letters
DOOR · LAMP · SINK · CHAIR · TABLE · CLOCK · SHOES · COUCH

### 🥇 Hard — two words or a new language
RED CHAIR · SOFT RUG · BIG WINDOW
🌎 Or Spanish: PUERTA (door) · MESA (table) · SILLA (chair) · CAMA (bed)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔗 Seeing a word **on** the real thing links word ↔ thing in your brain.
- 🏫 Preschool classrooms label everything for this reason.
- 🔁 You see the words 20 times a day without trying!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🟨 Masking tape peels clean from most things. Skip fresh paint and fancy wood finishes.
- 🔠 Use ALL CAPS for beginners, or match what their school uses.
- 🎯 Start with 5 words. Add 2–3 new ones each day.

## 🧽 Cleanup
**$** — leave them up a few days!
- [ ] 🟨 Peel labels when done
- [ ] 🫙 Save them in a "word jar" to review
