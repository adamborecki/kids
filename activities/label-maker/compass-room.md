# 🧭 Compass Room

Which way is **NORTH**? 🧭 Label the walls, then play the direction game!

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** N E S W on masking tape. The **NORTH** label gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧭 A compass (or a phone compass app)
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Find north 🧭
Hold the compass flat. Turn until the needle points to N.

### 2️⃣ Label the walls 🟨
N on the north wall. Then E, S, W going clockwise.

### 3️⃣ Direction game 🎮
"Take 3 steps NORTH. 2 steps EAST." Where did you end up?

### 4️⃣ Hide + seek 🫣
Hide a toy. Give directions only with N, E, S, W!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Label 4 walls. Point to each one when someone calls it.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Direction game with steps.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**In-between** ↗️ Add NE, SE, SW, NW.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Sun check** ☀️ The sun rises roughly in the east and sets in the west. Check it against your labels!

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Treasure directions** 🗺️ Write directions to a hidden object using directions + steps. Can someone follow them exactly?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧲 Earth is like a giant magnet. A compass needle lines up with it.
- 🧭 Remember clockwise: **N**ever **E**at **S**oggy **W**affles 🧇

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📱 Keep phones away from the compass — magnets and metal mess it up.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🟨 Labels can stay up — they're useful!
