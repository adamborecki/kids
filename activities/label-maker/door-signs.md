# 🚪 Door Sign Maker

**KNOCK FIRST!** 🚪✋ Make signs for your door — and your whole house.

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Practice signs on paper. Your **door name** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📄 Paper or cardstock
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a message 💭
KNOCK FIRST · OPEN · SLEEPING 😴 · GENIUS AT WORK

### 2️⃣ Design it 🎨
Big letters. Bright colors. A border.

### 3️⃣ Make it flip 🔄
Front: OPEN ✅ Back: CLOSED ⛔

### 4️⃣ Hang it 🚪
Tape it at eye level.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Your name + a picture of you.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
A flip sign: OPEN / CLOSED.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Hours sign** 🕐 "Open 8 am – 8 pm" — practice telling time!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Room rules** 📜 3 polite rules for visitors.

### 🔟 Age 10
**House signs** 🏠 Make helpful signs for other rooms: "TURN OFF LIGHT 💡", "WIPE FEET 👟".

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Sign design** 🎨 Real signs use few words, big contrast, and a symbol. Make one that someone can read from 3 meters away. Test it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🚪 Signs tell people what to do **without talking**.
- 🔤 Good signs = few words + big letters + a picture.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🟨 Painter's tape is gentlest on doors.
