# 🗳️ Family Vote Booth

What's for dinner? Which movie? **Let's VOTE!** 🗳️

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Ballots on paper. The **winning choice** gets the Magic Silver label ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A box with a slot (ballot box)
- [ ] 📄 Paper ballots (printable)
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] ✏️ Pencils

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick the question ❓
"Friday movie?" · "Saturday breakfast?" · "Weekend trip?"

### 2️⃣ List the choices 📋
2–4 choices on the ballot.

### 3️⃣ Vote in secret 🤫
Everyone marks one choice. Fold it. In the box!

### 4️⃣ Count 🔢
Open the box. Tally the votes out loud.

### 5️⃣ Announce 📣
The winner gets the silver label!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Be the **ballot box guard** 💂 and count the votes.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make the ballot box. Label it **VOTE HERE** 🗳️

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Tally marks** — IIII for 5. Count them up.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Campaign** 📣 Make a poster for your choice. Give a 30-second speech!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Ranked vote** 🔢 Everyone ranks choices 1st, 2nd, 3rd. Lowest choice is cut each round until one has more than half.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Fair rules** ⚖️ What if it's a tie? Write the family rules for ties and how often anyone can call a vote.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗳️ Secret ballots let people vote honestly.
- 🔢 Ranked voting is used in some real elections.
- ⚖️ Voting = fair way to decide when people disagree.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤝 Decide beforehand what's on the ballot — and stick to the result!
- 😊 Losing gracefully is the real lesson.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🗳️ Keep the ballot box for next time
