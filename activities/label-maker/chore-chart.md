# ✅ Chore Chart Builder

Design a chore chart you'll **actually** use. ✅🏷️

> 🪄 **Silver Rule:** Chore names on masking tape so you can move them. The **chart title** gets the Magic Silver ✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📄 Big paper or poster board
- [ ] 🟨 Masking tape + marker
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker
- [ ] 📏 Ruler
- [ ] 🌟 Stickers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ List the chores 📝
Make bed · Feed pet · Set table · Laundry to basket · Water plants

### 2️⃣ Draw the grid 📏
Chores down the side. Days across the top.

### 3️⃣ Chore labels 🟨
Masking tape so you can swap chores later.

### 4️⃣ Hang it up 📌
Somewhere you'll see it every day (fridge!).

### 5️⃣ Check it off ✅
Sticker or check when it's done.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
3 chores, 7 days. Stickers when done 🌟

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add **points** for each chore. Bigger job = more points.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Rotation** 🔄 Siblings switch chores every week. Plan the rotation.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fair split** ⚖️ Time each chore. Make sure everyone's minutes are about equal.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Proposal** 📋 Present your system to the family. What's the reward? What happens if chores are skipped?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ✅ Checking things off feels good — your brain gives you a little reward!
- 🔄 Rotations keep it fair and keep it from getting boring.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤝 Let kids help design it — they follow systems they built.
- 💰 Decide ahead: points → privileges, allowance, or just pride.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📌 Chart up on the fridge
