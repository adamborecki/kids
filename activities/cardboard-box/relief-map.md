# 🏔️ 3D Mountain Map

Stack cardboard layers into a **3D mountain** — just like the lines on real hiking maps! 🏔️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 Thin cardboard (cereal boxes) — 6 to 8 pieces
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paint (green, brown, white)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw the base 🏞️
On the first piece, draw a big blob shape — the bottom of your mountain.

### 2️⃣ Smaller and smaller 🔁
On each next piece, draw a smaller shape inside the last one's outline.

### 3️⃣ Cut ✂️
Cut out all the layers.

### 4️⃣ Stack + glue 🏔️
Biggest on bottom, smallest on top.

### 5️⃣ Paint 🎨
Green low, brown middle, white snow on top!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Build a 5-layer mountain. Paint it.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Contour lines** 🗺️ Trace each layer's outline on one paper, nested. That's a topographic map! Steep side = lines close together.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Real map match** 🔍 Find a real topographic map online. Build a mountain from it, one layer per contour line.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗺️ Hiking maps use **contour lines** — each line is one height.
- 📏 Lines close together = steep. Far apart = gentle slope.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Thin cardboard cuts with kid scissors. Thick boxes = grown-up.
