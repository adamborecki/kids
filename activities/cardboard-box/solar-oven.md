# ☀️ Pizza Box Solar Oven

Cook s'mores with **only sunlight!** ☀️🍫 No fire, no stove.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🍕 A clean pizza box
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🥈 Aluminum foil + 🎞️ plastic wrap
- [ ] ⚫ Black paper
- [ ] 🟨 Tape + a stick or ruler (to prop the flap)
- [ ] 🍫 Graham crackers, chocolate, marshmallows
- [ ] 🌡️ An oven thermometer (optional)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut a flap 🔪
Grown-up cuts 3 sides of a square in the lid (leave 3 cm border). Fold it up.

### 2️⃣ Foil the flap 🥈
Shiny side out. This is your reflector.

### 3️⃣ Seal the window 🎞️
Tape plastic wrap over the hole in the lid. Airtight!

### 4️⃣ Black floor ⚫
Black paper on the bottom inside.

### 5️⃣ Cook ☀️
S'mores on a plate inside. Aim the flap to bounce sun in. Wait 30–60 minutes on a hot sunny day.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Build it with a grown-up. Make s'mores!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Temperature log** 🌡️ Check the oven thermometer every 10 minutes. Graph it.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Improve it** 🔧 More foil? Insulation (newspaper) around the edges? Test changes and compare temperatures.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ☀️ Foil **reflects** extra sunlight into the box.
- ⚫ Black paper **absorbs** light and turns it into heat.
- 🎞️ Plastic wrap traps the warm air inside — like a greenhouse.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔥 It gets hot! Oven mitts to take food out.
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 😎 Don't stare at the bright foil.
- ☀️ Works best near midday on hot, clear days.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🍫 Wipe the plate
- [ ] 📦 Keep the oven for next sunny day
