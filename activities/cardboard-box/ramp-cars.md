# 🛝 Ramp Car Lab

How far will a toy car roll? 🚗 Change the ramp and find out! 🛝

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A long flat cardboard panel (the ramp)
- [ ] 📚 Books (to raise the ramp)
- [ ] 🚗 Toy cars
- [ ] 📏 Tape measure
- [ ] 🟨 Tape (to mark distances)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Build the ramp 📚
Panel on 1 book.

### 2️⃣ Let go 🚗
No pushing! Just let it roll.

### 3️⃣ Measure 📏
From the bottom of the ramp to where it stops.

### 4️⃣ Add a book 📚
Again! Then 3 books, 4 books.

### 5️⃣ Compare 📊

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
1 book vs. 3 books. Which rolls farther?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Measure 4 heights. Fill in the chart.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Surface test** 🧶 Carpet vs. floor at the bottom. Which car goes farther?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Car test** 🏎️ Which of your cars goes farthest from the same ramp? Why?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Graph + predict** 📈 Graph height vs. distance. Predict the distance for 5 books, then test.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⬆️ Higher ramp = more **stored energy** at the top.
- 🧶 Rough surfaces (carpet) have more friction — the car stops sooner.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📏 Same car, same release, every time = fair test.

## 🧽 Cleanup
📚 Books back on the shelf
