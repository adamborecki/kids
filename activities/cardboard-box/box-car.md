# 🚗 Drive-In Box Car

Build a car you can **sit in** 🚗 … then watch a movie at the drive-in! 🎬

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A box big enough to sit in
- [ ] 🍽️ 4 paper plates (wheels)
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps or paper (headlights, buttons)
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers
- [ ] 🟨 Tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Open the top 📦
Tape the flaps down inside, or have a grown-up cut them off.

### 2️⃣ Wheels 🍽️
Tape a paper plate on each corner.

### 3️⃣ Details 🎨
Headlights, a license plate with your name, a steering wheel (paper plate!).

### 4️⃣ Drive-in night 🎬
Park all the box cars in front of the TV. Popcorn! 🍿

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Build it and "drive" around the house. Beep beep!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**License plate** 🔤 Make one with your name and lucky number.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Car wash or gas station** ⛽ Build a second box as a station. Pretend-pay for fuel!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎭 Pretend play builds imagination and storytelling.
- 🚗 Drive-in movies were huge in the 1950s — people watched from their cars!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Grown-up trims flaps if needed.
