# 🖍️ Giant Coloring Wall

A **giant coloring page** the whole family can color at once! 🖍️👨‍👩‍👧

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A huge flat box panel (or several taped together)
- [ ] 🖊️ A thick black marker
- [ ] 🖍️ Crayons, markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Grown-up draws outlines 🖊️
Big simple shapes: a city, a jungle, an ocean scene.

### 2️⃣ Lay it on the floor 📦
Or tape it to a wall.

### 3️⃣ Everyone colors 🖍️
Pick a section!

### 4️⃣ Add your own 🎨
Hide a tiny drawing somewhere. Can others find it?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Color your favorite parts.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Hidden objects** 🔍 Hide 5 tiny things. Others find them.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Design it** ✏️ You draw the outlines for everyone else!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Team mural** 🧑‍🎨 Each person gets a section. Make the edges connect (a road that crosses all sections).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🤝 Big shared art = teamwork. Everyone adds a piece.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖍️ Crayons work great on cardboard. Paint gets soggy.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖍️ Crayons back in the box
- [ ] 📦 Hang the mural or recycle it
