# 🧩 Giant Floor Puzzle

Draw a **giant picture** on a flat box 🎨 … cut it up … put it back together! 🧩

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A large flat box panel
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers or paint
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil + 📏 ruler
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw a big picture 🎨
Fill the whole panel. Bright colors!

### 2️⃣ Draw puzzle lines ✏️
Wavy lines across and down. 10–20 pieces.

### 3️⃣ Grown-up cuts 🔪
Along the lines.

### 4️⃣ Mix + solve 🧩
Scramble the pieces. Put it back together!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
10 big pieces. Solve it with a grown-up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
15 pieces. Time yourself!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Swap puzzles** 🔄 Make one for a friend. Trade and solve!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tricky cuts** 🧠 Make wavy, jigsaw-style edges. Write a number on the back of each piece in case of emergencies.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧩 Puzzles train your brain to match shapes and colors.
- 🎨 Edges and corners are easiest to start — they have flat sides!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧩 Pieces in a big zip bag
