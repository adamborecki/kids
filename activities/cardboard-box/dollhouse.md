# 🏠 Box Dollhouse

Stack boxes into a **house with rooms!** 🏠 Decorate every room.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 3–4 shoeboxes or small boxes (rooms)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape or glue
- [ ] 🎨 Paper scraps, fabric, paint (wallpaper + rugs)
- [ ] 📦 Tiny boxes, caps, and spools (furniture)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Stack rooms 📦
Boxes on their sides, open side facing you. Tape them together.

### 2️⃣ Wallpaper 🎨
Glue paper inside each room.

### 3️⃣ Furniture 🛋️
Matchbox beds, cap tables, spool stools.

### 4️⃣ Move in 🧸
Small toys or paper people!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
2 rooms. Decorate them.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
4 rooms: kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Tiny details** 🔍 Paper books, a picture frame, a rug.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Lights** 🔦 Add a battery tea light in one room.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Floor plan** 📐 Draw the house from above with measurements first, then build to match.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏠 Building tiny rooms is how architects plan real ones.
- 🔍 Small details make it feel real!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Grown-up helps cut windows and doors.
