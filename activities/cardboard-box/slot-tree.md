# 🌳 Slot-Together Tree

Two flat cardboard trees **slot together** into one that stands up! 🌳

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 Thin cardboard (cereal box)
- [ ] ✏️ Pencil
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🎨 Paint or markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw a tree 🌳
On cardboard. Make it about 20 cm tall.

### 2️⃣ Copy it ✏️
Trace it again — you need 2 matching trees.

### 3️⃣ Cut both out ✂️

### 4️⃣ Cut the slots ✂️
Tree 1: a slit from the TOP down to the middle. Tree 2: a slit from the BOTTOM up to the middle.

### 5️⃣ Slide together 🌳
Cross them. It stands!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make one tree with help.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Seasons** 🍂 Make 4 trees: spring, summer, fall, winter.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Animals too** 🦌 Try a deer or a dinosaur the same way.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Forest diorama** 🌲 Make 5 different trees and a ground base.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ✂️ Two flat shapes crossing make a 3D shape that stands on its own.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ✂️ Slits must be exactly half the height. Pre-mark them for 6-year-olds.
