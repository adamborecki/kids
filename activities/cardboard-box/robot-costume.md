# 🤖 Box Robot Costume

Become a **ROBOT!** 🤖 BEEP BOOP. A box body, a box head, and lots of buttons.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 1 big box (body) + 1 smaller box (head)
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🥈 Foil or silver paint
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps, lids, tubes (dials + buttons)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape + 🖊️ glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Grown-up cuts holes 🔪
Body: head hole + 2 arm holes. Head: face window.

### 2️⃣ Robot skin 🥈
Foil or silver paint.

### 3️⃣ Controls 🎛️
Glue bottle caps, draw screens and dials.

### 4️⃣ Antennae 📡
Pipe cleaners or straws on the head.

### 5️⃣ Power on ⚡
Robot walk + robot voice!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Wear it and do the robot dance 🕺

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Label your buttons: LASER 🔦, DANCE 💃, SNACK MODE 🍪

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Robot commands** 🎮 Someone gives commands. The robot obeys exactly.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Robot name + backstory** 📝 Who built you? What's your mission?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Program a friend** 💻 Write step-by-step instructions to make a sandwich. The "robot" follows them exactly — even the silly mistakes!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🤖 Robots follow instructions **exactly** — that's why coding has to be precise.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 👀 Make sure the face window is big enough to see where you walk.
