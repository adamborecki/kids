# 🕹️ Box Pinball Machine

Build a **real pinball machine** from a box lid! 🕹️ Flippers, bumpers, and a launcher.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A box lid or shallow box (tray)
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🪵 Popsicle sticks (flippers) + paper fasteners (pivots)
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps + rubber bands (bumpers)
- [ ] 🔮 A marble
- [ ] 🟨 Tape + 🖊️ glue

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Tilt the tray 📐
Prop the top end up on a book.

### 2️⃣ Flippers 🪵
Grown-up pokes holes near the bottom. Attach sticks with fasteners so they swing. Push their ends through slots in the side so you can flip from outside.

### 3️⃣ Bumpers ⚪
Glue caps in the middle. Stretch rubber bands between them for bouncy walls.

### 4️⃣ Targets 🎯
Paper cups or bonus zones with points.

### 5️⃣ Play! 🕹️
Roll the marble from the top. Keep it alive!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Build with flippers and 3 bumpers.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Launcher** 🚀 Add a plunger: a pencil + rubber band in a side lane.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Scoring design** 🧮 Balance the points so the game is hard but fair. Playtest 10 times and adjust.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎱 Rubber bands bounce the marble back with energy (they stretch, then snap back).
- 🕹️ Flippers are **levers** — a small push at one end makes a big swing at the other.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters + holes are grown-up jobs.
- 🔮 Marbles: choking hazard for toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🔮 Marble in a bag
- [ ] ✂️ Scraps in recycling
