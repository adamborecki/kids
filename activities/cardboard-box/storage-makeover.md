# 🎒 Storage Box Makeover

Turn a plain box into a **beautiful storage bin** for your stuff. 🎒✨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A sturdy box
- [ ] 🎨 Wrapping paper, fabric, or paint
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue or 🟨 tape
- [ ] 🏷️ Label maker (or masking tape)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a purpose 🤔
Art supplies? LEGO? Hair stuff? Secret treasures?

### 2️⃣ Cover it 🎨
Wrap in paper or paint it.

### 3️⃣ Handles ✂️
Grown-up cuts handle holes on the sides.

### 4️⃣ Label it 🏷️
Big and clear!

### 5️⃣ Fill + shelve 🗂️

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Decorate and fill it.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add a label with a word AND a picture.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Dividers** 🧩 Add cardboard dividers inside.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Matching set** 🎨 Make 3 matching boxes for a shelf.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Room plan** 📐 Look at your room. What needs a box? Make a plan.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Declutter** 📦 Sort what you have: keep, donate, toss. THEN make boxes only for the keep pile.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏠 Labeled boxes make cleanup fast and finding things easy.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Handle holes are a grown-up cut.

## 🧽 Cleanup
✂️ Scraps in recycling
