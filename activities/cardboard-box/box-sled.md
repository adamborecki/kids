# 🛷 Cardboard Grass Sled

No snow? No problem! Slide down a **grassy hill** on flattened cardboard. 🛷🌿

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big flattened box
- [ ] 🟨 Duct tape (on the bottom edges — makes it slide better)
- [ ] 🌿 A gentle grassy hill (no trees, roads, or rocks at the bottom!)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Flatten + tape 📦
Tape all bottom edges smooth.

### 2️⃣ Check the hill 👀
Walk it first. Clear run-out at the bottom!

### 3️⃣ Sit + push 🛷
Hold the front edge up like a toboggan.

### 4️⃣ WHEEEE! 🎉
Walk back up. Again!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Slide with a grown-up or a buddy.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Slide solo on a gentle slope.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Speed test** ⏱️ Time your runs. Fastest?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Sled design** 🔧 Waxed paper bottom vs. plain cardboard. Which is faster?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Race** 🏁 Side-by-side races with a starter and finish line.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Friction lab** 🧪 Test 3 bottom materials, 3 runs each. Average them. Which has the least friction?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🛷 Smooth bottoms slide better — less **friction**.
- ⬇️ Steeper hills = more speed (and more risk!).

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ⛑️ Helmet for steeper hills.
- 🚫 No hills that end at roads, water, fences, or trees.
- 🌿 Dry grass is fastest. Wet grass = muddy.

- [ ] 📦 Recycle soggy cardboard
- [ ] 👖 Grass-stained pants to the laundry
