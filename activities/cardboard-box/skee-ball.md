# 🎳 Box Skee-Ball

An **arcade game** in your living room! 🎳🎟️ Roll the ball up the ramp, into the holes.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A long box (or 2 boxes)
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🥣 Bowls or cups to trace holes
- [ ] 🎾 Small balls (tennis balls or socks)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers + 🟨 tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the ramp 📐
Prop the box at an angle (books underneath) or tape a flat piece of cardboard as a ramp leading up.

### 2️⃣ Trace holes 🥣
Trace 4–5 circles of different sizes on the back panel.

### 3️⃣ Grown-up cuts holes 🔪
Small holes = big points.

### 4️⃣ Label points 🔢
10, 20, 50, 100!

### 5️⃣ Roll! 🎳
Roll up the ramp. Count tickets 🎟️

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Roll 5 balls each. Add the points.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Prize counter** 🎟️ 100 points = 1 ticket. Trade tickets for prizes (stickers, picking dessert).

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Ramp angle** 📐 Steeper or flatter ramp — which scores better?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Design** ✏️ Change the hole sizes and points so the game is fair but tricky.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Expected points** 🧮 Roll 20 times. Average points per roll? Use it to set prize prices.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎳 The ramp turns rolling into a small jump — the "skee" part.
- 🧮 Arcade designers make hard holes worth more so it's fair.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 🎾 Soft balls protect walls and TVs.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🎾 Balls in a bin
- [ ] 📦 Store the game flat
