# 🎯 Box Cornhole

Toss bean bags at a slanted board 🎯 **In the hole = 3 points!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 2 large flat box panels
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🥣 A bowl to trace the hole
- [ ] 📚 Books or bricks to prop the back up
- [ ] 🧦 Bean bags (or rolled socks)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Trace + cut 🔪
Trace a circle near the top of each panel. Grown-up cuts it out.

### 2️⃣ Slant it 📐
Prop the top end up on books.

### 3️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Team colors!

### 4️⃣ Play 🧦
Stand 3 meters away (closer for little kids). On the board = 1 point. In the hole = 3!

### 5️⃣ First to 21 wins 🏆

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Stand close. Count your points.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Teams! Keep score on the printable.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Cancel scoring** ➖ Real rules: subtract the other team's points each round. Only the difference counts!

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Distance challenge** 📏 Step back 1 meter for every win.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Tournament** 🏆 Bracket for 4+ teams.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Stats** 📊 Track holes vs. boards for each player. Best accuracy?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎯 Soft, high tosses land and stay. Hard throws slide off!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 🧦 Rice-filled socks (tied off) make easy bean bags.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧦 Bags in a bin
- [ ] 📦 Boards flat against a wall
