# 🧱 Slot-Together Blocks

Cut cardboard into shapes with **slots** 🧱 … slide them together to build anything!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 Flat cardboard panels
- [ ] 📏 Ruler + ✏️ pencil
- [ ] 🔪 Box cutter (**grown-up only**)
- [ ] 🎨 Markers or paint

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Cut shapes 🔪
Grown-up cuts 20+ squares, circles, rectangles (about hand size).

### 2️⃣ Cut slots 🔪
2–4 slots in each piece, about 2 cm deep, as wide as the cardboard.

### 3️⃣ Decorate 🎨
Color the pieces.

### 4️⃣ Build! 🧱
Slide slots together. Towers, animals, robots!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Build a tower. How tall?

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Build an animal 🦒

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Build challenge** 🏆 Make something that stands on its own and holds a book.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Copy a building** 🏛️ Look at a photo. Rebuild it with your pieces.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Design new pieces** ✏️ Draw new shapes. What would make building easier?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Flat-pack furniture** 🪑 Design a tiny chair that slots together with no tape — like real flat-pack furniture.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧱 Slots lock pieces together without glue — the same idea as flat-pack furniture.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 📏 Slot width = cardboard thickness for a snug fit.
