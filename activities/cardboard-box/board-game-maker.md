# 🎲 Board Game Maker

Design your **own board game** on a flattened box! 🎲 Path, rules, pieces — all yours.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big flat box panel (the board)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers
- [ ] 🎲 A die (or make one!)
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps or small toys (pieces)
- [ ] 📇 Index cards (chance cards)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Theme 💡
Space race? Candy land? Escape the volcano?

### 2️⃣ Draw the path 🛣️
START to FINISH. 30–50 spaces.

### 3️⃣ Special spaces ⭐
"Go back 3." "Draw a card." "Slide down the slide!"

### 4️⃣ Chance cards 📇
Write 10 surprises.

### 5️⃣ Rules 📜
Write them down. Playtest! Fix what's boring or unfair.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
A path game with a die and 5 special spaces.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
Add **chance cards** with surprises.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Choices** 🤔 Add a fork in the path: short + risky vs. long + safe.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Balance it** ⚖️ Playtest 3 times. Track who wins and why. Change the rules so it's fair but exciting.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🎲 Game designers **playtest** over and over to make games fun.
- 📜 Clear rules prevent arguments!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🤝 Let them make it unfair at first — then discover why it's not fun.
