# 🏰 Epic Box Fort

Boxes + blankets + tape = **the best fort ever.** 🏰

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 2–6 big boxes (appliance or moving boxes are best)
- [ ] 🟨 Packing tape or duct tape
- [ ] 🛏️ Blankets, pillows, flashlights
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers
- [ ] ✂️ Grown-up with a box cutter (for doors + windows)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Plan it ✏️
Rooms? Tunnels? A tower? Sketch it first.

### 2️⃣ Connect boxes 🟨
Tape boxes together, open side to open side = tunnels!

### 3️⃣ Doors + windows 🚪
Grown-up cuts them. Leave one side attached so doors swing.

### 4️⃣ Decorate 🖍️
Bricks, vines, a sign: "NO GROWN-UPS (except snack delivery)" 😄

### 5️⃣ Move in 🛏️
Pillows, flashlight, books!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
One-room fort with a door and a window.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
2 rooms + a tunnel.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Fort map** 🗺️ Draw a map of your fort with labels.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Secret entrance** 🕵️ A hidden flap only you know about.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Architect** 📐 Plan rooms on paper first. Measure the boxes.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Strong roof** 🏗️ Design a roof that doesn't sag. Try folded triangles (they're strong!).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔺 Folded cardboard and triangles are much stronger than flat sheets.
- 🏠 Planning before building saves a ton of tape.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Box cutters are grown-up only.
- 🔦 Flashlights, not candles, inside forts.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📦 Fold the fort flat (or keep it a few days!)
- [ ] ✂️ Scraps in recycling
