# 📺 Box TV News Show

Cut a TV screen from a box 📺 … and **go live** with the family news! 🎤

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big box
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors (grown-up helps with the screen hole)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers (knobs, logo)
- [ ] 🎤 A "microphone" (hairbrush works)
- [ ] 📝 The news script (printable)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the TV 📺
Cut a big screen window. Draw knobs and a channel number.

### 2️⃣ Write the news 📝
Top story, weather, sports, a funny moment.

### 3️⃣ Get behind the screen 🎤
Hold the box in front of you (or set it on a table).

### 4️⃣ GO LIVE! 🔴
"Good evening, I'm ___ and this is the Family News!"

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Read 2 headlines and the weather.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Weather map** 🌦️ Draw a weather map and point to it.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Interviews** 🎤 Interview a family member about their day.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Commercial break** 📺 Write a 30-second ad for something silly.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Real reporting** 📰 One true story: who, what, when, where, why. Check your facts!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 📰 Reporters answer **who, what, when, where, why**.
- 🎤 Speaking on "camera" builds confidence.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📱 Film it for grandparents!

## 🧽 Cleanup
📺 Keep the TV for next week's show
