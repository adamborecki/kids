# 🏘️ Cardboard Town

Draw roads on a big box panel 🛣️ add buildings from small boxes 🏘️ **drive your toy cars around town!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big flat panel (the ground)
- [ ] 📦 Small boxes: cereal, tea, toothpaste boxes (buildings)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers + 🎨 paint
- [ ] 🚗 Toy cars

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw roads 🛣️
Gray roads, a crosswalk, a roundabout.

### 2️⃣ Make buildings 🏠
Small boxes become a school, a fire station, a bakery, your house!

### 3️⃣ Add details 🌳
Parks, a lake, trees from paper.

### 4️⃣ Drive! 🚗
Deliver pizza, race the fire truck, park at school.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Roads + 3 buildings. Drive around!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Signs** 🛑 STOP signs, street names, a speed limit.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Map key** 🗺️ Make a key for your town: 🏥 = hospital, 🏫 = school…

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Town planner** 📐 Where should the fire station go so it reaches everything fast? Plan it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🗺️ Building a town teaches how real maps and neighborhoods work.
- 🚒 Town planners decide where schools, parks, and fire stations go.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 📦 Start saving small boxes a week ahead!
