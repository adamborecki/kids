# 🌀 Box Lid Marble Maze

Tilt the box… guide the marble… **don't fall in the trap!** 🌀🔮

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A shallow box lid (shoebox lid or tray)
- [ ] 🧃 Straws or cardboard strips (walls)
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue (tacky glue works well)
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🔮 A marble

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw the path ✏️
Pencil a START and FINISH, and a winding path.

### 2️⃣ Cut walls ✂️
Straws or strips of cardboard.

### 3️⃣ Glue walls 🖊️
Along your lines. Let dry.

### 4️⃣ Traps 🕳️
Draw circles — if the marble rolls in, start over!

### 5️⃣ Tilt + roll 🔮

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Simple maze with 3 turns.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Add 3 traps.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Timed runs** ⏱️ Race a friend.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Dead ends** 🚫 Add fake paths that go nowhere.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Two marbles** 🔮🔮 Design a maze for 2 marbles at once. Way harder!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ⬇️ Tilting changes which way gravity pulls the marble.
- 🧠 Mazes train planning and steady hands.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔮 Marbles are a choking hazard for toddlers.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue away
- [ ] 🔮 Marble in a bag
