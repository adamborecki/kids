# 🚀 Spaceship Cockpit

3… 2… 1… **LIFTOFF!** 🚀 Build a cockpit with buttons, screens, and switches.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big box
- [ ] ⚪ Bottle caps (buttons), 🧻 tubes (thrusters), foil
- [ ] 📄 Paper for screens and labels
- [ ] 🖊️ Glue + 🟨 tape
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Control panel 🎛️
Glue bottle caps in rows. Label them: LAUNCH 🚀, SHIELDS 🛡️, HYPERSPEED ⚡

### 2️⃣ Screen 🖥️
Draw a space view or a map on paper. Tape it inside.

### 3️⃣ Thrusters 🧻
Tape tubes to the back. Stuff with red tissue (flames!).

### 4️⃣ Countdown 🔟
Climb in. 10, 9, 8…

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Big launch button + countdown!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Mission control** 🎧 One person in the ship, one at "mission control" giving orders.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Planet trip** 🪐 Pick a planet. Draw the screen view of it.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Space log** 📓 Write a captain's log: where you went, what you found, what broke!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🚀 Real cockpits have lots of labeled buttons so pilots never guess.
- 🔟 Countdowns help everyone get ready at the same moment.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖊️ Glue needs to dry before blastoff.

## 🧽 Cleanup
