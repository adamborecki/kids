# 🌉 Bridge Builder

Build a cardboard bridge between two chairs 🌉 … how many cans can it hold? 🥫

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 Cardboard (cereal boxes + shipping boxes)
- [ ] 🟨 Tape
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors
- [ ] 🪑 2 chairs (30 cm apart)
- [ ] 🥫 Cans for weight

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Flat test 📄
Lay one flat piece across the gap. Add cans. How many before it sags?

### 2️⃣ Fold it 🔺
Fold cardboard into a triangle tube or an "I" shape. Test again!

### 3️⃣ Build your design 🏗️
Combine shapes. Tape well.

### 4️⃣ Load it 🥫
One can at a time. Count until it fails!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Test flat vs. folded cardboard.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Shapes test** 🔺⬜⭕ Triangle tube vs. square tube vs. round tube.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Design challenge** 🏆 Strongest bridge from just 2 cereal boxes.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Strength to weight** ⚖️ Weigh your bridge. Divide what it held by what it weighs. Best ratio wins!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔺 Triangles don't bend out of shape — that's why bridges use them (trusses).
- 📏 Folding makes cardboard **deeper**, and deeper beams are way stiffer.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥫 Keep toes out from under the bridge when it's loaded!
