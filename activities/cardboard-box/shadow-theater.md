# 🔦 Shadow Puppet Theater

Cut out puppets 🐉 shine a flashlight 🔦 and tell a story with **shadows!**

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A box (cut a big window in one side)
- [ ] 📄 Parchment or white tissue paper (screen)
- [ ] ⚫ Black paper or cereal-box cardboard (puppets)
- [ ] 🪵 Sticks or straws (puppet handles)
- [ ] 🔦 A flashlight
- [ ] ✂️ Scissors + 🟨 tape

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make the screen 📄
Grown-up cuts a window. Tape paper over it.

### 2️⃣ Cut puppets ✂️
Outlines: a dragon, a knight, a castle, a tree.

### 3️⃣ Handles 🪵
Tape a stick to each puppet.

### 4️⃣ Lights 🔦
Lights off! Flashlight behind the puppets, shining at the screen.

### 5️⃣ Show 🎭
Audience sits in front.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
2 puppets + a short story.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Size trick** 🔍 Move a puppet closer to the light = giant shadow! Use it for a monster.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Moving parts** 🦾 Attach an arm with a paper fastener so it moves.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Script** 📝 Write a 3-scene show with sound effects.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Colored shadows** 🌈 Cut windows in puppets and cover them with colored cellophane.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔦 Shadows form where the puppet **blocks** light.
- 🔍 Closer to the light = bigger shadow.
- 🌏 Shadow puppetry is over 1,000 years old in places like China and Indonesia.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔪 Window cutting is a grown-up job.
