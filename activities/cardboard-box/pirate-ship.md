# 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Ship

**AHOY!** 🏴‍☠️ A big box becomes a pirate ship. Raise the flag and sail!

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 📦 A big box (the ship)
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towel roll (telescope + mast)
- [ ] 📄 Paper (sail + flag)
- [ ] 🪵 A broom handle or stick (mast — optional)
- [ ] 🖍️ Markers

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Ship shape 📦
Draw the wood planks and round windows (portholes) on the sides.

### 2️⃣ Mast + sail ⛵
Tape a stick or tube upright. Paper sail!

### 3️⃣ Pirate flag 🏴‍☠️
Design your own flag.

### 4️⃣ Telescope 🔭
A tube for the lookout.

### 5️⃣ Set sail! 🌊
"Land ho!" Find treasure (hide some first).

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Sail to the couch island. Find the hidden treasure!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Pirate code** 📜 Write 3 ship rules.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Treasure map** 🗺️ Make a map of the house with an X. The crew follows it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏴‍☠️ Real pirate flags warned other ships — designs were meant to scare.
- 🔭 Lookouts used telescopes to see far away.
