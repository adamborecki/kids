# 🕸️ Spiderweb Hunt

Spiderwebs are **invisible**… until you mist them! 💦🕸️ Then they sparkle like diamonds.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] 🌅 Early morning or evening
- [ ] 🔍 Magnifying glass (optional)
- [ ] 📋 The printable + pencil

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Go slow 🐢
Check bushes, fences, corners, and between plants.

### 2️⃣ Mist gently 💦
One soft spray from a step away.

### 3️⃣ Look! ✨
Water drops stick to the silk. The web lights up!

### 4️⃣ Sketch it ✏️
What shape is it? Draw it on the printable.

### 5️⃣ Leave it 👋
Say goodbye. Don't touch — it's the spider's home.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Find 3 webs. Count them!

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Web types** 🕸️ Find a round web, a messy web, and a sheet web.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Web map** 🗺️ Draw your yard and mark every web you find.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Count the spokes** 🔢 On one round web, count the spokes (lines from the middle).

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Field guide** 📖 Make a page for each web type: shape, where found, what spider made it (look it up).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🕸️ Spider silk is super thin — thinner than a hair.
- 💧 Water drops bend light, so the web suddenly shows up.
- 🧵 Round webs are made by **orb weavers**. Messy ones by **cobweb spiders**. Flat ones by **sheet weavers**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🕷️ Look, don't touch. Most spiders are harmless, but a few bite.
- 💦 A gentle mist won't hurt the web; a stream will wreck it.
- 🌅 Dew-covered mornings show webs even without spraying.

## 🧽 Cleanup
Nothing! 🌿
