# 🏓 Ping-Pong Push Race

No hands! Push a ping-pong ball to the finish with **only water**. 🏓💦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottles (twist to **STREAM**)
- [ ] 🏓 Ping-pong balls
- [ ] 🟦 Tape for lanes (or a table edge, or a rain gutter)
- [ ] 🧻 Towel

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Make lanes 🟦
Tape 2 lanes on a table or patio. Mark START and FINISH.

### 2️⃣ Balls on START 🏓
One per racer.

### 3️⃣ GO! 💦
Squirt the ball to push it. No touching!

### 4️⃣ Off the lane? 🔙
Back to START!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Just race! Count down: 3, 2, 1, GO!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Best of 3** — first to 2 wins 🏆

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Obstacle lane** 🚧 Add cups to steer around.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Tournament** 📋 Make a bracket for 4+ players on the printable.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💨 The water stream pushes the ball. Lighter things are easier to push.
- 🎯 Steering = aiming at the **side** of the ball to curve it.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧻 Indoors: towel the floor. Outdoors: easy.
- 🏓 Ping-pong balls dent if stepped on — pick them up.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🏓 Collect all balls
- [ ] 🟦 Peel tape
- [ ] 🧻 Dry the table
