# 🪟 Stained-Glass Window

Wet tissue paper **sticks to glass** like magic! 🪄🪟 Make a glowing window picture.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🪟 A window (glass)
- [ ] 🎨 Colored tissue paper, cut or torn into pieces
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towels

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Tear the pieces ✂️
Squares, triangles, strips — any shape.

### 2️⃣ Spray the glass 💦
Mist one small area.

### 3️⃣ Press tissue on 🖐️
It sticks! Overlap colors.

### 4️⃣ Spray over the top 💦
A light mist helps it stay.

### 5️⃣ Glow time ☀️
Look from inside when the sun shines through!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make a rainbow with strips 🌈

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Make a sun, flower, or butterfly shape 🦋

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Color mixing** — overlap yellow + blue. What do you see? 🟢

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Mosaic** 🧩 Draw a design on paper first, then build it piece by piece.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💧 Water fills tiny gaps between paper and glass. That creates a pull that holds the paper.
- 🌈 Light shines **through** the thin paper, so colors glow.
- ⛪ Real stained-glass windows work the same way — colored glass + sunlight.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Some tissue paper **bleeds** dye when wet. Use on glass only — not painted frames or walls.
- 🪟 When it dries, it falls off. Re-spray to keep it up longer.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖐️ Peel off the paper (or let it fall)
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the glass
