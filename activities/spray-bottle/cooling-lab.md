# 🌡️ Cool-Down Lab

Why does a spray of water feel **so cold**? 🥶 Prove it with a thermometer! 🌡️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with room-temp water
- [ ] 🌡️ 2 thermometers (kitchen or weather ones)
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towels
- [ ] 🌬️ A fan (or a windy day)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Feel it first 🖐️
Spray your arm. Blow on it. Cold, right?

### 2️⃣ Set up two thermometers 🌡️
One dry. One with a **wet** paper towel wrapped on the tip.

### 3️⃣ Add wind 🌬️
Put both in front of the fan.

### 4️⃣ Wait 5 minutes ⏱️
Read both. Which is colder?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Do the test. Which one got colder?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Record** every minute for 5 minutes on the printable.

### 🔟 Age 10
**No-fan test** ⚖️ Same test with no fan. Is the difference smaller? Why?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Explain it** ✍️ Write how sweating cools your body. Bonus: look up "wet-bulb temperature" and why weather scientists use it.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💧 Water needs energy (heat) to evaporate.
- 🌡️ It takes that heat **from your skin** — so you feel cooler.
- 😅 That's exactly why you sweat when you're hot!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🌡️ Digital kitchen thermometers work great.
- 🌬️ Keep fingers away from fan blades.

## 🧽 Cleanup
