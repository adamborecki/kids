# 🌧️ Rain Cloud in a Jar

Make a fluffy cloud ☁️ … then make it **RAIN** color! 🌈

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🫙 A clear jar or tall glass
- [ ] 💧 Water
- [ ] ☁️ Shaving cream
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with water + a few drops of blue food coloring
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towels

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Fill the jar 💧
Water up to about ¾ full.

### 2️⃣ Make the cloud ☁️
Squirt shaving cream on top. Make it puffy!

### 3️⃣ Make it rain 💦
Spray the blue water onto the cloud. Lots of sprays!

### 4️⃣ Watch 👀
When the cloud gets too heavy, blue "rain" drips down into the water!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Make the cloud. Spray until it rains. Shout "IT'S RAINING!" 🌧️

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Count the sprays until the first drop falls 🔢

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Thick vs thin** ☁️ Try a thin cloud and a thick cloud. Which rains first? Why?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Weather report** 🎤 Explain how real rain works (see 🧠). Draw the water cycle on the printable.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ☁️ Real clouds are made of tiny water drops.
- 💧 When the drops bump together, they get big and heavy.
- 🌧️ Too heavy to float → they fall as rain!
- 🔄 Water goes up (evaporation), makes clouds (condensation), falls (rain). That's the **water cycle**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧴 Use a **separate** spray bottle for colored water and label it.
- 🎨 Food coloring stains — paper towels under the jar.
- ⚠️ Shaving cream is not for tasting.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🫙 Pour jar down the sink
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe drips
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands
