# 🏁 Evaporation Race

Which surface dries **fastest**? Place your bets! 🏁💧

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] 🧪 4 surfaces: paper, plastic lid, metal pan, cloth
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer
- [ ] 📋 The printable

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Line them up 🧪
Put the 4 surfaces side by side.

### 2️⃣ Predict 🤔
Which one dries first? Write it down!

### 3️⃣ Spray each one 💦
Exactly **3 sprays** each. Same distance.

### 4️⃣ Check every 2 minutes ⏱️
Touch-test with a dry finger. Still wet?

### 5️⃣ Record 📋
Write down when each one is dry.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Test 4 surfaces. Was your prediction right?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Sun vs shade** ☀️🌳 Same 4 surfaces, two spots. Which spot wins?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Fair test** ⚖️ Change only ONE thing (fan on/off, sun/shade). Keep everything else the same. Explain why that matters.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Graph it** 📈 Bar graph of drying times. Explain: why does cloth or paper act differently from metal? (Hint: absorbing vs. beading.)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💧 Water molecules escape into the air when they get enough energy (heat).
- 🌬️ Moving air carries the vapor away — so a breeze speeds drying.
- 🧽 Paper and cloth **soak up** water, spreading it out. Metal and plastic make it **bead up**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔬 This is a great first "fair test" — the key idea is changing one thing at a time.
- ☀️ Dark metal in full sun gets hot — touch carefully.
