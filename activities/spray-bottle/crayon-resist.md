# 🕵️ Secret Crayon Pictures

Draw with an **invisible** white crayon… spray color… and the secret appears! 🪄

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖍️ White crayon (or a candle)
- [ ] 📄 White paper
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with watercolor water (liquid watercolor or paint + water)
- [ ] 🍪 Baking tray
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towels

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Draw in secret 🤫
Press hard with the white crayon. You can barely see it!

### 2️⃣ Swap papers 🔄
Give your secret to a partner.

### 3️⃣ Spray it 💦
Mist the paper with color.

### 4️⃣ Reveal! 🪄
The crayon lines stay white. The secret shows up!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Draw a shape or your name. Reveal it!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write a **secret message** for someone else to reveal 💌

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Hidden picture puzzle** 🧩 Draw part of a picture. Partner reveals and finishes it.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Treasure map** 🗺️ Draw a hidden map. The finder reveals it and follows it!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🕯️ Crayon is made of **wax**.
- 💧 Wax and water don't mix — the water slides off.
- 🎨 So the paint can't soak in where the crayon is. That's called **resist art**.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧴 Separate bottle for colored water, labeled.
- 🖍️ Press HARD — light crayon won't resist.
- 📄 Thick paper (cardstock, watercolor paper) curls less.
