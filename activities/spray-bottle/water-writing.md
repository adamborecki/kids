# ✍️ Water Writing Wall

Write giant words with water 💦 … then watch the sun **erase** them! ☀️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **STREAM**)
- [ ] 🧱 A dry fence, wall, or sidewalk in the sun
- [ ] ⏱️ A timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Pick a dry spot 🧱
Dark, dry spots show water best.

### 2️⃣ Write BIG ✍️
Squirt your name, a word, or a smiley 😊

### 3️⃣ Start the timer ⏱️
How long until it disappears?

### 4️⃣ Write it again 🔁
Try a sunny spot vs. a shady spot. Which wins?

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Write the first letter of your name. Then the whole name!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Write 3 words. Read them out loud before they vanish 🏃

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Sun vs. shade** ☀️🌳 Same word in both spots. Time each one. Write the seconds down.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Secret message relay** 🕵️ Write a word. Your partner must read it **before it dries**. Make it harder: smaller letters!

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ☀️ The sun's heat turns water into invisible vapor. That's **evaporation**.
- 🌬️ Wind and warm air make it go faster.
- 🌳 Shade is cooler, so the writing lasts longer.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧴 Water only — this is the kitchen bottle.
- 🧱 Wet wood and concrete dry with no marks. Skip painted walls if they're chalky.

## 🧽 Cleanup
Nothing! The sun cleans up for you ☀️
