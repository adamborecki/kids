# 🐌 Garden Critter Safari

Mist the garden at dusk 🌆 … and the **critters come out!** 🐌🪱🐞

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle
- [ ] 🌆 Evening (or after rain)
- [ ] 🔦 Flashlight
- [ ] 🔍 Magnifier (optional)
- [ ] 📋 The printable

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Mist a patch 💦
Leaves, soil, under plants. Make it damp.

### 2️⃣ Wait 10 minutes ⏳
Damp + dark = critter time.

### 3️⃣ Search 🔦
Under leaves, near rocks, along the soil.

### 4️⃣ Look closely 🔍
How does it move? How many legs?

### 5️⃣ Tally it 📋
Check off each critter you spot.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Find a snail 🐌 or a worm 🪱. Watch it move!

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Fill in the tally chart.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Slow race** 🐌 Two snails on a wet board. Who's faster? (Be gentle!)

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Count the legs** 🦗 Sort critters: 0 legs, 6 legs (insects), 8 legs (spiders), lots (millipedes).

### 🔟 Age 10
**Habitat map** 🗺️ Where did you find the most critters? Draw the garden and mark them.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🐌 Snails and slugs dry out easily, so they come out when it's **damp**.
- 🪱 Worms come up when the soil is wet.
- 🌍 These critters recycle dead leaves into soil.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🖐️ Wash hands after touching snails, slugs, or soil.
- 🐞 Gentle hands. Put every critter back where you found it.
- 🐝 Don't touch bees, wasps, or fuzzy caterpillars (some sting).

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands with soap
- [ ] 👟 Muddy shoes off at the door
