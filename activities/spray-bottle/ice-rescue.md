# 🧊 Ice Rescue

Toys are **frozen in ice!** 🥶 Blast them free with warm water. 💦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧊 Small toys frozen in ice (freeze them the night before)
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with **warm** tap water (not hot!)
- [ ] 🧂 A little salt
- [ ] 🍪 Baking tray or bin
- [ ] 🧻 Towel

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ The night before 🌙
Put small toys in a container. Add water. Freeze.

### 2️⃣ Pop out the ice block 🧊
Run the outside of the container under warm water. Slide it onto the tray.

### 3️⃣ Spray! 💦
Aim warm water at one spot. Watch it melt.

### 4️⃣ Sprinkle salt 🧂
Salt makes ice melt faster. Try it on one side.

### 5️⃣ RESCUE! 🦸
Free every toy. Count them!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Free **one** toy. Cheer! 🎉

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Race a grown-up: who frees their toy first?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Salt test** 🧂 Spray one side, salt the other side. Which melts faster? Draw what you see.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Speed plan** ⏱️ Time yourself. Next time, change ONE thing (warmer water? more salt? tapping?) and beat your time.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔥 Warm water carries heat into the ice. Heat melts ice.
- 🧂 Salt mixes with the thin layer of water on the ice and makes it **harder to freeze again**. That's why roads get salted in winter!
- 🧊 Bigger ice blocks take longer — they hold more cold.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🌡️ **Warm, not hot.** Test the water on your wrist.
- 🧸 Use toys that don't mind water (plastic animals, LEGO figures).
- 🧂 Wash hands after — salt stings small cuts.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧊 Dump leftover ice outside
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the tray
- [ ] 🧺 Towel to the laundry
