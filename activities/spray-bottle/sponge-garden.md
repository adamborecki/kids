# 🌱 Sponge Seed Garden

Grow a tiny garden on a **kitchen sponge!** 🧽🌱 Sprouts in about 2–5 days.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🧽 A new kitchen sponge
- [ ] 🌱 Fast seeds: cress, chia, or alfalfa
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle
- [ ] 🍽️ A small plate or lid

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Soak the sponge 💧
Wet it. Squeeze out extra.

### 2️⃣ Sprinkle seeds 🌱
A thin layer on top. Press gently.

### 3️⃣ Find a bright spot ☀️
A windowsill, but not blazing hot.

### 4️⃣ Mist twice a day 💦
Morning + after school. Never let it dry out!

### 5️⃣ Watch it grow 👀
Draw it every day on the chart.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Mist it every day. Tell someone what changed.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Count how many seeds sprouted on day 3 🔢

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Shape garden** — cut the sponge into a letter or heart first ❤️

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Light test** ☀️🌑 Two sponges: one in the window, one in a cupboard. Compare after 5 days.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Measure** 📏 Measure the tallest sprout each day in mm. Make a line graph.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Eat your experiment** 🥗 Cress is edible! Grow a clean batch, snip it with scissors, and add it to a sandwich (grown-up OK first).

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💧 Seeds wake up when they get water + warmth. That's **germination**.
- 🌱 The root goes down first, then the shoot goes up.
- 🌑 In the dark, sprouts grow tall and pale, stretching to find light.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧽 Use a **brand-new** sponge (no soap!).
- 🍄 Fuzzy mold? Toss it and start over — too wet, not enough air.
- 🥗 Only eat sprouts grown from seeds sold for sprouting/eating.

## 🧽 Cleanup
🧻 Wipe drips under the plate
