# 🪴 Plant Mist Patrol

A **real job** with a real squirt bottle. 💦
You're the Plant Patrol Officer! 👮

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with room-temp water (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] 🪴 The house plants
- [ ] 📋 The Patrol Chart (printable)
- [ ] ✏️ A pen or stickers

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Patrol time 🚶
Visit **every** plant. Say hi! 👋

### 2️⃣ Finger test 👆
Poke a finger into the dirt, up to the first bump.
- 🏜️ **Dry?** Tell a grown-up: "This one needs a drink!"
- 💧 **Damp?** It's OK today.

### 3️⃣ Mist the leafy ones 💦
3 to 5 sprays on the leaves.
✅ Ferns, palms, big shiny-leaf plants
🚫 **NOT** fuzzy leaves, cactus, or succulents! 🌵

### 4️⃣ Look closely 🔍
- 🌱 A new leaf?
- 🟤 Crunchy brown tips?
- 🐛 Any bugs?

### 5️⃣ Report it 📋
Check the box on the chart. Add notes!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Patrol with a grown-up. Finger-test one plant. Check the box ✅

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Do the **whole patrol yourself** for one week. Fill in the chart.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Name tags** 🏷️
Find each plant's real name (ask, or use a plant app).
Make a label for each one — practice on paper first, save the Magic Silver! ✨

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Bean in a Bag** 🫘
1. Wet a paper towel with your spray bottle.
2. Put it in a zip bag with 3 dry beans.
3. Tape it to a sunny window. Mist it when it looks dry.
4. Draw it every 2 days. Roots in about 3–7 days! 🌱

### 🔟 Age 10
**Growth graph** 📈
Measure your bean sprout (or a plant's newest leaf) every 2 days in mm.
Make a line graph. When did it grow fastest?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Real experiment** 🔬
Two bean bags: one misted daily, one only on day 1. (Or: sunny window vs dark cupboard.)
Write a prediction. Test for 10 days. Write a conclusion.
Bonus: make a **care card** for each house plant — light, water, toxic or not.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🌴 Many house plants come from **rainforests** where the air is wet.
- 👀 The best part of patrol? **Noticing.** You catch problems early.
- 🫘 Seeds wake up when they get water + warmth.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔬 Honest truth: misting only raises humidity for a few minutes. A pebble tray or humidifier does more. Misting is mostly about the **daily care habit** — that's the real win.
- 🚫 Skip fuzzy-leaf plants (African violets), cacti, and succulents.
- ☀️ Mist in the morning so leaves dry by night.
- ☠️ Some plants (pothos, peace lily, dieffenbachia) are toxic if chewed. Tell kids: look, don't taste.
