# 🚲 Bike & Scooter Wash

Open a **real car wash** for bikes, scooters, and wagons! 🧽💦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottles
- [ ] 🪣 Bucket of soapy water + sponges
- [ ] 🧻 Old towels for drying
- [ ] 🚲 Bikes, scooters, wagons, ride-on toys
- [ ] 👕 Clothes that can get soaked

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Set up stations 🏁
1️⃣ Spray · 2️⃣ Soap · 3️⃣ Rinse · 4️⃣ Dry

### 2️⃣ Spray it down 💦
Wet the whole bike. Loosen the mud.

### 3️⃣ Scrub 🧽
Soapy sponge. Wheels, seat, handlebars.

### 4️⃣ Rinse + dry ✨
Spray off the soap. Towel it dry. Shiny!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
You're the **Rinse Crew** 💦 Spray off all the soap.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Run one station all by yourself.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
Make a **price sign** (pretend money) and a "line starts here" sign 🪧

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Inspector** 🔍 Check every bike: tires squishy? Chain squeaky? Report to a grown-up.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Shift boss** 📋 Run the whole wash. Assign jobs. Keep a list of what got washed.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Bike check-up** 🔧 With a grown-up: check tire pressure with a gauge, oil the chain, test the brakes. Write a maintenance log.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧼 Soap grabs dirt and grease so water can wash it away.
- ⛓️ A clean, oiled chain lasts longer and pedals easier.
- 🛑 Checking brakes keeps riders safe.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 💧 Lots of water + soap = everyone gets wet. Swimsuits are smart.
- 🧼 Use mild dish soap, not harsh cleaners.
- 🔧 Chain oil and tire pumps are grown-up-supervised jobs.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🪣 Dump soapy water on gravel or a drain (not the flower bed)
- [ ] 🧺 Wet towels to the laundry
- [ ] 👕 Change into dry clothes
- [ ] 🧴 Spray bottles back home
