# 🏃 Sprinkle Tag

"It" has the spray bottle. Get sprinkled? **FREEZE!** 🥶💦

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 1 spray bottle (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] ☀️ A hot day + swimsuits
- [ ] 🌳 A yard with clear boundaries
- [ ] 👫 3+ players

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Set boundaries 🚩
Mark the edges with cones, shoes, or chalk.

### 2️⃣ Pick "It" 💦
"It" holds the spray bottle.

### 3️⃣ Run! 🏃
Sprinkled = **freeze** like a statue 🗿

### 4️⃣ Unfreeze friends 🤝
A free player high-fives you to unfreeze you.

### 5️⃣ Switch ⏱️
New "It" every 2 minutes.

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Play with a grown-up as "It" first.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Add **safe zones** 🏠 (a towel = safe for 5 seconds).

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Two Its** — two bottles, more chaos! 💦💦

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Freeze poses** 🗿 Frozen players must hold a funny pose. Laughing = still frozen!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Referee** 📋 Make the rules sheet. Settle arguments fairly. Keep time.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧠 Running and stopping fast builds balance and quick reactions.
- 🤝 Unfreezing friends = teamwork.
- 🌡️ Water on skin + a breeze = cool! (Evaporation takes heat away.)

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🚫 Rule #1: **below the shoulders only.** No faces.
- 🌱 Wet grass is slippery — bare feet or water shoes.
- 🥤 Water breaks for everyone.
