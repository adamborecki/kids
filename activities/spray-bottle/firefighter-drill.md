# 🚒 Firefighter Drill

🔥 Paper flames are on the box! **Firefighters, GO!** 🚒💦
Then learn the real fire-safety moves.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottles (twist to **STREAM**)
- [ ] 📦 A cardboard box (the "building")
- [ ] 📄 Red/orange paper + tape (the "flames")
- [ ] 🧢 A hat or helmet (optional)

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Build the building 📦
Tape paper flames to the box. Lightly so they can fall.

### 2️⃣ Alarm! 🚨
Make the siren sound: wee-ooo wee-ooo!

### 3️⃣ Spray the flames 💦
Squirt each flame until it falls off. Count the flames you knock down.

### 4️⃣ Fire safety talk 🗣️
Sit down and practice the real moves (see printable).

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Knock down the flames. Then practice **Stop, Drop, and Roll** 🔄

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Practice **crawl low under smoke** 🐛 across the yard. Smoke rises, so the air is better down low.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Escape map** 🗺️ Draw your home on the printable. Find **2 ways out** of every room. Pick a family meeting spot outside.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔥 Fire needs heat, fuel, and air. Water cools it down.
- 🌫️ Smoke rises, so the cleanest air is near the floor.
- 🚪 Having 2 ways out means you're never stuck.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🔥 **No real fire**, ever. This is pretend only.
- 🚫 Spray the box, not people.
- ☎️ Teach your address and when to call 911 (or your local emergency number).
- 🔋 Bonus: test the smoke alarms together.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 📄 Pick up soggy paper flames
- [ ] 📦 Flatten the box for recycling
