# 🎯 Squirt Target Range

Aim… squeeze… **SPLASH!** 💥
Knock down targets with your water stream.

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **STREAM**)
- [ ] 🥤 Plastic cups (6–10)
- [ ] 🏓 Ping-pong balls (if you have them)
- [ ] 🖍️ Sidewalk chalk
- [ ] 📋 The score card (printable)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Twist to STREAM 💧
Turn the tip until it squirts a straight line.

### 2️⃣ Build targets 🏗️
Pick one (or all!):
- 🥤 Stack cups in a pyramid on a step or table
- 🏓 Balance balls on top of upside-down cups
- 🎯 Draw chalk circles on a fence or wall

### 3️⃣ Make a throw line 📏
Draw a chalk line **3 big steps** away.
Toes stay behind the line!

### 4️⃣ Take turns 🔁
**5 squeezes** each. Then switch.

### 5️⃣ Keep score 🔢
- 💥 Knock something down = **3 points**
- 💦 Hit it = **1 point**
- 🎯 Wash away a chalk circle = **5 points**

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Big targets, **2 steps** away.
Count your hits out loud. Can you get to 10? 🔟

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Stand **3 steps** back.
Keep your own score on the card. Add up the totals! ➕

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Back it up** 👣
Take one step back after every round.
What's the farthest you can still hit? Measure it with a tape measure 📏

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Design the course** 🏗️
5 targets. Hard ones are worth more points.
Write the rules on the card. Test them. Change any rule that's unfair.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Accuracy %** 🎯
Take 20 squeezes. Count your hits.
hits ÷ 20 × 100 = your %
Try: left hand vs right hand ✋🤚 Close vs far. Which is better?

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Launch angle lab** 📐
From the same spot, squirt **flat**, **a little up**, and **way up**.
Mark where each one lands with chalk. Which angle goes farthest?
Graph distance vs. angle. (Without air, 45° wins. Does water agree?)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 👀✋ Aiming trains your eyes and hands to work as a team.
- ⬇️ Gravity pulls the water down into a curve 🌙
- 🎯 Far away? **Aim a little high!**
- 🏀 Basketball players aim the same way.

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🚫 Rule #1: **targets only.** No faces, no eyes, no surprise attacks.
- 🩱 Want a real water fight? Swimsuits + everyone says "yes" first.
- 🥤 Plastic cups survive. Paper cups get soggy fast.

## 🧽 Cleanup
quick!
- [ ] 🥤 Stack the cups
- [ ] 🏓 Find every ball (count them!)
- [ ] 🌧️ Chalk? Leave it. Rain cleans it.
