# 🥤 Float-the-Ball Relay

Fill the cup by spraying… until the ball **pops up to the top!** 🏓⬆️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 1 spray bottle per team
- [ ] 🥤 Tall clear cups or bottles with the top cut off (grown-up cuts)
- [ ] 🏓 Ping-pong balls (one per cup)
- [ ] 🪣 A bucket of water for refills
- [ ] ⏱️ Timer

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Set up 🥤
Put a ping-pong ball in each cup. Line cups up on a table.

### 2️⃣ Teams 👫
Each team gets a bottle and a cup.

### 3️⃣ Spray INTO the cup 💦
Fill it with water — spray only!

### 4️⃣ Ball at the top? 🏓
First ball to reach the rim WINS!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Fill the cup with a buddy. Take turns squeezing.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Team race! Switch sprayers every 10 squeezes.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Distance rule** 📏 Sprayers stand 2 steps back.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Relay** 🏃 Run to the bucket, refill, run back, spray. Next teammate!

### 🔟 Age 10
**Squeeze math** 🔢 Count squeezes to fill the cup. Estimate how many for a bigger cup — then test.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Volume lab** 🧪 Measure the cup in mL. Divide by squeezes = mL per squeeze. How many squeezes to fill a 2-liter bottle?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🏓 The ball is lighter than water, so it **floats**.
- ⬆️ More water = the ball rises. It rides the water level up.
- 🧪 One squeeze is only about 1 mL — that's why it takes so many!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🥤 Cut bottles have sharp edges — grown-ups cut and tape the edge.
- 💦 Everyone gets wet. Hot-day game!

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🥤 Dump cups on the grass
- [ ] 🏓 Collect balls
- [ ] 🧴 Bottles back home
