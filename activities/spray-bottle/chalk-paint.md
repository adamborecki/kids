# 🎨 Chalk Paint Magic

Plain chalk + a few sprays = **bright, gooey paint!** 🤩

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🖍️ Sidewalk chalk (fat kind)
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] 🖌️ Old paintbrush (if you have one)
- [ ] 👕 Old clothes
- [ ] 🧻 A towel for hands
- [ ] 🟦 Painter's tape (Age 9 challenge)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Draw it dry ✏️
Draw your picture with **dry** chalk.
Big shapes work best!

### 2️⃣ Spray one part 💦
Mist one area until it's wet and shiny.

### 3️⃣ WOW! 🤩
Watch the color get **dark and bright**.

### 4️⃣ Smoosh it 🖐️
Rub with your fingers or a brush.
Now it's paint!

### 5️⃣ Add chalk to the wet spot 🖍️
Draw on the wet part. It's **super** bright!

### 6️⃣ Snap a photo 📸
Ask a grown-up. It changes when it dries!

**3D hole illusion** 🕳️
Draw a "hole" in the sidewalk. Make one side dark, one side light.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Mural like a pro** 🖼️
1. Sketch it on graph paper (or draw a grid in the printable box).
2. Draw a big chalk grid on the sidewalk (1 paper square = 1 sidewalk square).
3. Copy square by square.
Real mural painters scale up pictures **exactly** this way.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🔍 Dry chalk is bumpy and dusty. It bounces light everywhere, so it looks pale.
- 💧 Water fills the tiny bumps. Light goes **in** instead of bouncing off.
- 🎨 So the color looks deep and bright — like wet sand looks darker!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👕 Chalk washes out of most clothes. Still: old clothes.
- 🟦 Painter's tape: peel it the **same day** or it can stick.
- 🧱 Some porous surfaces (unsealed brick, wood decks) can hold bright chalk for a while. Test a small spot first.
