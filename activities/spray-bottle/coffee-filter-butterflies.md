# 🦋 Coffee Filter Butterflies

Scribble ✏️ → spray 💦 → watch the colors **run and swirl** 🌀 → fly! 🦋

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] ☕ White coffee filters (round basket kind)
- [ ] 🖍️ **Washable** markers (must be washable!)
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **MIST**)
- [ ] 🍪 Baking tray or plastic placemat
- [ ] 🧻 Paper towels
- [ ] 📎 Clothespins or pipe cleaners

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Flatten it 🫓
Put a coffee filter flat on the tray.

### 2️⃣ Scribble! ✏️
Dots, lines, spirals — all over.
Leave some white space.

### 3️⃣ Spray 2 or 3 times 💦
Just a mist! Don't flood it.

### 4️⃣ Watch 👀
The colors **crawl** and mix. So cool!

### 5️⃣ Let it dry ⏳
About 20–30 minutes. A sunny window is faster ☀️

### 6️⃣ Scrunch the middle 🎀
Pinch it like a bow tie.

### 7️⃣ Add a body 📎
Clip a clothespin on the middle.
Or twist a pipe cleaner and curl the ends for antennae.

### 8️⃣ FLY! 🦋
Hang it in a window or on a string.

## 🌟 Level Up
### 🥉 Easy
Use **one color**. Watch it spread.

### 🥈 Medium
**Matching wings** 🪞
Fold the filter in half. Color one side. Press hard. Open it. Spray!

### 🥇 Hard
**Secret colors in black** 🕵️
1. Draw a thick **black** washable ring in the middle.
2. Spray lightly.
3. Watch black split into blue, purple, maybe yellow!
(Every brand is different. Test a few!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🧻 Paper is full of tiny tubes. Water **climbs** through them.
- 🚂 The water carries the ink along for a ride.
- 🏃 Some inks run fast, some run slow — so colors **split apart**.
- 🔬 Scientists call this **chromatography**. They use it to find out what's mixed in things!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- ⚠️ **Washable** markers only — permanent markers won't run.
- 🧻 Paper towels under the filter save your tray from stains.
- 🖐️ Fingers will get inky. Soap + water fixes it.

## 🧽 Cleanup
**$$** — wet inky stuff
- [ ] 🧻 Toss wet paper towels
- [ ] 🧽 Wipe the tray
- [ ] 🖐️ Wash hands with soap
- [ ] 🖍️ Caps ON markers (click!)
