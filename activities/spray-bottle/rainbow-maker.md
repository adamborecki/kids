# 🌈 Rainbow Maker

Make your **own** rainbow! ☀️ + 💦 = 🌈

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle full of water
- [ ] ☀️ A sunny day
- [ ] 🌳 A sunny spot outside
- [ ] 🖍️ Crayons + the printable (if you want)

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Find your shadow 👤
Stand with the sun at your **back**.
Your shadow points to where the rainbow will be.

### 2️⃣ Twist to MIST ☁️
Turn the tip until it sprays a soft cloud.
Not a squirt. A cloud!

### 3️⃣ Spray up high ⬆️
Squeeze fast, fast, fast!
Aim a little above your shadow's head.

### 4️⃣ Look INTO the mist 👀
See the stripes? That's YOUR rainbow! 🎉

### 5️⃣ Name the colors 🗣️
🔴 Red · 🟠 Orange · 🟡 Yellow · 🟢 Green · 🔵 Blue · 🟣 Purple

## 🌟 Level Up
### 🥉 Easy
Find the rainbow. Shout "I MADE IT!" 📣

### 🥈 Medium
Look for a **double rainbow** 🌈🌈
It's fainter and outside the first one.
👀 The colors are **flipped**!

### 🥇 Hard
Stand next to a friend. You spray.
Who sees the rainbow? Do you BOTH see it? 🤔
(Hint: everyone sees their **own** rainbow!)

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ☀️ Sunlight looks white… but it is **all colors mixed**.
- 💧 Each tiny drop bends the light, like a mini prism.
- 🌈 Each color bends a different amount, so they spread into stripes.
- 🌧️ A real rainbow is the same trick, with rain!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧴 Use a **new** bottle, or one that only ever held water. Never reuse a cleaner bottle.
- 🏷️ Label it **WATER ONLY**.
- 😎 Remind kids: never look at the sun.
- 🕙 Best before ~10 am or after ~4 pm. A rainbow needs the sun **low** in the sky (under about 42°). At noon in summer it often won't work — that's physics, not you!

## 🧽 Cleanup
**$** — almost nothing!
- [ ] 🪴 Pour leftover water on a plant
- [ ] 🧴 Bottle back in its spot
