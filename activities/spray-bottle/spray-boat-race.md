# ⛵ Spray Boat Race

Build a tiny foil boat 🛥️ … then **blast it** to the finish line! 💨

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle (twist to **STREAM**)
- [ ] 🛁 Baking dish, plastic bin, or the bathtub
- [ ] 🥈 Aluminum foil
- [ ] 🪙 Pennies (Age 10 challenge)
- [ ] ⏱️ A timer (phone works)
- [ ] 🧻 Towel underneath

## 👣 Steps
### 1️⃣ Add water 💧
About 2 inches (5 cm) deep. That's like your finger to the 2nd bump.

### 2️⃣ Build a boat 🥈
- Tear a square of foil, as big as your hand ✋
- Fold up all 4 edges
- Pinch the corners tight

### 3️⃣ Start + finish 🏁
One end is START. The other end is FINISH.

### 4️⃣ Ready… set… SPRAY! 💨
Squirt the **back** of your boat to push it.

### 5️⃣ Time it ⏱️
How many seconds to the finish? Try to beat it!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Push your boat around. Make it spin! 🌀 Race a grown-up.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
**Beat your time** ⏱️
Race with a timer. Can you beat your best time 3 times in a row?

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Slalom** 🚧
Put 3 cups or blocks in the water. Steer around them. Time it.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Boat Lab** 🔬
Build 3 boats: 🟫 flat raft, ⛵ paper sail, 🔺 pointy front.
Time each one. Fill in the Boat Lab table. Which is fastest?

### 🔟 Age 10
**Cargo challenge** 🪙
How many pennies before it sinks?
Pennies **spread out** vs **piled in the middle** — which holds more?
Redesign and beat your record.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Fair test + averages** 📊
Use the **same size** foil square for every boat — only the shape changes.
Run each boat **3 times**. Find the average time.
Why is an average better than one try? Graph your results.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 💨 The water squirt **pushes** the boat. Push one way → boat goes the other.
- ⛵ A sail is a bigger target, so it catches more push.
- 🔺 A pointy front slices through water. A flat front has to shove it.
- 🚢 Real boats are shaped pointy for the same reason!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 👀 **Stay close.** Little kids can drown in very shallow water. Empty the bin when you're done.
- 🧻 Indoors? Big towel underneath. Outside is easier.
- 🥈 Foil edges can be a bit sharp — fold them over.

## 🧽 Cleanup
a few splashes
- [ ] 🪴 Pour the water on plants or the grass
- [ ] 🧻 Dry the bin and the floor
- [ ] ♻️ Foil in recycling
- [ ] 🧺 Wet towels to the laundry
