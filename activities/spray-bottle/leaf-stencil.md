# 🍃 Leaf Stencil Spray Art

Put leaves on paper, spray colored water over them… lift them up and **WOW** — leaf shadows! 🍂

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 🍃 Flat leaves, ferns, flowers
- [ ] 📄 Thick paper or cardstock
- [ ] 💦 Spray bottle with watered-down paint (or liquid watercolor)
- [ ] 🪨 Small rocks to hold leaves down
- [ ] 👕 Old clothes

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Collect leaves 🍃
Flat ones work best. Ferns look amazing!

### 2️⃣ Arrange 🎨
Lay leaves on the paper. Hold them down with tiny rocks.

### 3️⃣ Spray from above 💦
Hold the bottle high. Mist over everything.

### 4️⃣ Wait 1 minute ⏳
Let the paint soak in.

### 5️⃣ LIFT! ✨
Peel off the leaves. Leaf shadows!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
One leaf, one color.

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Three leaves, two colors.

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Layers** 🌈 Spray light color, move leaves, spray dark color.

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Name that tree** 🌳 Label each leaf shape with its tree name (ask, or use a leaf guide).

### 🔟 Age 10
**Your initial** 🔤 Cut your first letter from paper. Use it as a stencil with leaves around it.

### 🚀 Age 11+
**Print series** 🖼️ Make 4 prints that match: same leaves, different colors. Frame them as a set.

## 🧠 Why It Works
- 🚫 The leaf **blocks** the paint, so that spot stays white.
- 🎨 This is called a **stencil** — artists and sign makers use it.
- 🌍 Ancient cave artists sprayed paint around their hands the same way!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🎨 Paint mist **drifts**. Do it outside, away from cars and walls.
- 🧴 Use a separate bottle for paint. Label it **PAINT**.
- 🧪 Mix: about 1 part washable paint to 3 parts water. Too thick = clogged nozzle.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🚿 Rinse the spray bottle **right away** (pump clean water through)
- [ ] 🧽 Hose off the table
- [ ] 🖐️ Scrub hands
- [ ] 👕 Paint clothes to the laundry
