# ❄️ Snow Painting

The yard is a giant white canvas! 🎨❄️

## 🧰 Gather
- [ ] 💦 2–4 spray bottles with water + food coloring
- [ ] ❄️ Fresh snow
- [ ] 🧤 Waterproof gloves

## 👣 Steps

### 1️⃣ Mix colors 🎨
Water + 10 drops of food coloring in each bottle.

### 2️⃣ Find clean snow ❄️
Flat, fresh snow is best.

### 3️⃣ Paint! 💦
Stream for lines, mist for color clouds.

### 4️⃣ Step back 📸
Take a photo before it snows again!

## 🎚️ By Age
🎂 Pick the age. Every age can do the main steps — this is the **extra challenge**.

### 6️⃣ Age 6
Spray rainbow stripes 🌈

### 7️⃣ Age 7
Paint a snowman's face and buttons ⛄

### 8️⃣ Age 8
**Color mixing** — spray blue over yellow. What happens?

### 9️⃣ Age 9
**Snow mural** 🖼️ Plan a picture on paper first. Then paint it big.

### 🔟 Age 10
**Snow maze** 🌀 Paint a maze path. Can someone walk it without stepping off?

## 🧠 Why It Works
- ❄️ Snow is white because ice crystals bounce all colors of light.
- 🎨 The colored water soaks between the crystals.
- 🌡️ Cold slows down how fast it spreads — sharper lines!

## 🛟 Grown-Up Notes
- 🧤 Food coloring stains mittens and skin for a day or two.
- 🥶 Cold hands: take warm-up breaks every 10 minutes.
- 🧴 Separate bottles for colors — not the kitchen water bottle.

## 🧽 Cleanup
- [ ] 🧴 Rinse the bottles
- [ ] 🧤 Hang gloves to dry
- [ ] ❄️ The snow? It melts away by itself
